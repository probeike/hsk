# Time & date expressions

**Dates go big → small**: year → month → day → weekday. Year digits are read out one at a time: 2026年 → èr líng èr liù nián.

YYYY年 + M月 + D号/日 + 星期X

**号** is spoken, **日** is written / formal. Same day.

**Clock**: N点 (N分). Half past = N点半. Quarters = 一刻 / 三刻. "To" = 差 N分 N点 (差五分八点 = 7:55).

**Time phrases go BEFORE the verb** — never after like English. With a topic (usually the subject), it can also sit at the very start.

(Topic/Subject) + TIME + Verb …

**…的时候** — the whole Verb-phrase + 的时候 acts like a "when-clause": 我吃饭的时候不说话.
