Quick reminder

比 comparison

A + 比 + B + Adj (+ degree)

"A is more Adj than B." The degree (how much more) comes after the adjective:

  • 一点儿 / 一些 — a little more
  • 得多 / 多了 — a lot more
  • a concrete amount: 三岁 / 五块钱
他比我大三岁。 He's three years older than me.
今天比昨天冷一点儿。 Today is a bit colder than yesterday.
他的汉语比我好得多。 His Chinese is a lot better than mine.

Never use 很 / 非常 / 太 / 真 inside a 比 sentence — the degree slot goes after the adjective.

Negation: 没有 replaces 比. A 没有 B + Adj = "A is not as Adj as B".

Round-up: sentence types

  • Pivotal sentences with 让 / 叫 / 请: 让我想想再告诉你。
  • Imperatives: "don't" — 别 / 不要 + V + 了. The 了 signals "stop doing the thing you're doing": 别找了。
  • 对 preposition — 对 N + V/Adj = "toward/to N": 他对我很好。
  • Confirmation: 是不是 / ……好吗?

Structured practice — 比 comparison

  1. Fix: 他比我很高。
  2. Fix: 他比我不高。
  3. Translate: My Chinese is a lot better than last year. (得多)
  4. Translate: This bag is five yuan more than that one.
  5. Translate: My sister is not as tall as me. (use 没有)
  6. Add a degree: 他比我大。 → "he's much older" and then "he's a little older".

Round-up — sentence types

  1. Translate: Teacher Wang asked me to write a report. (pivotal 让/叫)
  2. Translate: Don't worry — everything is fine.
  3. Translate: He's really good to his mother. (对)
  4. Confirmation: turn into an A-not-A question: 你是中国人。
  5. Confirmation: turn into a ……好吗? question: 我们一起吃饭。
