我是去年搬到纽约的，现在我家离公司很近，我过得比以前好得多。

Wǒ shì qùnián bāndào Niǔyuē de, xiànzài wǒ jiā lí gōngsī hěn jìn, wǒ guò de bǐ yǐqián hǎo de duō.

It was last year that I moved to New York; now I live close to work, and my life is much better than before.

What this sheet is

No new grammar. This is the finale: everything from sheets 1–5, shuffled so that no two exercises in a row test the same thing — which is the only way to find out whether you actually own a pattern or were just riding the momentum of a page about it.

Look at the title sentence again. It is not one grammar point, it is four stacked into a single breath — and that stacking is the real HSK 2 exit exam:

1. Warm-up Five quick ones, five different topics — no two in a row alike

C1 · S3 选词填空。 Three de's, three jobs. One word in the bank is a decoy.

我老师高兴说：你跳真好！

S1 Fix the sentence: 纽约比北京很大。

纽约比北京大得多。 — or 纽约比北京大一点儿。 if the gap is small. Two rules collided here: 很 / 非常 / 太 / 真 are banned inside a 比 sentence, and if you want to say how much more, that amount goes in the slot after the adjective (大得多, 大一点儿, 大一些), never before it.

S2 Fill in the coverb: 我家 ___ 地铁站不远。
(My place isn't far from the subway station.) One character — the coverb that measures the gap between two things that don't move.

C2 "Two months ago I moved house." Which one says that?

  • 两个月前我搬家了。✓ 前 after the number = a point on the timeline. One event, one moment.
  • 前两个月我搬家了。✗ 前 before the number = a span: "over the past two months I was moving house" — a two-month removal job.
  • 我搬家了两个月前。✗ Time words go before the verb in Chinese, never trailing at the end like English "…two months ago".

S3 Fill in the result complement: 我还没做 ___ 作业呢。
(I haven't finished my homework yet.) One character, glued straight onto 做 — and notice that with 没 in front, there is no 了.

2. Build it Word order is where these patterns actually break

S4 Build: "This job — it was he who got it for me." (Two orders are accepted.)

这个工作 是 他 帮我 介绍 的

S5 小丽问你去没去过上海。你说：我去 ___ 两次上海。
(I've been to Shanghai twice — ever, in my life.) Not "I went twice on that trip" — you want the marker that answers have you ever, the one that survives 没.

C3 Build: "I haven't had breakfast yet."

我 还没 吃 早饭 呢

S1 Build: "My life now is much better than before." (Two orders are accepted — 比 may sit before or after the 得 verb.)

我现在 过得 比以前 好 得多

S2 Build: "I eat lunch at the office every day." (Two orders are accepted — 每天 can lead, or follow 我. Everything else is fixed: time → place → verb.)

我 每天 在公司 吃 午饭

3. Mixed bag Including the easy tier you never drilled because you already had it

Fill in: 虽然我很累，___ 我很高兴。
(Although I'm tired, I'm happy.) Chinese keeps both halves of the pair — unlike English, which drops one.

S5 Fill in: 你看，门还开 ___ 呢。
(Look — the door is still open.) Nobody is opening anything; a state from an earlier action is simply still true. One character.

S4 小丽 quit her job yesterday. Your friend hasn't heard any of this. You break the news:

  • 小丽昨天辞职了。✓ Brand-new information — the event itself is the news, so plain 了.
  • 小丽是昨天辞职的。✗ 是…的 assumes you both already know she quit and only spotlights when. Your friend doesn't know yet.
  • 小丽辞职了昨天。✗ Word order: the time word sits before the verb — 昨天辞职, never 辞职…昨天.

Translate: "Stop looking! Your phone is on the desk."

别找了，(你的)手机在桌子上。 — 别…了 is not plain "don't"; the 了 is what makes it "stop doing what you're already doing". Plain 别找 would mean "don't go looking" before you'd even started. And note the second half: 在 as a full verb (be located at), with the place word 桌子上 — Chinese needs that 上.

S1 Fill in: 我的汉语 ___ 小丽好。
(My Chinese isn't as good as Xiao Li's.) You are stating a fact, not arguing with anyone — so don't negate 比, replace it.

4. Longer stretches Two things at once, then a whole paragraph

S4 · S2 Translate: "I learned dance from a Korean teacher." (Your friend knows you dance — the news is from whom.)

我的舞蹈是跟韩国老师学的。 — or 我是跟一个韩国老师学的舞蹈。
Two sheets at once: 跟 is a coverb, so it goes before the verb it serves (跟老师学, never 学跟老师), and the whole finished fact gets wrapped in 是…的 because the point isn't that you learned — it's from whom.

Build: "Let me think about it and then I'll tell you."

让我 想想 再 告诉 你

C3 Fill in: 新年快要到 ___ 。
(New Year is almost here.) 快要 never travels alone — it needs its closing partner at the end of the sentence.

S2 选词填空 — 我告诉老板了。 Five coverbs, one decoy. Every one of them sits before the verb it serves — and the first sentence stacks two of them in a row.

今天我很高兴。上午我办公室老板说：我想辞职，我要去当舞蹈老师。老板问我：新公司你家远不远？我说不远，我家到那儿只要二十分钟。下午我南走十分钟去舞蹈教室，练习了三个小时。

Wǒ shì 2024 nián bāndào Niǔyuē de. Wǒ jiā lí gōngsī bú tài yuǎn, wǒ měitiān cóng jiā zǒudào dìtiězhàn. Zhège xīngqī gōngzuò hěn máng, xīngqīwǔ wǒ hěn wǎn cái zuòwán gōngzuò. Xīngqīliù wǎnshang wǒ qù shàng wǔdǎokè le, lǎoshī xiàozhe shuō: nǐ tiào de bǐ yǐqián hǎo de duō! Suīrán yǒudiǎnr lèi, dànshì wǒ guò de hěn kāixīn.

It was in 2024 that I moved to New York. My place isn't too far from the office; every day I walk from home to the subway station. Work was busy this week — on Friday I didn't finish until late. Saturday evening I went to dance class, and my teacher said with a smile: you dance much better than before! Tired, yes, but I'm living happily.

Count them: 是…的 (S4) · 离 / 从…到 (S2) · 搬到, 走到, 做完 (S3) · 了 and 着 (S5) · 跳得比以前好得多 — 比 + 得 + the degree slot all at once (S1, S3) · plus 虽然…但是 for free. That is every sheet in six lines.
