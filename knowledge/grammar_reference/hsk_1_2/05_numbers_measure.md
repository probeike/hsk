# Numbers & measure words

**Golden rule**: a number can't sit directly in front of a noun. You need a measure word. The same rule holds for 这 / 那 / 几: they also want a measure word.

Number / 这 / 那 / 几 + MW + Noun

**Common MWs (HSK 1–2)**:

- 个 — general, the safe default (people, things, months).

- 口 — family members only: 五口人.

- 本 — books, notebooks.

- 只 — small animals: 一只猫.

- 件 — upper-body clothes, matters: 一件衣服 / 一件事.

- 张 — flat objects: 一张票 / 一张纸.

- 杯 / 瓶 — cups / bottles.

- 块 — yuan; pieces: 三块钱.

- 岁 — age: 二十岁.

**Reduplication**: MW + MW = "every one". 个个都好 = "every one is good".

**一下** — after a verb, softens or briefens the action: 等一下 (wait a moment), 看一下 (take a look).

**Tone of 一 (yī)**: 4th before 1/2/3 tone; 2nd before 4th; stays 1st when alone or in a multi-digit number.
