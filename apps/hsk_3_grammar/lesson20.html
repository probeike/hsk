Lesson 20

我被他影响了

Wǒ bèi tā yǐngxiǎng le

I've been influenced by him

What you'll do

This lesson introduces no new grammar — it's the synthesis. You'll revisit the HSK 3 structures most prone to English-speaker error, and drill them in mixed practice.

  1. The big four: 把 / 被 / 着 / 了 — one mental model for all of them.
  2. Conditional pairings — 如果…就, 只要…就, 只有…才.
  3. Mixed practice — end-of-course exercises drawing from all 20 lessons.

1. The big four: 把 / 被 / 着 / 了 One unified picture

Why these four matter: each rearranges English word order in a specific way. Confusing them costs more accuracy than any other HSK 3 error. Here they are together:
what happened to B Lessons 10, 11, 13 被 — passive flipped: patient → agent → V B 被 A V + result focus on what was done to B Lesson 19 着 — state / frame freeze-frame, still happening V 着 (…) existence or backdrop action Lessons 3, 4 了 — state change boundary crossed / new state V 了 / … 了 completion OR new situation Lessons 4, 10, 11, 13, 19
Four icons, one gallery. When you encounter one of these in a sentence, the first question isn't "what does it mean?" but "which schema am I looking at?"

Active vs passive — same event, two angles

把 (active)
他把我的书拿走了
He took my book.
被 (passive)
我的书被他拿走了
My book was taken by him.

Both describe the same event. Choose based on what you want to foreground: the doer (把) or the thing done-to (被).

他的话使我想起了小时候。

Tā de huà shǐ wǒ xiǎng qǐ le xiǎoshíhou.

His words made me recall my childhood. (causative 使 + extended directional 起 + state-change 了.)

我被他影响了，现在我也喜欢运动了。

Wǒ bèi tā yǐngxiǎng le, xiànzài wǒ yě xǐhuan yùndòng le.

I've been influenced by him; now I like exercising too. (被 passive + 了 change-of-state, twice.)

Practice

What happened in 我的照相机被朋友拿走了?

  • I took my friend's camera.✗ 被 is passive; the subject is the patient, not the agent.
  • My camera was taken (away) by my friend.✓ B 被 A V + result (拿走) + 了.
  • My friend lost my camera.✗ 拿走 means "take away", not "lose".

Which is the correct 把-sentence for "He finished drinking the coffee"?

  • 他把咖啡喝完了。✓ S + 把 + O + V + result + 了.
  • 他把喝完咖啡了。✗ The object must come right after 把, before the verb.
  • 他把咖啡喝。✗ 把 needs a result/complement; a bare verb is incomplete.

Fill the blank: 他拿___一个碗吃饭。
(He's eating (while) holding a bowl.)

着 — the backdrop action "holding" frames the main action "eating".

Build: "I've been influenced by him."

我 被 他 影响 了

Build a 把-sentence: "He put the camera on the table."

他 把 照相机 放 在 桌子上 了

2. Conditional pairings Three "if/then" frames, sharply distinguished

如果 … 就 … (general if)
如果下雨，我就不去
If it rains, I won't go. (Lesson 15)
只要 … 就 … (sufficient)
只要不下雨，我就去
As long as it doesn't rain, I'll go. (Lesson 17)
只有 … 才 … (necessary)
只有天气好，我才去
I'll only go if the weather's good. (Lesson 19)

Notice the pivot word disambiguates: 就 for generic or sufficient, 才 for necessary.

Practice

"Only if you work hard can you do well." Pick the natural Chinese:

  • 只有努力，才能做得好。✓ Necessary condition pairs 只有 with 才.
  • 只有努力，就能做得好。✗ 只有 must pair with 才, not 就.
  • 只要努力，才能做得好。✗ 只要 pairs with 就, and weakens the claim to "sufficient".

What does 只要不下雨，我就去。 mean?

  • Only if it doesn't rain will I go.✗ That would be 只有…才. 只要…就 is weaker ("sufficient").
  • As long as it doesn't rain, I'll go.✓ 只要 X 就 Y — "X is enough for Y".
  • If it rains, I'll still go.✗ The sentence says "not raining", not "raining".

Fill the blank: 只有真正做事情的时候，___能了解有多难。
(Only when you really do things can you understand how hard it is.)

才 — 只有 pairs with 才 (necessary condition).

Build: "Only by working hard can one do well."

只有 努力 才 能 做得好

Build: "As long as you work hard, you'll do well."

只要 你 努力 就 会 做得好

3. Mixed practice Drawing from all 20 lessons

Who ended up with the phone in 我的手机被朋友借走了?

  • I took my friend's phone.✗ First-noun trap. 被 flips the roles.
  • My friend borrowed my phone.✓ B (my phone) 被 A (friend) V (borrow-away) — passive.

Which sentence is correctly formed?

  • 我把作业写完了。✓ 把 + B + V + result + 了. Lesson 10/13 pattern.
  • 我写完作业把了。✗ 把 must come before the object, not after.
  • 我把作业写。✗ 把 requires a result or complement; bare V is incomplete.

"As long as you try, you'll do well" — pick the natural Chinese:

  • 只要你努力，就会做得好。✓ Sufficient condition — 只要…就…
  • 只有你努力，才会做得好。✗ This says "only if you try" — a stricter claim than "as long as".
  • 如果你努力才做得好。✗ 如果 pairs with 就, not 才.

Fill: 天气 ___ 冷了。 ("The weather is getting colder and colder.")

Fill (twice): 他 ___ 吃饭，___ 看电视。 ("He eats and watches TV at the same time.")

Build a 被-sentence: "This book was finished (being read) by him."

这本书 被 他 看 完 了

Build a 把-sentence with placement: "He put the money on the table."

他 把 钱 放 在 桌子上 了

Build a conditional: "If you have time, come find me."

如果 你 有时间 就 来 找 我

Which sentence correctly says "The problem has finally been solved"?

  • 问题终于被解决了。✓ 被 passive + result verb 解决 + state-change 了.
  • 问题终于把解决了。✗ 把 is active (S 把 O V + result); you need 被 for the passive here.
  • 问题终于被解决。✗ Missing 了 — a 被-sentence with a new-state reading needs the particle.

Build: "They are often mistaken for each other by other people."

他们 经常 被 别人 认错
