Review 06-10

时间、比较、把

Shíjiān, bǐjiào, bǎ

Cumulative review for Lessons 6-10

How to use this review

This page focuses on Lessons 6-10: duration, 对…感兴趣, 半/刻/差 time, 又 vs 再, flexible question words, 越A越B, 跟…一样, 比 comparisons, approximate numbers, and the first 把 pattern. Earlier grammar appears only as recycling.

1. Quick diagnostic Choose the structure that matches the job

What does the sentence mean?
我学中文学了三年了。

  • I have been studying Chinese for three years and still am.The final 了 shows the state continues up to now.
  • I studied Chinese once, three years ago.That is not a duration complement.
  • I will study Chinese in three years.No future time is stated.

Fill the interest expression: 我对历史很___。

对 + topic + 感兴趣 = be interested in a topic.

Which one means 5:45?

  • 五点一刻This is 5:15.
  • 差一刻六点A quarter short of six = 5:45.
  • 五点半This is 5:30.

Which sentence asks to do something again in the future?

  • 他昨天又迟到了。又 looks back at another repeated event.
  • 请你再说一遍。再 points forward: do it again.
  • 他又高又瘦。又…又… lists two qualities.

What does the flexible question word mean?
谁都可以参加。

  • Anyone can take part.谁都 means "everyone / anyone" in this frame.
  • Who can take part?This is not a real question here.
  • Nobody can take part.That would need negation.

Which sentence says "the more I listen, the more clearly I understand"?

  • 我越来越听，越来越明白。越来越 does not link two changing conditions this way.
  • 我越听越明白。越A越B links "the more A, the more B."
  • 我听得一样明白。一样 compares sameness, not a changing relation.

Which comparison means "Math is much harder than history"?

  • 数学比历史难得多。比 + adjective + 得多 gives a large difference.
  • 数学跟历史一样难。This says they are equally hard.
  • 数学比历史一样难。比 and 一样 do not combine like this.

What happens to the object in this 把 sentence?
他把旧自行车换了。

  • He replaced the old bicycle.把 fronts the handled object and tells what happened to it.
  • The old bicycle replaced him.Do not treat the first noun after 把 as the doer.
  • He is comparing two bicycles.No 比 comparison appears.

2. Contrast drills Small words, big differences

Which sentence says both people speak equally clearly?

  • 他说得跟老师一样清楚。跟…一样 marks sameness.
  • 他说得比老师清楚得多。This says he is much clearer than the teacher.
  • 他说得老师一样清楚。The comparison needs 跟.

Fill the approximate-number word: 从学校到银行要二十分钟___。

左右 follows the number phrase: about twenty minutes.

Choose the sentence that uses 把 because the object is handled and left in a good state.

  • 请把书放好。The book is the handled object; 放好 gives the result.
  • 我喜欢书。No disposal or result.
  • 书很贵。This only describes the book.

Interpret the direction of 再:
明天我们再聊。

  • We will chat again tomorrow.再 points to another occurrence in the future.
  • We chatted again yesterday.Past repeated events use 又.
  • We are both tall and thin.That is 又…又… from earlier lessons.

3. Sentence-building Build the frame before the verb

Build: I have studied Chinese for two years.

我学中文学了两年了

Build: Xiaoli is very interested in Chinese.

小丽对中文很感兴趣

Build: Every place is quiet.

什么地方都很安静

Build: The colder the weather, the more I want hot tea.

天气越冷我越想喝热茶

Build: I finished the homework.

5. Mini-reading A day with comparisons and 把

小刚：小丽，你学中文学了多久了？
小丽：我学了三年了。以前我对中文不太感兴趣，现在越学越喜欢。
小刚：所以你说得跟老师一样清楚。今天你几点去学校？
小丽：差一刻八点。我骑自行车去，二十分钟左右就到。
小刚：我也想骑车，可是我的自行车太旧了。
小丽：你可以把旧自行车换了。学校附近有一个地方，比较方便。
小刚：好。我先把作业写完，再去看看。

Xiǎogāng: Xiǎolì, nǐ xué Zhōngwén xué le duōjiǔ le?
Xiǎolì: Wǒ xué le sān nián le. Yǐqián wǒ duì Zhōngwén bù tài gǎn xìngqù, xiànzài yuè xué yuè xǐhuan.
Xiǎogāng: Suǒyǐ nǐ shuō de gēn lǎoshī yíyàng qīngchu. Jīntiān nǐ jǐ diǎn qù xuéxiào?
Xiǎolì: Chà yí kè bā diǎn. Wǒ qí zìxíngchē qù, èrshí fēnzhōng zuǒyòu jiù dào.
Xiǎogāng: Wǒ yě xiǎng qí chē, kěshì wǒ de zìxíngchē tài jiù le.
Xiǎolì: Nǐ kěyǐ bǎ jiù zìxíngchē huàn le. Xuéxiào fùjìn yǒu yí ge dìfang, bǐjiào fāngbiàn.
Xiǎogāng: Hǎo. Wǒ xiān bǎ zuòyè xiě wán, zài qù kànkan.

小丽学中文学了多久了？

  • 三年了我学了三年了。
  • 二十分钟左右That is the ride time.
  • 一刻钟That is not her study duration.

小丽现在对中文怎么样？

  • 越来越不感兴趣The passage says the opposite.
  • 越学越喜欢现在越学越喜欢。
  • 一点儿也不明白She speaks clearly.

小丽几点去学校？

  • 八点一刻That is 8:15.
  • 七点四十五分差一刻八点 = 7:45.
  • 三点半Not in the passage.

小刚最后打算先做什么？

  • 把作业写完我先把作业写完。
  • 马上换自行车He will look after finishing homework.
  • 去银行工作No bank job is mentioned.

6. Translation and production Make the grammar yourself

Translate: I have worked at the bank for five years.

我在银行工作了五年了。

Translate: I am very interested in history.

我对历史很感兴趣。

Translate: He is a little taller than me.

他比我高一点儿。

Translate: Please put the book away properly.

请把书放好。

Write 4-5 sentences comparing two classes, two places, or two ways to travel. Use at least two of: 比、跟…一样、左右、越…越、把.

我家附近的地方比学校安静一点儿。骑自行车去学校要二十分钟左右，坐车更方便。天气越好，我越想骑车。到学校以后，我先把自行车放好，再去上课。
