Lesson 11

别忘了把空调关了

Bié wàng le bǎ kōngtiáo guān le

Don't forget to turn off the air conditioner

What you'll learn

  1. 才 vs 就 (and the other 就) — two time-scalers with opposite attitudes, plus 就 as sequential "then".
  2. 把字句 2: A 把 B V + 在 / 到 … — disposal, with a destination.

See also: discourse particles appendix · the three 了s appendix

1. 才 vs 就 "Only then" vs "already" — the speaker's feelings about timing

When a speaker reaches for this: to express an attitude about how fast or slow something happened. 就 = "earlier/faster than expected" (already). 才 = "later/slower than expected" (only then).

The English trap

English uses already vs only, but lazily: "I only got home at 9pm" vs "I was already home by 9pm". Chinese marks this tiny attitude shift directly — and misusing them flips the whole feeling of your sentence.

就 — "sooner than expected"
他七点就来了
He already arrived at 7 (impressively early).
才 — "later than expected"
他九点才来
He didn't come until 9 (annoyingly late).

我六点就起床了。

Wǒ liù diǎn jiù qǐ chuáng le.

I was already up at 6 (early for me).

我九点才起床。

Wǒ jiǔ diǎn cái qǐ chuáng.

I didn't get up until 9 (later than I usually do).

Notice: sentences with 就 usually end with 了 (completed early); sentences with 才 typically don't use 了 (the speaker is still "complaining" about the lateness).

Practice

What's the attitude in: 他六点才回来。

  • Speaker thinks 6 o'clock is late.✓ 才 marks "later than expected".
  • Speaker thinks 6 o'clock is early.✗ For that you'd use 就: 他六点就回来了.
  • Speaker has no feeling about the time.✗ 才/就 always carry an attitude about timing.

Fill with 才 or 就: 考试明天就开始，她昨天 ___ 复习完了。
(She finished reviewing yesterday — impressively early.)

就 — "already done (ahead of schedule)". 就 typically pairs with 了 at the end.

Fill with 才 or 就: 电影七点开始，他八点 ___ 到。
(He didn't arrive until 8 — late.)

才 — "only at 8 (later than expected)". Note no 了: the speaker is still annoyed.

What does this mean? 我五岁就开始学英语了。

  • I didn't start English until I was 5.✗ That would use 才: 我五岁才开始…
  • I started English as early as age 5.✓ 就 + 了 = impressively early.
  • I have been studying English for 5 years.✗ No duration is expressed here.

Build: "He didn't get up until 8 (late)."

他 八点 才 起床

Build: "I was already up at 6."

我 六点 就 起床 了

Fill with 才 or 就: 等他到了 ___ 告诉我。
(As soon as he gets here, tell me.)

就 — "…then (right away)". 就 also links two actions to mean "as soon as… then…".

The OTHER 就 — conditional / sequential "then"

When a speaker reaches for this: to say "once X happens, Y follows immediately" — "as soon as he gets here, tell me", "if it rains, let's not go", "finish this and then we'll eat". Same character, completely different meaning from the timing-attitude 就 above.

The English trap

The 就 you just learned means "earlier than expected" — an attitude about clock time. The 就 in 等他到了就告诉我 has nothing to do with expectations. It links two clauses: [condition / first event] → 就 → [immediate consequence]. English translates it as "then" or "as soon as".

[Condition / first event] , Subj + 就 + V + …

等他到了就告诉我。

Děng tā dào le jiù gàosu wǒ.

wait / he / arrive / PERF / then / tell / me

Once he arrives, tell me (right away).

外边下雨了，我们就不去了。

Wàibian xiàyǔ le, wǒmen jiù bù qù le.

outside / rain-START / we / then / not / go / SETTLED

It's raining outside — in that case, we won't go.

你写完作业就可以看电视。

Nǐ xiě wán zuòyè jiù kěyǐ kàn diànshì.

you / write-finish / homework / then / can / watch / TV

Once you finish your homework, you can watch TV.

Timing 就 (earlier than expected)
他六点就起床了。
He was already up at 6 — impressively early.
Sequential 就 (once X, then Y)
他起床了就吃早饭。
As soon as he gets up, he has breakfast.

How to tell them apart

Look at what comes before 就. A time expression (六点, 昨天, 五分钟) → timing 就. A clause describing an event or condition (等他到了, 写完作业) → sequential 就. The sequential version often pairs with 如果…, 只要…, or a bare event clause with a completion marker.

Practice

What does 就 mean here? 他一回家就睡觉。

  • He already sleeps by the time he gets home — impressively early.✗ "一…就…" is the sequential pattern, not timing.
  • As soon as he gets home, he goes to sleep.✓ 一…就… = "once X happens, Y immediately follows".
  • He only sleeps at home.✗ No "only" sense here; 就 links two events.

Build: "Once you finish your homework, you can watch TV."

你 写完 作业 就 可以 看电视

Fill the blank: 外边下雨了，我们___不去了。
(It's raining — in that case, we won't go.)

就 — "then / in that case". 就 links the rain (cause) to the not-going (consequence).

Which 就? 他五分钟就做完了。

  • Timing 就 — he finished in an impressively short 5 minutes.✓ 五分钟 is a time expression → timing 就 ("as little as 5 minutes").
  • Sequential 就 — first he did 5 minutes, then he finished.✗ There's no event clause before 就 here.
  • Neither — 就 is optional here.✗ 就 carries the "only X much time was needed" feel; removing it changes meaning.

2. 把字句 2: A 把 B V + 在 / 到 … Disposal, with a destination

When a speaker reaches for this: to say they put/sent/moved the object to a place. The "where does B end up" version of the 把 frame.

The English trap

English: "put the book on the table" — place after the verb. Chinese demands 把 here: 把书放在桌子上. You cannot write *放书在桌子上 (✗). When the verb means placing or moving something to a specific location, 把 is obligatory.

A + 把 + B + V + 在 / 到 + place

请把书放在桌子上。

Qǐng bǎ shū fàng zài zhuōzi shang.

please / [disposal] / book / put / at / table-on

Please put the book on the table.

我把行李送到机场了。

Wǒ bǎ xíngli sòng dào jīchǎng le.

I / [disposal] / luggage / deliver / to / airport / PERF

I took the luggage to the airport.

他把钱放在包里了。

Tā bǎ qián fàng zài bāo lǐ le.

He put the money in his bag.

Common pitfall

You can't split 在 / 到 from the verb. Always verb + 在/到 + place, inside the 把 frame. Writing 在 before the verb is the English-speaker instinct and always wrong here.

Practice

Build: "Please move the chair into the classroom."

请 把 椅子 搬 到 教室里

Pick the correct sentence:

  • 我放书在桌子上。✗ English order. Chinese needs 把 + V + 在/到 + place.
  • 我把书放在桌子上。✓ 把-frame with placement complement.
  • 我在桌子上把书放。✗ 在+place sits with the verb, not at the front.

What's the end state? 他把车开到学校了。

  • He drove away from school.✗ 到学校 = arrived at school.
  • The car ended up at school.✓ 开到学校 = drove (all the way) to school; the car is now there.
  • He is driving toward school now.✗ The 了 here signals completion — the car has arrived.

Build: "I took the luggage to the airport."

我 把 行李 送 到 机场 了

Build: "He put the money in his bag."

他 把 钱 放 在 包里 了

Fill the blank: 请把词典放 ___ 桌子上。
(Please put the dictionary on the table.)

在 — 放在 focuses on the resulting location (sitting on). 到 would emphasise movement to a destination.

Which sentence means "Don't forget to turn off the air conditioner"?

  • 别忘了把空调关了。✓ 把 disposes of 空调; 关了 marks completion.
  • 别忘了关空调把。✗ 把 must precede the object, not follow the verb.
  • 别把忘了关空调。✗ 把 needs a concrete object (空调), not 忘了.

Mixed practice Both points together

Build: "He already finished the homework by 8 (impressively early)."

他 八点 就 把 作业 写完 了

Fill with 才 or 就: 她五分钟 ___ 把电子邮件写完了。
(She finished the email in 5 minutes — fast!)

就 — five minutes is impressively fast, so 就 (not 才).

Build: "Please put the laptop on the table."

请 把 笔记本电脑 放 在 桌子上

What does this mean? 我九点才把词典还到图书馆。

  • I returned the dictionary at 9 — nice and early.✗ 才 signals "later than expected".
  • I didn't return the dictionary to the library until 9 (late).✓ 才 = only then; 还到图书馆 = returned it (all the way) to the library.
  • I will return the dictionary at 9.✗ The context is past; 才 complains about lateness that already happened.
