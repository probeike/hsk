Sheet 08 · Tier 1

Conjunctions: 和 · 因为…所以 · 虽然…但是 · 也 · 还

~25 min
Reference
Quick reminder

和 = "and" for NOUNS only. Don't use 和 to glue verbs, sentences, or clauses.

我和哥哥都在北京。 ✓    我吃饭和看电视。 ✗

因为…所以… — Chinese loves to keep both halves in place, unlike English which usually drops one. If the order is effect → cause, a common alternative is "A, 因为 B" with the 所以 dropped.

虽然…但是… — also frequently "double-marked". The 但是 can upgrade to 可是 / 不过 for a softer tone.

也 ("also") / 还 ("also, in addition") — both are pre-verbal adverbs.

  • 也 echoes the subject or predicate from the previous clause ("me too / I also do X").
  • 还 adds another thing of the same kind: "and also…".

Neither 也 nor 还 can start a sentence the way English "also" and "and" can.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: My brother and I are both in Beijing.
  2. Translate: Because it rained, I didn't go.
  3. Translate: Although it's cold, I'm very happy.
  4. Translate: I'm a student too.

Structured practice

  1. Fix: 我吃饭和看电视。
  2. Fix: 他很累，也他很高兴。
  3. Fill in 也 or 还:
    (a) 我喜欢喝茶，我哥哥 喜欢。 (b) 我买了一本书， 买了一支笔。 (c) 他会说汉语， 会说英语。 (d) 你去，我 去。
  4. Combine with 因为…所以…: 今天下雨。我不想出去。
  5. Combine with 虽然…但是…: 汉语很难。我很喜欢学。
  6. Translate: I have one older brother and two younger sisters.
  7. Translate: She's smart, and she's also very kind. (use 还)
  8. Translate: He's tired, but he still wants to go. (use 虽然…但是…)
  9. Error-spot: 他和我没来。和他妹妹没来。
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — Why aren't you going?
    — Because I have a lot of homework. But I'll go tomorrow.
    — Me too, I have a lot of homework.

Warm-up

  1. 我和哥哥都在北京。
  2. 因为下雨，所以我没去。
  3. 虽然很冷，但是我很高兴。
  4. 我也是学生。

Structured

  1. 我一边吃饭一边看电视。 or 我吃饭，(同时)看电视。 — 和 can't join actions. (A simpler fix: drop 和 and use a comma.)
  2. 他很累，但是他很高兴。 (也 can't be a sentence-connector here.)
  3. (a) 也 (b) 还 (c) 还 (d) 也
  4. 因为今天下雨，所以我不想出去。
  5. 虽然汉语很难，但是我很喜欢学。
  6. 我有一个哥哥和两个妹妹。
  7. 她很聪明，还很热心。
  8. 虽然他很累，但是还想去。
  9. You can't start a sentence with 和. Rewrite as 他、我和他妹妹都没来。
  10. 你为什么不去? — 因为我的作业很多。不过我明天会去。— 我也(是)，我的作业也很多。
