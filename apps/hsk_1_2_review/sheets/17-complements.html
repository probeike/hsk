Sheet 17 · Tier 2

Complements: 结果 & 得-state

~30 min
Reference
Quick reminder

Result complement — V + [result]

A second verb or adjective fuses onto the main verb to say what the result was. The result-word completes the idea. The pair acts like a single verb for 了/过/没 purposes.

我做完了作业。 I finished the homework.
我没听懂。 I didn't catch / understand it.
你说错了。 You said (it) wrong.

Common result words: 完 (finish), 到 (reach/obtain), 见 (perceive), 好 (well-done), 错 (wrongly), 懂 (understand), 会 (mastered), 清楚 (clearly).

Negate the whole cluster with 没: 没做完 / 没听懂 / 没看见.

State complement with 得

V + 得 + [state phrase] · if there's an object: V + O + V + 得 + [state phrase]

Describes how the action is/was done: fast, fluent, poorly.

你穿得太少了。 You're wearing too little.
她(说)汉语说得很流利。 She speaks Chinese very fluently.

Notice the double-verb when there's an object: the first verb carries the object, the second carries 得. The first verb can drop colloquially.

Negate the state, not the verb: 说得不好.

Don't confuse 得 (de, state-complement) with 的 (de, possessive/nominaliser). Different characters, same pinyin.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: Too many questions — I didn't finish.
  2. Translate: I didn't catch what you said.
  3. Translate: You're wearing too little.
  4. Translate: She speaks Chinese very fluently.

Structured practice

  1. Pick the best result complement: 完 / 到 / 见 / 好 / 错 / 懂 / 会
    (a) 我没买 票。 (couldn't manage to buy) (b) 你看 我的书了吗? (c) 作业已经做 了。 (d) 对不起，我说 了。 (e) 我学 开车了。 (f) 老师说的话你听 了吗?
  2. Negate: 我做完了作业。
  3. Fix: 他跑得不。
  4. Fix: 她说汉语得很好。
  5. Translate: He runs really fast.
  6. Translate: You did this exercise well.
  7. Translate: My handwriting isn't great, but I write quickly.
  8. Translate: Did you hear me clearly?
  9. Decide 得 or 的: 这是他写 字。 · 他写 很漂亮。
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — How did the exam go?
    — Not well. I didn't finish — there were too many questions.
    — Did you understand the teacher's explanation?
    — I understood some of it, but I wrote too slowly.

Warm-up

  1. 题太多，我没做完。
  2. 我没听懂(你说的)。
  3. 你穿得太少了。
  4. 她(说)汉语说得很流利。

Structured

  1. (a) 到 (b) 见 / 到 (c) 完 (d) 错 (e) 会 (f) 懂 / 清楚
  2. 我没(有)做完作业。
  3. 他跑得不快。 — the complement can't be bare; negate with the state word.
  4. 她(说)汉语说得很好。 — with an object, double the verb; 得 attaches to the second.
  5. 他跑得很快。
  6. 这个练习你做得很好。
  7. 我字写得不漂亮，不过写得很快。
  8. 你听清楚(我说的)了吗? / 你听清楚我了吗?
  9. 的 · 得 — "the character he writes" (possessive 的); "he writes beautifully" (state complement 得).
  10. 考试怎么样? — 不太好，没做完，题太多了。— 老师讲的你听懂了吗? — 听懂了一点儿，不过写得太慢了。
