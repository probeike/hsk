Review 01-05

周末到最近

Zhōumò dào zuìjìn

Cumulative review for Lessons 1-5

How to use this review

Try each item before opening a model answer. This page reviews Lessons 1-5: result complements, strong negation, 那, direction complements, successive actions, rhetorical questions, 还是/或者, location + 着, 会 possibility, 又…又, accompanying 着, change 了, 越来越, V得/不+C, N呢, and 刚/刚才.

1. Quick diagnostic Pick the meaning or missing form

Which sentence politely tells a host you are finished and satisfied?

  • 我吃好了，谢谢。好 adds the "done and satisfied" result.
  • 我吃完了，谢谢。完 only says the eating is finished.
  • 我吃了，谢谢。This only says you ate.

Which sentence means "I did not drink even one cup of tea"?

  • 我没有喝茶。This is plain negation, not "not even one."
  • 我一杯茶都没喝。一 + measure + noun + 都/也 + 没 gives strong negation.
  • 我喝了一杯茶。This says one cup was drunk.

What does the speaker mean?
你不去？那我也不去了。

  • Since you are not going, I am not going either.那 means "in that case."
  • That place is far away.This 那 is not the demonstrative "that."
  • I already went yesterday.No past trip is mentioned.

Fill with 还是 or 或者: 你想喝茶，___喝咖啡？

还是 asks the listener to choose between options.

What picture does this sentence create?
桌子上放着一杯茶。

  • Someone is actively putting tea on the table now.That would focus on the action 放.
  • There is a cup of tea sitting on the table.Location + V着 + noun describes a scene-state.
  • Someone wants to drink tea later.No plan is stated.

Which sentence predicts a likely result?

  • 你穿得太少，会感冒的。会 marks a likely possibility.
  • 你穿得太少，感冒了。This says the cold already happened.
  • 你穿得太少，感冒呢。呢 does not create a prediction here.

Fill the change pattern: 最近天气___了。 (The weather has been getting colder and colder.)

越来越冷 = more and more cold / getting colder and colder.

Which one means "just now" as a completed time point?

  • 我刚开始复习。刚 means "just / newly" before the verb.
  • 我刚才看见小丽了。刚才 is a time word: "just now."
  • 我越来越复习。越来越 does not fit this verb directly.

2. Contrast drills Do not let English choose for you

You have secured the movie tickets. Which one fits?

  • 电影票我买好了。买好 = bought and ready.
  • 电影票我买完了。买完 sounds like the buying action is finished, but not as naturally "sorted."
  • 电影票我买不了。This means you cannot buy them.

Fill with 还是 or 或者: 天气冷了___工作累了的时候，我想喝热茶。

Use 或者 inside a statement. 还是 is for a choice question.

Interpret the 着:
她笑着跟客人说话。

  • There is a smile sitting somewhere.This is not the location-existence 着.
  • She speaks to guests while smiling.V1着 + V2 means an accompanying manner/action.
  • She will smile tomorrow.No future meaning is present.

Which answer uses N + 呢 to ask where something is?

  • 我的地图呢？N + 呢 asks "where is N?"
  • 我去呢。This does not ask location.
  • 你呢喜欢茶？呢 cannot be placed there.

5. Mini-reading Characters first

小丽：周末你有什么打算？
小刚：我还没想好。你呢？
小丽：我想请几个同学来家里吃饭。桌子上放着很多饮料，还有面包。
小刚：你准备好了吗？
小丽：还没有。我找不到那条绿裤子，也找不到地图。
小刚：地图刚才还在椅子上。你要带地图吗？
小丽：当然。饭后我们会出去玩。最近天气越来越好，会很舒服的。
小刚：那我也来吧。

Xiǎolì: Zhōumò nǐ yǒu shénme dǎsuan?
Xiǎogāng: Wǒ hái méi xiǎng hǎo. Nǐ ne?
Xiǎolì: Wǒ xiǎng qǐng jǐ ge tóngxué lái jiā li chī fàn. Zhuōzi shang fàngzhe hěn duō yǐnliào, hái yǒu miànbāo.
Xiǎogāng: Nǐ zhǔnbèi hǎo le ma?
Xiǎolì: Hái méiyǒu. Wǒ zhǎo bu dào nà tiáo lǜ kùzi, yě zhǎo bu dào dìtú.
Xiǎogāng: Dìtú gāngcái hái zài yǐzi shang. Nǐ yào dài dìtú ma?
Xiǎolì: Dāngrán. Fàn hòu wǒmen huì chūqù wán. Zuìjìn tiānqì yuèláiyuè hǎo, huì hěn shūfu de.
Xiǎogāng: Nà wǒ yě lái ba.

小丽周末想做什么？

  • 请同学来家里吃饭She says she wants classmates to come eat at her home.
  • 去上课No class is mentioned.
  • 一直玩游戏No game plan is mentioned.

桌子上有什么？

  • 裤子和地图Those are the things she cannot find.
  • 饮料和面包桌子上放着很多饮料，还有面包。
  • 照片和衬衫Not in this passage.

地图刚才在哪儿？

  • 在椅子上地图刚才还在椅子上。
  • 在桌子上The table has drinks and bread.
  • 在学校School is not mentioned.

小刚最后做什么？

  • 不来了He does not refuse.
  • 也来那我也来吧。
  • 一个人出去玩No solo plan is stated.
