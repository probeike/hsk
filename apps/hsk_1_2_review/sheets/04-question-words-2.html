Sheet 04 · Tier 1

Question words II: 几 / 多少 / 怎么 / 怎么样 / 多

~25 min
Reference
Quick reminder

几 vs 多少 — both ask "how many", but:

  • 几 expects a small number (< 10) and requires a measure word.
  • 多少 is open-ended, measure word optional. Always use 多少 for money and phone numbers.

怎么 + V = "how to V" or (with surprise) "why on earth". 怎么样 stands alone as a predicate: "how is it / what's it like".

多 + adj asks how-something: 多大, 多高, 多远, 多长时间. Usually for measurable qualities.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: How many people are in your family?
  2. Translate: How many students are at your school?
  3. Translate: How old are you this year?
  4. Translate: What's the weather like tomorrow?

Structured practice

  1. 几 or 多少?
    (a) 今天星期 ? (b) 你的手机号码是 ? (c) 这件衣服 钱? (d) 你家有 口人? (e) 教室里有 个学生?
  2. Translate with 怎么: How do you write this character?
  3. Translate with 怎么 (surprise): How come you didn't eat?
  4. Translate with 怎么样: This restaurant — how is it?
  5. Translate with 多: How far is your home from the office?
  6. Fix: 你有多少书? (the speaker can see only a couple of books on the desk) — which question word would be more natural?
  7. Fix: 你家有几个人? — and why does HSK textbook prefer a different measure?
  8. Answer in a full sentence: 你每天睡多长时间?
  9. Translate: My older brother is three years older than I am. (hint: 多 returns in comparisons too; this one will reappear in sheet 18)
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — How's your Chinese?
    — So-so. How about yours?
    — My Chinese is a little better now.

Warm-up

  1. 你家有几口人? Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?
  2. 你们学校有多少学生? Nǐmen xuéxiào yǒu duōshao xuésheng?
  3. 你今年多大? Nǐ jīnnián duō dà?
  4. 明天天气怎么样? Míngtiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?

Structured

  1. (a) 几 (b) 多少 (c) 多少 (d) 几 (e) 多少 — 几 for weekdays and small/counted groups; 多少 for unknown large numbers, money, phone numbers.
  2. 这个字怎么写?
  3. 你怎么没吃? / 你怎么不吃了?
  4. 这家饭馆怎么样?
  5. 你家离公司多远?
  6. If only a few books are visible, use 几: 你有几本书?
  7. HSK prefers 几口人 for family members — 口 is the traditional measure word for family size; 个 sounds more like counting generic people.
  8. e.g. 我每天睡八个小时。
  9. 我哥哥比我大三岁。
  10. 你(的)汉语怎么样? — 马马虎虎。你呢? — 我的汉语现在好一点儿了。
