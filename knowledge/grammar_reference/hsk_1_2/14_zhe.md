# 着 — continuing state

**V + 着** freezes the action into its *resulting state*. It describes the way something currently *is*, not the moment of doing.

门开着呢。 The door is open.

他穿着一件红色的衣服。 He's wearing red clothes.

**着 vs 在…呢**:

- 他在开门呢。 — he's in the act of opening the door.

- 门开着。 — the door is (standing) open.

**V₁着 V₂** — doing V₂ *while* V₁-ing: the V₁着 describes the manner.

他笑着说。 He said, smiling.

我喜欢站着学习。 I like to study standing up.

**Negation**: 没(有) + V + 着: 门没开着。

Typical 着-compatible verbs describe states: 开、关、穿、戴、拿、坐、站、躺、放、写.
