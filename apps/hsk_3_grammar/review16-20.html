Zuìhòu zōnghé

Cumulative review for Lessons 16-20

How to use this review

This final page reviews Lessons 16-20: ABAB verb reduplication, matched flexible question words, 只要…就, 关于, extended directionals, 使/叫/让, 不但…而且, 被, 只有…才, and final 把/被/着/了 synthesis.

1. Quick diagnostic Identify the grammar job

What does the reduplication do?
你检查检查这个句子。

  • Check the sentence a bit / give it a check.ABAB softens and makes the action brief or casual.
  • Check the sentence again and again for a long time.The reduplication is not an intensifier here.
  • The sentence was checked by someone.That would need 被.

Which matched question-word sentence is complete?

  • 你怎么做，我就怎么做。The same question word appears in both clauses.
  • 你怎么做，我就做。The second 怎么 is missing.
  • 你怎么做，我就什么做。The repeated question word must match.

Which sentence means "As long as you agree, I will go"?

  • 只要你同意，我就去。只要…就… gives a sufficient condition.
  • 只有你同意，我才去。This is stricter: only if you agree.
  • 如果你同意，我才去。如果 normally pairs with 就, not 才.

Fill the preposition: ___这个问题，我想听听你的想法。

关于 sets the topic: regarding this question.

What does the directional complement mean here?
我没看出来他是小刚。

  • I could not tell from looking that he was Xiaogang.看出来 means "recognize / figure out visually."
  • I did not see Xiaogang come out.That would be 看见他出来.
  • I kept looking at Xiaogang.Keep looking would use 看下去.

Which sentence uses 使 in its most natural textbook slot?

  • 这个故事使我很感动。使 fits an abstract cause plus emotion.
  • 妈妈使我去买东西。For everyday instructions, use 让 or 叫.
  • 老师使我们下课以后来。This is an instruction; 让/叫 is natural.

Which sentence correctly uses 不但…而且…?

  • 他不但会说中文，而且说得很好。不但 introduces the first gain; 而且 adds another.
  • 他不但会说中文，但是说得很好。但是 contradicts; 而且 adds.
  • 他而且会说中文，不但说得很好。The pair is in the wrong order.

Who acted in the 被 sentence?
我的照相机被朋友拿走了。

  • The friend took my camera away.The subject is the affected thing; 被 introduces the doer.
  • I took my friend's camera.That follows the English first-noun trap.
  • The camera took my friend away.The camera is the patient, not the actor.

Fill the necessary-condition hinge: 只有努力学习，___能考得好。

只有…才… = only if / only under this condition.

2. Contrast drills Choose the right frame

Which sentence is the weakest condition: "if this is enough, then..."?

  • 只要天气好，我们就去。Good weather is sufficient.
  • 只有天气好，我们才去。Good weather is necessary; stronger claim.
  • 天气好，我们才去。Without 只有, the condition is less explicit.

Compare 把 and 被:
朋友把我的书拿走了。 / 我的书被朋友拿走了。

  • Same event, different focus: friend as doer vs book as affected thing.把 foregrounds what the doer does to the object; 被 foregrounds the affected object.
  • The two sentences describe opposite events.The event is the same.
  • Only the 把 sentence has a result.Both use 拿走了.

Which sentence means "keep speaking"?

  • 说起来This can mean "speaking of..." or "start speaking," depending on context.
  • 说下去下去 extends the action forward in time: continue.
  • 说出来This means say it out / express it.

For an everyday instruction, which causative is natural?

  • 老师让我们用汉语回答问题。让 is the everyday causative for having someone do something.
  • 老师使我们用汉语回答问题。使 is too formal/abstract for a concrete instruction.
  • 老师被我们用汉语回答问题。被 is passive and breaks the roles here.

5. Mini-reading The final synthesis

小丽：我的照相机被朋友借走了，我刚才很难过。
小刚：你看出来他为什么借了吗？
小丽：现在看出来了。他想试试新的照相机，可是没先告诉我。
小刚：这件事使你生气，也让他不好意思吧。
小丽：是啊。后来我们关于这件事谈了谈。只要他先讲清楚，我就会同意。
小刚：只有把事情说清楚，朋友才不会难过。
小丽：对。问题终于被解决了。他不但把照相机还回来了，而且说了对不起。

Xiǎolì: Wǒ de zhàoxiàngjī bèi péngyou jiè zǒu le, wǒ gāngcái hěn nánguò.
Xiǎogāng: Nǐ kàn chūlai tā wèishénme jiè le ma?
Xiǎolì: Xiànzài kàn chūlai le. Tā xiǎng shìshi xīn de zhàoxiàngjī, kěshì méi xiān gàosu wǒ.
Xiǎogāng: Zhè jiàn shì shǐ nǐ shēngqì, yě ràng tā bù hǎoyìsi ba.
Xiǎolì: Shì a. Hòulái wǒmen guānyú zhè jiàn shì tán le tán. Zhǐyào tā xiān jiǎng qīngchu, wǒ jiù huì tóngyì.
Xiǎogāng: Zhǐyǒu bǎ shìqing shuō qīngchu, péngyou cái bú huì nánguò.
Xiǎolì: Duì. Wèntí zhōngyú bèi jiějué le. Tā búdàn bǎ zhàoxiàngjī hái huílai le, érqiě shuō le duìbuqǐ.

照相机发生了什么事？

  • 被朋友借走了我的照相机被朋友借走了。
  • 被老师拿走了The doer is a friend, not a teacher.
  • 被小丽卖了No selling is mentioned.

朋友为什么借照相机？

  • 想试试新的照相机他想试试新的照相机。
  • 想买电脑No computer is involved.
  • 想去留学No study-abroad plan is mentioned.

小丽说什么条件下她会同意？

  • 只要朋友先讲清楚只要他先讲清楚，我就会同意。
  • 只有天气好Weather is not the condition.
  • 如果朋友不还回来He did return it.

最后问题怎么样了？

  • 越来越严重了The passage says it was solved.
  • 终于被解决了问题终于被解决了。
  • 谁都不说话了They talked about it.

昨天我的书被同事借走了，我开始有点儿难过。后来我们关于这件事谈了谈。他的话让我想起来，我也常常忘记告诉别人。只要先讲清楚，大家就不会生气。最后问题被解决了，他不但把书还回来了，而且请我喝咖啡。
