ng 四时开口度较大。

When saying the alveolar nasal [nj, the tongue tip should press the upper alveolar
ridge; when saying the velar nasal ng[n], the back part of the tongue forms an arch and the
root of the tongue moves back and presses the soft palate. Compared with n[n], which is
pronounced with the upper and lower teeth close to each other, ng [ID] is pronounced with
the mouth more wide-open.
