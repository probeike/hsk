# 了 part 2 — change of state & imminent action

**Sentence-final 了**: the situation *is now different from before*. Often means "now", "already", "finally".

我会说汉语了。 Now I can speak Chinese (I couldn't before).

下雨了。 It's (started) raining.

他不吃了。 He's not eating (anymore).

**都…了** — "already, as much as" — flags that something has reached a surprising point.

都十点了，他还没来。
It's already 10 and he still isn't here.

**Imminent action** — "about to":

- (快)要 + V + 了 — general "about to"

- 快 + V + 了 — colloquial "about to"

- 就要 + V + 了 — "will soon"; *can* take a specific time phrase: 明天就要到了

Rule: only 就要…了 accepts a time adverb. 快要…了 doesn't.
