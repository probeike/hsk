Sheet 12 · Tier 2

了 part 2 — change of state & imminent action

~30 min
Reference
Quick reminder

Sentence-final 了: the situation is now different from before. Often means "now", "already", "finally".

我会说汉语了。 Now I can speak Chinese (I couldn't before).
下雨了。 It's (started) raining.
他不吃了。 He's not eating (anymore).

都…了 — "already, as much as" — flags that something has reached a surprising point.

都十点了，他还没来。 It's already 10 and he still isn't here.

Imminent action — "about to":

  • (快)要 + V + 了 — general "about to"
  • 快 + V + 了 — colloquial "about to"
  • 就要 + V + 了 — "will soon"; can take a specific time phrase: 明天就要到了

Rule: only 就要…了 accepts a time adverb. 快要…了 doesn't.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: It's raining (it started).
  2. Translate: I can speak Chinese now.
  3. Translate: The new year is about to arrive.
  4. Translate: It's already 10 and he still isn't here.

Structured practice

  1. Which flavour of 了? Label each A (completed action, sheet 11) or B (change of state):
    (a) 我吃了三个苹果。 (b) 我不饿了。 (c) 他六点就来了。 (d) 下雨了。 (e) 我会开车了。
  2. Translate: Why aren't you eating anymore?
  3. Translate: The train is about to leave. (use 快要)
  4. Translate: Tomorrow the holiday will start. (use 就要)
  5. Fix: 明天快要放假了。
  6. Translate with 都…了: I'm already 30 — I really should get a job.
  7. Translate: It's getting cold — wear more clothes.
  8. Translate: My younger sister is now in first grade.
  9. Error-spot: 我现在不想吃。 (intended: I'm not hungry anymore — use 了.)
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — How come you're not eating anymore?
    — I'm full. It's already 8 — I have to leave.
    — It's going to rain — take an umbrella.
