“不可同日而语”的意思是不能放在同一个时间谈论，

来比喻差别很大，不能放在一起比较。例如: 通信技术;

步太快了，过去跟现在不可同日而语。

“不可同日而语”literally“(two things) cannot be mentioned
in the same breath”, is used metaphorically to mean that there
is no comparison between two people or two things because of |
the significant difference. For instance, in terms of science and
technology, there is no comparison between the past and the

present.
