Custom reading 03 · Aspect

我还没吃早饭呢！

Wǒ hái méi chī zǎofàn ne!

Reading & writing practice — action status and aspect

About this reading

Everything here uses only HSK 1–2 words plus HSK 3 lessons 1–8 and the twelve new words from the Aspect lesson. Every passage walks an action along the line you learnt there: 还没…呢 → 正在…呢 → 快要/就要…了 → 已经…了 / 刚, plus the four future-intent words 要 · 想 · 会 · 打算. The markers are highlighted in the passages so you can see the frame before you read the meaning.

Read the characters first. Tap Show pinyin only when you're genuinely stuck — the pinyin is hidden on purpose.

1. 课文 A phone call at eight in the morning

小丽：喂，马可，你起床了吗？
马可：我刚起床。昨天晚上我睡得不好，今天起得很晚。
小丽：你吃早饭了吗？
马可：还没吃呢。我正在准备呢。
小丽：你今天上午有什么打算？
马可：我要去练习舞蹈，下午还要工作。
小丽：你还在学韩国的舞蹈吗？
马可：对，我最喜欢韩国的舞蹈。我们的舞蹈老师跳得非常好，人也很漂亮。
小丽：你们几点上课？
马可：九点半。现在快九点了，我要快一点儿。
小丽：那早饭呢？你还没吃呢。
马可：我吃一点儿水果，中午再吃饭。
小丽：你太忙了。最近工作怎么样？
马可：公司的事情越来越多，压力很大。两个星期以后我就要辞职了。
小丽：真的吗？辞职以后你想做什么？
马可：我想当舞蹈老师，也想当老板。
小丽：好啊！你告诉老板了吗？
马可：已经告诉他了。他不太高兴，可是他明白。
小丽：那我不说了，你快去上课吧！
马可：好，晚上见！

马可今天为什么起得很晚？ (Why did Make get up late today?)

  • 昨天晚上他睡得不好✓ 昨天晚上我睡得不好，今天起得很晚。
  • 他生病了✗ No illness is mentioned anywhere.
  • 他昨天晚上去跳舞了✗ The dancing is this morning's plan, not last night.

小丽打电话的时候，马可吃早饭了吗？ (Had Make eaten breakfast when Xiaoli called?)

  • 还没吃，他正在准备✓ 还没吃呢。我正在准备呢。Station 1 heading for station 2.
  • 已经吃完了✗ That would be 已经吃完了 — the opposite end of the line.
  • 他不吃早饭✗ He does eat — he says he'll have some fruit.

马可为什么要辞职？ (Why is Make quitting?)

  • 公司的事情越来越多，压力很大✓ 公司的事情越来越多，压力很大。
  • 他要搬家✗ Moving isn't mentioned in this conversation.
  • 老板让他走✗ It's his own decision — 我就要辞职了.

老板知道马可要辞职吗？ (Does the boss know?)

  • 知道，马可已经告诉他了✓ 已经告诉他了。他不太高兴，可是他明白。
  • 不知道，马可还没告诉他呢✗ That was true earlier, but by now 已经告诉他了.
  • 知道，是小丽告诉他的✗ Xiaoli is only just hearing about it herself.

3. 短文 Two weeks' notice

我住在纽约，是一个软件工程师。我的工作很忙，公司的事情越来越多，压力也越来越大。两个月以前我搬家了，因为以前的家离公司太远，每天早上我都起不来。搬家以后，我离公司近了，可是压力还是很大。最近两个月我一直没练习汉语，也几乎没时间跳舞。 我最喜欢的事情是跳舞，特别是韩国的舞蹈。以前我每个星期都去练习，现在我不去了。上个月我想：我不能再等了。 那时候我还没告诉老板呢。我正在准备，也在想以后的事情。现在我已经告诉他了。他知道以后不太高兴，只说：“我明白。”我的同事说：“真的吗？我们会想你。” 两个星期以后我就要离开公司了。辞职以后我打算做什么？我想当舞蹈老师，也想开一个小舞蹈教室，当老板。我知道这不容易，可是我会很快乐。现在我还没开始呢，可是快要开始了。

他两个月以前为什么搬家？ (Why did he move house two months ago?)

  • 以前的家离公司太远✓ 因为以前的家离公司太远，每天早上我都起不来。
  • 他要开一个舞蹈教室✗ That's a future plan, not the reason for the move.
  • 以前的家太小了✗ Size is never mentioned — distance is.

最近两个月他的汉语练习得怎么样？ (How has his Chinese practice gone lately?)

  • 他一直没练习✓ 最近两个月我一直没练习汉语 — plain 没, a closed report on those two months.
  • 他每天都练习一个小时✗ That's someone else, later in this file.
  • 他还没开始练习呢，可是明天就要开始了✗ The passage never gives a start date for Chinese practice.

  • 不太高兴，可是说“我明白”✓ 他知道以后不太高兴，只说：“我明白。”
  • 很高兴✗ 不太高兴 — the opposite.
  • 他还不知道呢✗ 现在我已经告诉他了.

7. 改写 Move the passage along the line

Each item quotes a real sentence from the passages above and then moves the clock. Your job is to re-station it. Nothing you type here appears anywhere in the passages — you have to build it.

Transform. The 短文 ends: 现在我还没开始呢，可是快要开始了。
Half a year has gone by and the dance studio is running — he has already started teaching dance. Rewrite that at station 4. Talk about him as 他.
用: 他 · 已经 · 开始 · 教舞蹈 · 了

他已经开始教舞蹈了。 — station 1 → station 4 swaps both ends at once: 还没 at the front becomes 已经, and 呢 at the back becomes 了. The verb in the middle never changes shape. Dropping the final 了 (他已经开始教舞蹈 ✗) leaves 已经 with no partner — that's half a structure, not a sentence.

Transform. In the 课文 马可 says: 还没吃呢。我正在准备呢。
Twenty minutes later he is nearly there — he's about to be ready. No time is given, so don't invent one. Rewrite it at station 3, talking about him as 他.
用: 他 · 快要 · 准备好 · 了

他快要准备好了。 — the 正在…呢 wrapper comes off and the 快要…了 frame goes on; again the verb itself is untouched. Two things to check: the closing 了 is compulsory (他快要准备好 ✗), and because no time is stated, 快要 is the right marker. Put a time back in and it must flip: 五分钟以后他就要准备好了 — a stated time forces 就要.

8. 回答问题 Answer in a full sentence

Both questions are about the passages, but neither answer is written there. Read the question, work out which station the answer sits at, and type the whole reply.

Answer the question. 短文里的这个人离开公司了吗？
No — he still has two weeks to go, and he is definitely going. Start with 他.
用: 还没 · 离开 · 呢

他还没离开呢。 (or 他还没离开公司呢) — a 了吗 question about a completed action is answered in the negative with 没, never 不, and the 了 from the question never survives into the answer: 他还没离开了 ✗. The 还…呢 pair is what tells your listener the departure is still coming.

Answer the question. The 课文 tells you 马可's dance class starts at 九点半. So: 十点的时候，马可在做什么呢？
Start with 他 and freeze the action at ten o'clock.
用: 正在 (or 在) · 上课 · 呢

他正在上课呢。 — the question already set up the freeze-frame with 在…呢, so the reply matches it. Ten o'clock is half an hour into a class that began at 九点半: the action has started and hasn't finished, which is exactly what station 2 is for. Answering 他上课了 would report the class as a finished event, and 他要上课 would put it back in the future.

9. 翻译 English → Chinese

Translate. "The bus hasn't come yet."
用: 公共汽车 · 还没 · 来 · 呢

公共汽车还没来呢。 — English carries "yet" in a separate little word at the end; Chinese carries it in 还, in front of 没. Two traps: 公共汽车没来了 ✗ (a negated verb never takes 了) and 公共汽车不来 ✗ (不 would mean the bus refuses to come at all). Drop the 还 and 公共汽车没来 becomes a closed report — it never turned up.

Translate. "Next Wednesday my parents are coming to see me."
用: 下个星期三 · 爸爸妈妈 · 就要 · 来看我 · 了

下个星期三我爸爸妈妈就要来看我了。 — two decisions, both forced. (1) A day is named, so the marker must be 就要: 下个星期三我爸爸妈妈快要来看我了 ✗ — 快要 will not stand next to a stated time. (2) The closing 了 is part of the frame, not decoration. Take the day away and the same news becomes 我爸爸妈妈快要来看我了 — "soon", no date. The time phrase may sit before or after the subject, but never at the end.

10. 改错 Fix the bad summary

Three errors. Someone summarised the 短文 in three sentences and made exactly one mistake in each. The facts are all right — the grammar isn't. Find the three, write the corrected summary, then check.
最近两个月他一直没练习了汉语。他已经告诉老板他要辞职。两个星期以后他快要离开公司了。

最近两个月他一直没练习汉语。他已经告诉老板他要辞职了。两个星期以后他就要离开公司了。

  1. 没练习了汉语 → 没练习汉语. 没 and 了 cannot share a clause. 了 marks a boundary that was crossed; 没 says no crossing happened, so there is nothing left for 了 to mark. This one error accounts for more broken sentences in this lesson than the other two put together.
  2. 已经告诉老板他要辞职 → …要辞职了. 已经 is only half a structure and needs its sentence-final 了. English already is a free-standing adverb — "he already told his boss" is a whole sentence — and that is exactly why the 了 goes missing here.
  3. 快要 → 就要. 两个星期以后 is a stated time, and 快要 refuses to stand next to one. Memory hook: 就 already means "right then", so it is the marker that can hold hands with a clock. The 了 at the end was fine — it was the marker that was wrong.

11. 复述 Retell it from memory

Cover the 短文 — scroll it off the screen, don't look back — and retell the story in 3–4 sentences of your own, walking it along the line in this order: what is already done (已经…了) → what is under way right now (正在…呢) → what is about to happen (就要…了). Use your own words; a retell is not a copy.

短文里的这个人住在纽约，是软件工程师。因为公司的事情越来越多，压力太大，他已经决定辞职了，也已经告诉老板了。现在他正在准备以后的事情呢：他想当舞蹈老师，也想开一个小舞蹈教室。两个星期以后他就要走了，那时候他会很快乐。

Duǎnwén lǐ de zhège rén zhù zài Niǔyuē, shì ruǎnjiàn gōngchéngshī. Yīnwèi gōngsī de shìqing yuèláiyuè duō, yālì tài dà, tā yǐjīng juédìng cízhí le, yě yǐjīng gàosu lǎobǎn le. Xiànzài tā zhèngzài zhǔnbèi yǐhòu de shìqing ne: tā xiǎng dāng wǔdǎo lǎoshī, yě xiǎng kāi yí ge xiǎo wǔdǎo jiàoshì. Liǎng ge xīngqī yǐhòu tā jiù yào zǒu le, nà shíhou tā huì hěn kuàilè.

The man in the passage lives in New York and is a software engineer. Because there is more and more to do at the company and the pressure is too much, he has already decided to quit, and he has already told his boss. Right now he's getting his next step ready: he'd like to be a dance teacher, and to open a small dance studio. In two weeks he'll be gone, and he thinks he'll be very happy then.

Check your own retell:

  • Does it actually travel — done, then under way, then about to — rather than list three unconnected facts?
  • Every 已经 sentence closed with 了; the 正在 sentence closed with 呢?
  • You named the time (两个星期以后), so is the marker 就要 and not 快要?
  • No 了 anywhere after a 没. Check every negative sentence.
  • Are these your sentences? If a whole line matches the 短文 word for word, rewrite it — say 他决定辞职 instead of quoting 我不能再等了.

12. 写一写 Your own life, one station at a time

Personalise it. Write four true sentences about your own life, one at each station, in this order:

  1. Something you have already done this year — 已经…了
  2. Something you are in the middle of right now — 正在…呢
  3. Something happening on a named day soon — 就要…了
  4. Something you still haven't done and intend to — 还没…呢

Four different verbs, please — and four things that are genuinely true this week.

今年我已经看完三本汉语书了。现在我正在跟姐姐聊天儿呢。下个星期六我朋友就要过生日了。可是他的礼物我还没买呢。

Jīnnián wǒ yǐjīng kàn wán sān běn Hànyǔ shū le. Xiànzài wǒ zhèngzài gēn jiějie liáotiānr ne. Xià ge xīngqīliù wǒ péngyou jiù yào guò shēngrì le. Kěshì tā de lǐwù wǒ hái méi mǎi ne.

This year I've already finished three Chinese books. Right now I'm chatting with my older sister. Next Saturday is my friend's birthday. But I still haven't bought his present.

Check your own four sentences:

  • Four sentences, four different stations, four different verbs?
  • Sentence 1 ends in 了 — that one is compulsory, 已经 alone is half a structure. Sentence 2 ends in 呢: strictly optional after 正在, but write it in here so the pair becomes automatic.
  • Sentence 3 names a day, so the marker is 就要 — 快要 refuses a stated time.
  • Sentence 4: is 还 in front of 没, and is the verb bare? No 了 after a 没, ever.
  • Is number 4 something you actually still plan to do? If you've given up on it, 还没…呢 is the wrong frame — that's just 我没….
