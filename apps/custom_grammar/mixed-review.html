HSK 2 Review · Sheet 06 · Mixed review

我是去年搬到纽约的，现在我家离公司很近，我过得比以前好得多。

Wǒ shì qùnián bāndào Niǔyuē de, xiànzài wǒ jiā lí gōngsī hěn jìn, wǒ guò de bǐ yǐqián hǎo de duō.

It was last year that I moved to New York; now I live close to work, and my life is much better than before.

What this sheet is

No new grammar. This is the finale: everything from sheets 1–5 and the four custom lessons, shuffled so that no two exercises in a row test the same thing — which is the only way to find out whether you actually own a pattern or were just riding the momentum of a page about it. Twenty-five items, nine structure families, every family at least twice, and not one sentence you have seen anywhere else in this app.

Look at the title sentence again. It is not one grammar point, it is four stacked into a single breath — and that stacking is the real HSK 2 exit exam:

1. Warm-up Five quick ones, five different sheets — no two in a row alike

C3 What does this mean?
客人还没到呢。

  • The guests didn't come.✗ That closed report is 客人没来 — no 还, no 呢. It would fit an evening nobody turned up to.
  • The guests aren't here yet.✓ 还 is the word that carries "yet"; 呢 holds the situation open. They are still expected — Sheet C3, station 1.
  • The guests don't want to come.✗ A wish or a refusal takes 不, never 没: 客人不想来。

S3 Fill in the result complement: 刚才我在电梯里看 ___ 你妹妹了。
(I just saw your sister in the lift.) One character glued straight onto 看 — the one that says your eyes actually landed on her.

见 (到 also works) — this is Sheet 3's point: 看 is only the looking, and the complement is what reports the outcome of the looking. Bare 我看你妹妹了 says you had a look at her, not that you spotted her by chance.

S1 Which sentence has a mistake?

  • 这次的成绩比上次好一点儿。This one is fine — the degree word sits after the adjective, exactly where 比 wants it. Keep looking.
  • 这个练习比那个非常难。✓ Found it. 很 / 非常 / 太 / 真 are all banned inside a 比 sentence. Say 比那个难得多 or 比那个难一点儿 — the amount goes after the adjective.
  • 这个房间没有那个干净。This one is fine — to say "not as … as", you don't negate 比, you swap it for 没有. Keep looking.

C2 What does this mean?
前两年他一直在医院工作。

  • Two years ago he worked at a hospital.✗ That single point on the line is 两年前 — number first, 前 second.
  • For the last couple of years he's been working at a hospital.✓ 前 in front of the number marks off a stretch of time running up to now — and 一直 confirms it: a span, not a moment.
  • He's going to work at a hospital for the next two years.✗ Nothing here points forward. A 前 span always runs backwards from now.

C4 Fill in one character: 上个星期我一直在家，一分钱 ___ 没花。
(I was home all last week — I didn't spend a single cent.) The word that turns "one cent" from a count into "not even one".

也 or 都 — either is fine, and that is Sheet C4's shape: 一 + measure word + 也/都 + 没/不 + verb. Drop the 也/都 and 一分钱没花 collapses into a plain count; the emphasis lives entirely in that one character.

2. Build it Word order is where these patterns actually break

S2 Build: "It takes forty minutes by taxi from our hotel to the train station." (Two orders are accepted — the two coverb phrases can swap. What is fixed is that both sit in front of 要.)

从我们的宾馆到火车站 坐出租车 要 四十分钟

C1 "The children ran happily into the classroom." One character apart — which one is right?

  • 孩子们高兴地跑进教室。✓ 地 hooks a manner word onto the front of a verb: 高兴地跑. That is the whole job of 地.
  • 孩子们高兴得跑进教室。✗ 得 comes after the verb and introduces a judgement of how it went (跑得很快). Here 高兴 arrives first, so 得 has nothing to hang off.
  • 孩子们高兴的跑进教室。✗ 的 must be followed by a noun (高兴的孩子们 = happy children). Putting a verb after it is the classic slip.

S5 Fill in one character: 你看，爷爷还拿 ___ 那张照片呢。
(Look — Grandpa is still holding that photo.) Nobody is picking anything up; a position he took earlier is simply still true.

着 — Sheet 5's middle marker. 了 would report the picking-up as a finished event; 着 reports the state left behind by it, which is what 还…呢 is pointing at. Rule of thumb: if a photograph of the scene would still show it, use 着.

S4 Build: "This skirt — I bought it at the airport." (Two orders are accepted: topic first, or the object parked after 的.)

这条裙子 是 我 在机场 买 的

C3 Fill in one character: 明年九月他 ___ 要上大学了。
(Next September he's off to university.) A date is on the table — and only one of the two "about to" markers will stand next to a date.

就 → 就要…了. This is Sheet C3's station-3 rule: 快要 refuses a stated time, so 明年九月他快要上大学了 ✗. Memory hook: 就 already means "right then", so it is the one that can hold hands with a calendar. The closing 了 is compulsory either way.

3. Mixed bag Six decisions, six sheets, no warning which is which

S5 小刚 asks: 你吃过中国菜吗？ You want to say "yes — lots of times, over the years." Which reply?

  • 我吃过很多次中国菜。✓ 过 counts experiences across a whole life. No "when" is needed, and none is implied.
  • 我吃了很多次中国菜。✗ 了 packages the eating into one bounded stretch, so it needs a frame around it — 那个星期我吃了很多次. On its own it sounds like you're mid-story.
  • 我很多次吃中国菜。✗ A count of times goes after the verb in Chinese (吃过很多次), never in front of it. This is English word order wearing Chinese characters.

C1 Fill with 的, 得 or 地: 他今天来 ___ 特别早，八点半就到公司了。

得 — this is Sheet C1's positional test, and it beats trying to remember meanings. The gap sits after the verb and what follows it is a judgement of how the arriving went, so it can only be 得. 地 would have to come before 来 (他特别早地来 — unnatural here), and 的 would demand a noun after it.

S2 "Mum made me a bowl of noodles." Which one?

  • 妈妈给我做了一碗面条。✓ 给 is a coverb, so it and the person it serves both sit in front of the verb they belong to.
  • 妈妈做了一碗面条给我。✗ English parks "for me" at the end; Chinese will not. The coverb phrase leads, the main verb follows.
  • 妈妈对我做了一碗面条。✗ 对 marks an attitude aimed at someone (对我很好, 对我说). The coverb that hands something over is 给.

C4 Which sentence has a mistake?

  • 这次考试太难了，我一个问题也没做对。This one is fine — a past non-event, so 没, and the complement 对 reports the outcome. Keep looking.
  • 那天我很生气，一句中文也不说。✓ Found it. 那天 pins this to one past occasion, and a past non-event takes 没: 一句中文也没说。 不 would make it a standing refusal ("I refuse to speak a word of Chinese").
  • 我叔叔一点儿甜的都不吃。This one is fine — a lifelong habit, so 不 is exactly right. 一点儿…都不 is the mass-noun version of the pattern. Keep looking.

S3 You went through your bag looking for your phone, and it was in there. Which sentence reports that?

  • 我在包里找到手机了。✓ 找 is the trying; 到 is the succeeding. Without a complement, 找 says nothing about whether you got there.
  • 我在包里找完手机了。✗ 完 says the activity ran to its end — you finished searching — which is compatible with never finding it at all.
  • 我在包里看见手机了。✗ Grammatical, but it means you happened to spot it. It deletes the searching, which was the whole point.

C2 Fill in one character: ___ 两天一直下雨，我每天都在家看电视。
(It's rained non-stop for the past couple of days — I've been at home watching TV every day.) One character, and its position is the whole exercise.

前 → 前两天. Sheet C2's headline contrast: 前 + number marks off a stretch running up to now, while number + 前 (两天前) marks a single point. 一直 and 每天 both demand a stretch, so 两天前 would contradict the rest of the sentence.

4. Longer stretches Two sheets per sentence — and everything typed out in full

From here on, nothing is a click. Every item below wants a whole sentence typed in Chinese, and most of them make you operate two sheets at once. Where the prompt names the words you must use, use them — that's what keeps the grader honest. If your answer is rejected but you think it's fine, use the AI check.

S1 Transform. 弟弟的房间比我的干净。 → say the same fact from the other side. Start with 我的房间, use 没有 instead of 比, and keep the adjective 干净. Type the whole sentence.

我的房间没有弟弟的干净。 — Sheet 1's rule: to say "not as … as", you do not negate 比. 我的房间不比弟弟的干净 is a different, argumentative sentence ("it's not as if mine were cleaner"), and 我的房间没比弟弟的干净 ✗ is simply broken. 比 gets replaced by 没有, and the adjective stays bare — no 很.

S2 · S5 Write the paragraph — 女儿的第一次。 An afternoon in the park where a child rides a bike for the first time: how you got there, which way you walked, what she had never done before, what the person on the next bench was doing, what somebody said to you, who you told that evening. Write it in five sentences, using all six of 从 · 往 · 对 · 给 · 过 · 着.
Two sheets in one paragraph: the coverbs go before the verb they serve, the aspect markers go straight after it. Decide which kind each word is before you place it.

上个星期天下午我带女儿去公园，我们从家走过去，只用了二十分钟。进了公园往左走五分钟就能看见那些大树。女儿以前没骑过自行车，那天她学得特别快。旁边有一位阿姨坐着看报纸，她对我说：你女儿真聪明。晚上我给她奶奶发了一个电子邮件，告诉她这件事。

Shàng ge xīngqītiān xiàwǔ wǒ dài nǚ'ér qù gōngyuán, wǒmen cóng jiā zǒu guòqu, zhǐ yòng le èrshí fēnzhōng. Jìn le gōngyuán wǎng zuǒ zǒu wǔ fēnzhōng jiù néng kànjiàn nàxiē dà shù. Nǚ'ér yǐqián méi qí guo zìxíngchē, nà tiān tā xué de tèbié kuài. Pángbiān yǒu yí wèi āyí zuò zhe kàn bàozhǐ, tā duì wǒ shuō: nǐ nǚ'ér zhēn cōngming. Wǎnshang wǒ gěi tā nǎinai fā le yí ge diànzǐ yóujiàn, gàosu tā zhè jiàn shì.

Last Sunday afternoon I took my daughter to the park; we walked there from home and it only took twenty minutes. Once inside the park you go left for five minutes and the big trees come into view. My daughter had never ridden a bike before, and that day she picked it up remarkably fast. On the next bench a lady sat reading a newspaper; she said to me, your daughter really is clever. That evening I emailed her grandmother to tell her about it.

Self-check — one line per word:

  • 从: did the journey land? 从 A needs a motion verb and an end point — 走过去, 走到公园. And it is 从, not 离: 离 measures a gap that nobody crosses.
  • 往: a direction word (前／左／右) handed straight to 走, sitting in front of it — not a destination, not after the verb.
  • 过: glued to the back of the verb, and the negative is 没 with no 了 anywhere near it (没骑过自行车 ✓ · 没骑过了 ✗).
  • 着: 坐着看报纸 — 着 marks the posture that frames the main action, and the main verb follows it bare (坐了看报纸 ✗).
  • 对 and 给: both load their person in front of the verb — 对我说, 给她奶奶发. English puts the person last ("said to me"); that word order is the whole trap.

C4 Transform. 昨天下午他没喝水。 → turn the plain negative into an emphatic one: "he didn't drink a single glass of water yesterday afternoon." Keep 昨天下午 at the front and the subject 他; use 一杯 and 也 (or 都).

昨天下午他一杯水也没喝。 — Sheet C4's frame: 一 + measure word + noun + 也/都 + 没 + verb. Two things move at once. The object 水 jumps in front of the verb (他也没喝一杯水 ✗), and the negative stays 没 because 昨天下午 pins it to one past afternoon.

S4 · S5 Transform. 他们坐船去那个城市了。 → your friend already knows they went; the only thing she's asking about is how. Rewrite it with 是…的 so that 坐船 is under the spotlight. Keep 他们 and 那个城市.

他们是坐船去那个城市的。 (also 他们是坐船去的那个城市) — Sheet 4 vs Sheet 5 in one move: the 了 disappears and is replaced by the 是…的 wrapper. 他们是坐船去那个城市了 ✗ — the two cannot share a clause, because 了 announces the event as news while 是…的 assumes it is old news and picks out one detail of it.

S3 · C1 Translate. "My little brother slowly finished that bowl of noodles."
用: 弟弟 · 慢慢 · 地 · 吃完 · 那碗面条 · 了

弟弟慢慢地吃完了那碗面条。 — two sheets in six words. 地 (Sheet C1) clips the manner word onto the front of the verb; 完 (Sheet 3) rides on the back of the same verb to report that the bowl actually got emptied. Nothing else may squeeze between 吃 and 完 — 吃了完 ✗, 吃面条完 ✗.

C2 · S1 Translate. "Two years ago my Chinese wasn't as good as it is now."
用: 两年前 · 我的汉语 · 没有 · 现在 · 好

两年前我的汉语没有现在好。 — Sheet C2 decides the shape of the time word: a single point on the line is number + 前 (两年前), and it sits at the front, before the subject or right after it — never at the end. Sheet 1 decides the comparison: "not as good as" is 没有…好, with the adjective bare. 我的汉语没有现在很好 ✗.

S4 · S2 Answer the question. 你们两个是在哪儿见面的？ — at the library (图书馆). Start with 我们 and keep the 是…的 frame.

我们是在图书馆见面的。 — a 是…的 question is answered with a 是…的 sentence: the meeting is settled fact, and only the where is being handed over. Sheet 2 supplies the other half — 在 is a coverb, so 在图书馆 goes in front of 见面, never after it. Answering 我们在图书馆见面了 would be reporting the meeting itself as news, which the question already knows.

5. Finale Repair a paragraph, then write your own

C1 · C3 · S1 Three errors. This paragraph has exactly three mistakes, and they come from three different sheets. Find them, write the corrected paragraph in the box, then check.
我的邻居阿姨是一位很认真地医生。上个月我发烧了，她给我检查了身体，还告诉我要多喝水。我到今天还没好了，可是我觉得这两天比上个星期很舒服。

我的邻居阿姨是一位很认真的医生。上个月我发烧了，她给我检查了身体，还告诉我要多喝水。我到今天还没好呢，可是我觉得这两天比上个星期舒服得多。

  1. 很认真地医生 → 很认真的医生 (Sheet C1). Decide by what comes next: 医生 is a noun, and only 的 may stand in front of a noun. 地 exists to hook a manner word onto a verb (认真地检查), which would be a different sentence.
  2. 还没好了 → 还没好呢 (Sheet C3). 没 and 了 cannot share a clause — a non-event has no completion to mark. The partner of 还没 is 呢, and it is 呢 that carries "…and that's still the situation", which is exactly what 到今天 sets up.
  3. 比上个星期很舒服 → 比上个星期舒服得多 (Sheet 1). 很 / 非常 / 太 / 真 are banned inside a 比 sentence, because the adjective is already being measured against something. If you want to say how much more comfortable, that amount goes in the slot after the adjective: 舒服得多, 舒服一点儿.

Self-check — one pass per line:

  • Four families or more — and can you point at the sentence carrying each one?
  • Every coverb (在 · 离 · 从…到 · 往 · 对 · 给 · 跟) sits in front of its verb, with nothing stranded at the end of the sentence?
  • No 了 after any 没; every 还没 closed with 呢, every 就要 / 快要 / 已经 closed with 了?
  • In every 比 sentence: no 很/非常/太 in front of the adjective, and any amount (得多 · 一点儿) sitting after it?
  • Every 的/得/地 checked by position, not by feel — 的 + noun, verb + 得 + judgement, manner + 地 + verb?
