# 了 part 1 — completed action

**Verbal 了**: attached directly to the verb. Signals the action was *completed*. It's *not* a past tense — it's about completion, which is why it also works for future hypotheticals: 我吃了饭就去。

S + V + 了 + (quantified O) + (rest)

Bare "V + 了 + O" is usually incomplete unless:

- the object is quantified: 买了三本书,

- there's a second clause: 吃了饭就去,

- or it's followed by a sentence-final 了 (see sheet 12): 我吃饭了.

**Negation**: 没(有), and 了 drops: 我没吃饭. "Haven't yet" = 还没…呢.

**Questions**: …了吗? or …了没有? A-not-A: V了O没有?
