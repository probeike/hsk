# 过 — experience · and the big three: 了 / 过 / 着

**V + 过**: the speaker has the experience of having done this *at some point in life*.

S + V + 过 + (O)

**Negation**: 没 + V + 过 — "have never done it". The 过 stays.

**Questions**: …过吗? or …过没有?

#### The big three at a glance

ParticleCapturesNegation
了the action was completed没(有) V (drop 了)
过the action has happened ≥ once in life没 V 过 (keep 过)
着the action's resulting state is ongoing没 V 着

Think of it as *when along the timeline*: 了 — a specific point done; 过 — "ever been to that point"; 着 — standing at that point right now.
