How to use this

Open one sheet every other day. Each sheet is ~20–30 minutes. Try the exercises before expanding the reminder — the point is active recall, not reading. At the end, mark yourself honestly: solid, shaky, or redo. Anything shaky or redo will bubble back up as "next up" on this page. Sheets you haven't seen in more than two days also go back in the queue.

The reference sheet is a one-page cheat sheet of every grammar pattern. Use it if a sheet's reminder isn't enough, or print it to keep on your desk.
