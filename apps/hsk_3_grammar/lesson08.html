Lesson 08

你去哪儿我就去哪儿

Nǐ qù nǎr wǒ jiù qù nǎr

I'll go wherever you go

What you'll learn

  1. Question-word correlatives — 你去哪儿我就去哪儿, "wherever you go, I go".
  2. 越 A 越 B — "the more A, the more B".
  3. A 跟 B 一样 (+ Adj) — "A is the same as B (in X)".
  4. Mixed practice — drills combining the patterns.

1. 你去哪儿，我就去哪儿 Question-word correlative: "wherever / whatever / whoever"

When a speaker reaches for this: to link two choices. The answer to the first clause controls the second: wherever you go, I go; whatever you eat, I eat.

The English trap

The question word is not asking a real question here. It works like English wherever / whatever / whoever. The second clause repeats the same question word, often with 就 to show "then that is what happens."

Clause 1 + question word , Clause 2 + 就 + same question word

你去哪儿，我就去哪儿。

Nǐ qù nǎr, wǒ jiù qù nǎr.

Wherever you go, I'll go.

你吃什么，我就吃什么。

Nǐ chī shénme, wǒ jiù chī shénme.

Whatever you eat, I'll eat.

谁想去，谁就去。

Shéi xiǎng qù, shéi jiù qù.

Whoever wants to go can go.

How this differs from Lesson 7

Lesson 7 used question words with 都 / 也 to mean "everyone / anything / anywhere". Here the question word is repeated across two clauses. The pair means "the choice in clause 1 is copied in clause 2."

What does this sentence mean?
你买什么，我就买什么。

  • What are you buying?✗ The repeated 什么 makes it a correlative, not a real question.
  • I'll buy whatever you buy.✓ The second clause copies the choice from the first clause.
  • I bought everything.✗ That would need 什么都, from Lesson 7.

2. 越 A 越 B "The more A, the more B"

When a speaker reaches for this: to say that one thing scales with another — eating more makes you fatter, practising more makes you faster. It's a correlation in degree.

The English trap

English says "the more… the more…", using the twice. Chinese uses 越 twice instead. The slots are tight: 越 + verb/adj, 越 + verb/adj. No extra connectors.

越 + A (V/Adj) , 越 + B (V/Adj)

我越说越快。

Wǒ yuè shuō yuè kuài.

The more I speak, the faster I go.

雨越下越大。

Yǔ yuè xià yuè dà.

It's raining harder and harder.

你越学越喜欢汉语。

Nǐ yuè xué yuè xǐhuan Hànyǔ.

The more you study, the more you'll love Chinese.

Related pattern: 越来越 + Adj

越来越 is the one-track version: one quality changes more and more over time. It does not need a second 越 clause.

天气越来越热了。

Tiānqì yuè lái yuè rè le.

The weather is getting hotter and hotter.

越来越 vs 越…越…

Don't confuse these. 越来越 means "more and more over time" (one adjective). 越 A 越 B links two different things — the first change drives the second.

Practice

Build: "The more he eats, the fatter he gets."

他 越 吃 越 胖

Build: "The more you study Chinese, the more interesting it gets."

汉语 越 学 越 有意思

Fill: 他跑步跑得___快。
(He runs faster and faster.)

越来越 — the fixed 越来越 + Adj frame expresses one-dimension increase over time.

"It's raining harder and harder." — which is natural?

  • 雨越下越大。✓ 越 V 越 Adj — the rain falls "more" and "bigger".
  • 雨越来下越大。✗ Don't mix 越来越 and 越…越… frames.
  • 雨很下很大。✗ 很 doesn't scale with itself; you need the 越…越… frame.

What does this sentence mean?
天气越来越热了。

  • The hotter the weather, the better.✗ That would need 越…越… with two different parts.
  • The weather is getting hotter and hotter.✓ 越来越 + Adj marks change over time in one dimension.
  • The weather used to be hot.✗ 越来越 points forward, not backward.

3. A 跟 B 一样 (+ Adj) "A is the same as B"

When a speaker reaches for this: to say two things are equivalent on some measure, or simply the same.

The English trap

English: "A is as tall as B" — two ases. Chinese uses the sandwich A 跟 B 一样 (高) — B sits inside the comparison frame. Word order matters.

A + 跟 + B + 一样 + (Adj)

我跟他一样高。

Wǒ gēn tā yíyàng gāo.

I'm as tall as him.

这本书跟那本书一样。

Zhè běn shū gēn nà běn shū yíyàng.

This book is the same as that one.

Negative: A 跟 B 不一样 ("A is not the same as B"). Notice 不 goes before 一样, not before the adjective.

中国菜跟美国菜不一样。

Zhōngguócài gēn Měiguócài bù yíyàng.

Chinese food is not the same as American food.

Practice

Build: "His car is the same as my car."

他的车 跟 我的车 一样

"A is as big as B" — pick the right template:

  • A 跟 B 一样大✓ Adj comes last: A 跟 B 一样 + Adj.
  • A 大跟 B 一样✗ Adj must be final, not mid-frame.
  • A 一样跟 B 大✗ 跟 precedes B, then 一样.

Build: "My younger sister is as tall as me."

我妹妹 跟 我 一样 高

Fill: 中国菜跟美国菜___。
(Chinese food is not the same as American food.)

不一样 — 不 attaches to 一样, not to a following adjective.

What does this sentence mean?
这本书跟那本书一样有意思。

  • This book is more interesting than that one.✗ That would use 比, not 跟…一样.
  • This book is as interesting as that one.✓ A 跟 B 一样 + Adj — equal on that measure.
  • This book and that book are both boring.✗ 一样 means "the same", and 有意思 is positive.

Which sentence correctly negates the comparison?

  • 我的手机跟他的不一样。✓ 不 goes before 一样.
  • 我的手机不跟他的一样。✗ 不 does not attach to 跟.
  • 我的手机跟他的一样不。✗ 不 never sits sentence-final like this.

Mixed practice All three patterns together

Fill: 他的汉语___好。
(His Chinese is getting better and better.)

越来越好 — one-dimension growth over time.

Build: "I like Chinese food just as much as he does."

我 跟 他 一样 喜欢 中国菜

"The older he gets, the healthier he is." — pick the natural Chinese:

  • 他越老越健康。✓ 越 + V/Adj, 越 + V/Adj links two scaling dimensions.
  • 他越来越老越健康。✗ Don't splice 越来越 into the 越…越… frame.
  • 他跟老一样健康。✗ 跟…一样 compares two entities, not two states of one entity.

What does this sentence mean?
这个洗手间跟那个一样安静。

  • This bathroom is quieter than that one.✗ 跟…一样 means "the same as", not "more than".
  • This bathroom is as quiet as that one.✓ A 跟 B 一样 + Adj — equal on that measure.
  • This bathroom is getting quieter and quieter.✗ That would use 越来越安静.
