大多数三音节词属于“中轻重”格式，即第一个音节为中音，音长次
长;第二个音节为轻音，音长最短; 第三个音节为重音，音长最长。例如:
Most trisyllabic words fall into this type of structure, in which the first syllable is
medium-stressed and medium in length, the second syllable is light and short, and the third
syllable is stressed and long. For example:
