HSK 2 Review · Sheet 03 · complements drill

题太多，我没做完。

Tí tài duō, wǒ méi zuòwán.

Too many questions — I didn't finish.

What this sheet drills

This is a drill pack, not a lesson. You already have the theory: 得 — the verb's report card, 地 — the manner marker and the which-de decision tree are all taught in Custom 1. Nothing about them is re-explained here — you said you just need reps, so this sheet is reps.

The one new thing is section 1: result complements (做完, 听懂, 买到, 看见) — a second word fused onto the verb that says how the action turned out. After that it's drills: 得-state (section 2), telling result and state apart (section 3), ten rapid-fire 的/得/地 blanks (section 4), and a mixed cloze (section 5).

1. Result complements V + 完 / 懂 / 到 / 见 / 错 / 好 / 会 — how the action turned out

When a speaker reaches for this: the verb alone doesn't say whether the action landed. 我看 = I looked; did I actually see anything? 我听 = I listened; did I get it? Chinese answers that by fusing a second word — a verb or an adjective — onto the back of the first: 看见, 听懂, 做完. The pair then behaves as one verb.

The English trap

English handles this three different ways, and none of them looks like a complement. Sometimes it's a whole extra verb — "finish doing it", "manage to buy it". Sometimes it's an adverb — "you said it wrong". And very often it's nothing at all: "Did you hear me?" already means "did the hearing succeed", so you translate it as 你听了吗 and it comes out wrong — Chinese needs 你听见了吗？ (perceive) or 你听懂了吗？ (understand). If the English implies an outcome, Chinese wants a character for it.

Lǎoshī shuō de huà wǒ méi tīngdǒng.

teacher / say / DE / words / I / not / listen-UNDERSTAND

I didn't catch what the teacher said.

Wǒ zài Niǔyuē méi mǎidào nà běn shū.

I / at / New York / not / buy-GET / that / CL / book

I couldn't get hold of that book in New York.

对不起，我说错了。

Duìbuqǐ, wǒ shuōcuò le.

sorry, / I / say-WRONG / LE

Sorry — I said that wrong.

Negating and questioning the cluster

Because V + result is one unit, you never split it. 没 goes in front of the whole thing, and 了 drops out after 没 — exactly like any other 没 sentence.

JobSentenceNote
Statement我做完作业了。The homework got finished.
Negative我没做完作业。没 in front of 做完 — and 了 is gone.
Question你做完作业了吗？Or 你做完作业了没有？

Common pitfall

我不做完作业 — 不 says "I'm not willing to / I don't habitually", which is never what you mean about a finished job. A result that failed to happen is always 没. And 我没做完了作业 keeps a 了 that must disappear.

Practice

Fill the result word: 老师说的话，你听 ___ 了吗？
(Did you understand what the teacher said?)

懂 — 听懂 = the listening succeeded in producing understanding. (听见 would only mean the sound reached you.)

Fill the result word: 我想买那本书，可是没买 ___ 。
(I wanted that book, but I couldn't get one.)

到 — 买到 = the buying actually landed the object. English hides this inside "manage to".

Fill the result word: 作业我已经做 ___ 了。
(I've already finished the homework.)

完 — 做完 = all of it is done. (做好 is also accepted here; it leans on "done properly / ready" rather than "got to the end".)

Fill the result word: 对不起，我说 ___ 了。
(Sorry, I said it wrong.)

错 — 说错 = the speaking came out wrong. English uses a separate adverb; Chinese fuses it to the verb.

"I didn't finish the homework." Which is right?

  • 我没做完作业。✓ 没 negates the whole cluster 做完, and 了 drops after 没.
  • 我不做完作业。✗ 不 means unwilling / not habitually. A result that didn't happen always takes 没.
  • 我没做完了作业。✗ Right verb, but 了 can't survive next to 没 — delete it.

Build: "Have you seen my phone?" (English says just "seen" — Chinese needs the perceive-result.)

你 看见 我的 手机 了吗

2. 得-state drills No new theory — just reps

Reminder in one line: V + 得 + (很) Adj grades how the action went, and if the verb already carries an object the verb repeats (跳舞跳得很好). Full explanation: Custom 1 · point 3. Now drill it.

Build: "She dances very well."

她 跳舞 跳 得 很好

Fix the sentence: 他说汉语得很好。

他说汉语说得很好。 — 汉语 pushed 得 off its verb, so 说 is repeated right before 得. (Or topicalise the object: 他汉语说得很好。)

Fill: 你昨天睡 ___ 怎么样？
(How did you sleep yesterday?)

得 — a verb (睡) in front, a verdict (怎么样) after.

Fill the missing verb: 小丽跳舞 ___ 得很认真。
(Xiao Li dances very earnestly.)

跳 — 舞 is in the way, so 跳 comes back for 得 to hold on to.

"Xiao Li doesn't dance well." Which is right?

  • 小丽跳舞跳得不好。✓ Negate the verdict, not the verb: 得不好.
  • 小丽不跳舞跳得好。✗ 不 in front of 跳舞 says she doesn't dance at all — a different claim.
  • 小丽跳舞没跳得好。✗ 没 negates a result cluster (没跳完), never a 得-verdict.

Translate: "He runs really fast."

他跑得真快。 (or 他跑得很快。) — 跑 has no object, so no verb repeat is needed; the adjective still wants 真 / 很 in front of it.

Translate: "I didn't sleep well last night."

我昨天晚上睡得不好。 — the 不 goes inside the verdict: 得不好. Never 我不睡得好.

3. Result or state? 你写完了吗 (did you FINISH) vs 你写得怎么样 (how WELL)

Both hang something off the back of the verb, and they answer completely different questions. 写完 is a yes/no fact: is it done? 写得很好 is a grade: how did it turn out? Pick by asking which question the English is really asking.

Result — did it land?
你写完了吗？
"Have you finished writing?" Answer: 写完了 / 还没写完.
State — how did it go?
你写得怎么样？
"How well did you write it?" Answer: 写得很好 / 写得不太好.

You want to ask: "Have you finished the homework?"

  • 作业你做完了吗？✓ 完 asks about completion — the yes/no fact.
  • 作业你做得怎么样？✗ That asks how well it went, not whether it's done.
  • 作业你做得很好吗？✗ Still a quality question ("did you do it well?"), not a completion one.

You want to ask: "How well does Xiao Li dance?"

  • 小丽跳舞跳得怎么样？✓ 得 + 怎么样 asks for a grade on the dancing.
  • 小丽跳完舞了吗？✗ That asks whether she has finished dancing.
  • 小丽会跳舞吗？✗ That asks whether she can dance at all, not how well.

You want to say: "I listened, but I didn't understand it."

  • 我听了，可是没听懂。✓ The outcome (understanding) failed to happen → result complement 懂, negated with 没.
  • 我听了，可是听得不好。✗ 得不好 grades the quality of your listening — it doesn't say you missed the meaning.
  • 我听了，可是不听懂。✗ 不 can't cancel a result; use 没听懂.

4. 的 / 得 / 地 rapid-fire Ten blanks, one character each — don't think, just place it

One character per blank. If you stall, the three-question tree is at Custom 1 · point 5 — but try to answer first and check after.

1. 这是我 ___ 房子。 (This is my house.)

的 — a noun (房子) comes next.

2. 他高兴 ___ 说：“我要当老板了！” (He said happily: "I'm going to be my own boss!")

地 — a verb (说) comes next, and 高兴 is the mood he says it in.

3. 你跳 ___ 真好！ (You dance really well!)

得 — a verb (跳) sits in front, a verdict (真好) after.

4. 妈妈做 ___ 菜很好吃。 (The food Mum makes is delicious.)

的 — 妈妈做 is a whole clause describing the noun 菜: "the food that Mum makes". Noun next → 的.

5. 他跑 ___ 很快。 (He runs fast.)

得 — verb in front, grade after.

6. 她慢慢 ___ 走出教室。 (She walked slowly out of the classroom.)

地 — before the verb 走, describing how she does it.

7. 老师说 ___ 话我都记得。 (I remember everything the teacher said.)

的 — the clause 老师说 describes the noun 话.

8. 你写 ___ 太慢了。 (You write too slowly.)

得 — 太慢了 is the verdict on the writing.

9. 他认真 ___ 练习舞蹈。 (He practises dance conscientiously.)

地 — the verb 练习 comes next; 认真 is how he goes into it.

10. 我昨天买 ___ 那本书很有意思。 (The book I bought yesterday is really interesting.)

的 — 我昨天买 describes 那本书. Noun next → 的.

5. Finale Result complements and all three de in one paragraph

选词填空。 Click a word, then a blank. One word is extra — and two blanks want the same character.

我朋友小丽学汉语学很认真。昨天作业她很快就做了，老师说的话她都听了。

Read it back: what does the paragraph above say?

我的朋友小丽学汉语学得很认真。昨天的作业她很快就做完了，老师说的话她都听懂了。

辞职以后，我一定要当舞蹈老师。 — Cízhí yǐhòu, wǒ yídìng yào dāng wǔdǎo lǎoshī. No 的 in 舞蹈老师: it's a job label, one unit.
