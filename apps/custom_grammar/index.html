Personalized Grammar Lessons & Review Sheets

Four lessons built from your own tutoring sentences — 的/得/地, time ordering, action status, and "not even one" negation — plus six drill-first HSK 2 review sheets weighted by your self-diagnosed weak spots. Same pedagogy throughout: function first, English traps named, image schemas, minimal pairs.

How these differ from the HSK 3 lessons

Each lesson pair grew out of sentences you actually produced in class (两个星期之后我要辞职, 跳舞跳得很好, 前两个月我搬家了…). The vocabulary draws on the full HSK 1–3 wordlists, with your personal words (辞职, 老板, 舞蹈, 工程师, 压力…) declared in each lesson's own "New vocabulary" table. Exercises deliberately use different sentences and vocabulary from the taught examples — varied practice is what builds the grammar, not the sentence (see PEDAGOGY.md for the research and authoring rules). Production-heavy mix throughout: transformations, translations, Q→A, error correction, and free writing with self-check lists — not just recognition drills.

All pages work offline. AI grading: free-writing exercises have a "Grade with AI" button — click AI ⚙ (bottom-right of any page) once and paste your Anthropic API key to enable it (stored only in this browser). Companion course: HSK 3 Grammar — 20 interactive lessons.

HSK 2 review sheets

Six drill-first practice sheets covering the HSK 2 grammar you flagged as shaky. They deliberately don't re-teach what Custom 01–03 already cover — where theory exists (得, 地, 是…的, status frames), the sheets link to it and go straight to reps. Suggested order: heaviest weak spots first.

Sheet 01
比 part 2
The degree slot lives after the adjective — 大三岁/贵得多, 比+得, 没有 vs 不比, 跟…一样
大三岁 好得多 跳得比我快 跟…一样
Sheet 02
在·离·从·往·对
Coverbs go before the verb — the placement rule, 在's two positions, static 离, 从…到, direction 往, attitude 对
在家吃饭 离…远 从…到 往前走 对我很好
Sheet 03

Sheet 04

The spotlight structure in 14 examples — when / where / how / who, and when to use 了 instead

Sheet 05

The three markers — done, still-true, ever — the piece Custom 03's status frames leave out
