Sheet 14 · Tier 2

着 — continuing state

~25 min
Reference
Quick reminder

V + 着 freezes the action into its resulting state. It describes the way something currently is, not the moment of doing.

门开着呢。 The door is open.
他穿着一件红色的衣服。 He's wearing red clothes.

着 vs 在…呢:

  • 他在开门呢。 — he's in the act of opening the door.
  • 门开着。 — the door is (standing) open.

V₁着 V₂ — doing V₂ while V₁-ing: the V₁着 describes the manner.

他笑着说。 He said, smiling.
我喜欢站着学习。 I like to study standing up.

Negation: 没(有) + V + 着: 门没开着。

Typical 着-compatible verbs describe states: 开、关、穿、戴、拿、坐、站、躺、放、写.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: The door is open.
  2. Translate: He's wearing a red shirt.
  3. Translate: He said, laughing.
  4. Translate: I like studying standing up.

Structured practice

  1. 着 or 在…呢 (or both)?
    (a) 她手里 ____ 一本书。 (b) 妈妈 ____ 做饭 ____。 (c) 墙上 ____ 一张照片。 (d) 弟弟 ____ 看电视 ____。
  2. Fix: 门在开呢。 (intended: the door is already open, not being opened)
  3. Translate: He's standing at the door, waiting for you. (V₁着 V₂)
  4. Translate: The window isn't open.
  5. Translate: I can't read lying down. (躺着 看书)
  6. Translate: On the desk is a book and a cup of tea. (existential 有 + 着 combo)
  7. Fix: 他坐一边吃饭。 (intended: "He eats sitting down.")
  8. Translate: Mum is cooking; she's wearing an apron. (combines progressive and 着)
  9. Describe in ≥3 sentences, using 着 and/or 在…呢: imagine a photograph of your family at dinner.
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — Is the light on?
    — Yes, it's on. Why?
    — Nothing. I thought the kids were sleeping.

Warm-up

  2. 他穿着一件红色的衣服。
  3. 他笑着说。
  4. 我喜欢站着学习。

Structured

  1. (a) 拿着 (b) 在…呢 or 正在…呢 (c) 挂/贴着 (d) 在…呢
  2. 门开着呢。
  3. 他站在门口等你呢。
  4. 窗户没开着。
  5. 我躺着看不了书。 (看不了 uses the potential complement, beyond HSK 2 but common.)
  6. 桌子上放着一本书和一杯茶。
  7. 他坐着吃饭。
  8. 妈妈在做饭呢，穿着围裙。
  9. e.g. 爸爸坐在桌子前边。妈妈拿着一个盘子。妹妹笑着看着我。
  10. 灯开着吗? — 开着呢。怎么了? — 没什么。我以为孩子们在睡觉呢。
