What you'll learn

  1. 又 vs 再 — two "agains" split by past vs. future.
  2. Question words used as generics — 谁都 / 什么都 / 哪儿都 meaning "everyone / anything / anywhere".

Title note: this recycles Lesson 6

我跟她都认识五年了 is not a new duration pattern. It points back to Lesson 6: a duration reaches up to now, so the sentence ends with 了. With a continuing relationship like 认识 ("know"), Chinese often says 认识五年了: "have known for five years and still know each other."

1. 又 vs 再 "Again" — but which again?

When a speaker reaches for 又: something already happened a second time (past-tense again). 再: something will (or should) happen again (future "again"). This matches English did it again vs will do it again, but English doesn't require two different words.

The English trap

One English word, two Chinese words. Rule of thumb: if the action already took place → 又. If it hasn't happened yet (request, plan, offer) → 再. Getting this backwards is one of the most frequent HSK 3 errors.

又 — already repeated (past)
他昨天又去了。
He went again yesterday (it's already happened).
再 — will repeat (future)
明天我再去。
I'll go again tomorrow (hasn't happened yet).

你再说一次。

Nǐ zài shuō yí cì.

Say it one more time. (Future request — 再.)

这个电影我又看了一次。

Zhège diànyǐng wǒ yòu kàn le yí cì.

I watched this movie one more time. (Already done — 又.)

2. Question words as "every/any" 谁都 · 什么都 · 哪儿都

When a speaker reaches for this: to sweep over all options or deny any. "Everyone likes her." "She doesn't eat anything." Chinese reuses the question words (谁, 什么, 哪儿) as generic placeholders, with 都 / 也 doing the "every/any" work.

The English trap

English has distinct words: everyone, anything, anywhere. Chinese overloads the question words for both asking and for "any/every". The giveaway: if 都 or 也 follows, it's the universal reading. The question words are no longer questions.

First ask: is it really a question?

A real question word asks for missing information. A question word followed by 都 or 也 sweeps across all possible answers.

Real question Generic sweep Meaning
谁喜欢音乐？ 谁都喜欢音乐。 Who likes music? → Everyone likes music.
你想吃什么？ 我什么都不想吃。 What do you want to eat? → I don't want to eat anything.
你去哪儿？ 我哪儿也不去。 Where are you going? → I'm not going anywhere.
谁 / 什么 / 哪儿 + 都 / 也 + V

谁都喜欢她。

Shéi dōu xǐhuan tā.

who / all / like / her

Everyone likes her.

她什么都不吃。

Tā shénme dōu bù chī.

she / what / all / not / eat

She doesn't eat anything.

我哪儿也不想去。

Wǒ nǎr yě bù xiǎng qù.

I don't want to go anywhere.
