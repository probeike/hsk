注释       Tl BB...Bho  The Structure “只要.……-，就……”
Notes     “只要……就……”连接的是一个条 hen. “只要”后边是必要条件，
“就”后边是其结果。主语可以放在“只要”前，也可以放在“只要”后。
如果有第二个主语，要放在“就”的前边。  例如，
“只要.和…，，就.……”is used to connect the two clauses of a conditional complex
sentence，with“只要”introducing a necessary condition and“就”followed by the

consequent result. The subject can be put before or after“只要” If there is a second subject
in the sentence, it is put right before “就” For example:

(1) 只要我有时间，就一定跟你去旅游。

见怪不怪 Jion guai bu gudi

One has become inured to the unusual

“见怪不怪”，这里第一个“怪”是名词，意思是奇怪的

思是奇怪的事物看得多了，习惯了

，也就不觉得奇怪了。

The first “PX” in the saying“见怪不怪”is a noun meaning
something strange, and the second “P&” is a verb meaning feeling

strange. The saying means that one has seen too many unusual

things to be surprised by any.
