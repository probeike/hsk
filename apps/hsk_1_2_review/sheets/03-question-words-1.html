Sheet 03 · Tier 1

Question words I: 什么 / 谁 / 哪 / 哪儿

~25 min
Reference
Quick reminder

Chinese keeps the question word in the slot it's asking about. You don't move it to the front the way English does. No 吗 needed — the question word is the question marker.

  • 什么 (shénme) — what. 什么 + noun = "what / which ___".
  • 谁 (shéi / shuí) — who. Same slot as any noun.
  • 哪 (nǎ) — which. Must take a measure word: 哪 + MW + N. For countries and books the measure is often elided: 哪国人 / 哪本.
  • 哪儿 (nǎr) — where. Often paired with 在: 在哪儿? = "where at?"

Echo principle: an answer typically slots the replacement right back into the same position as the question word.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: What's your name?
  2. Translate: Who is she?
  3. Translate: Which country are you from?
  4. Translate: Where does your son work?

Structured practice

  1. Fill in the right question word:
    (a) 这是 ? (b) 她叫 名字? (c) 你是 国人? (d) 你住在 ? (e) 你喜欢 运动?
  2. Turn the statement into a question about the underlined part. Example: 他是我同学。 → 他是谁?
    (a) 我买了一本书。   (b) 她是我姐姐。   (c) 他在医院工作。   (d) 我喜欢那个手机。
  3. Fix: 什么你叫?
  4. Fix: 你是哪人?
  5. Answer in a full sentence: 你的汉语老师叫什么名字?
  6. Translate: Which book do you want?
  7. Translate: Whose phone is that? (combines 谁 + possessive 的)
  8. Translate: Where are you going?
  9. Mini-dialogue translate:
    — What's that?
    — That's my Chinese teacher's book.
    — Which teacher?
    — Teacher Li.

Warm-up

  1. 你叫什么名字? Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
  2. 她是谁? Tā shì shéi?
  3. 你是哪国人? Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?
  4. 你儿子在哪儿工作? Nǐ érzi zài nǎr gōngzuò?

Structured

  1. (a) 什么 (b) 什么 (c) 哪 (d) 哪儿 (e) 什么
  2. (a) 你买了什么? / 你买了几本书? (b) 她是谁? (c) 他在哪儿工作? (d) 你喜欢哪个手机?
  3. 你叫什么? — question word stays in place.
  4. 你是哪国人? — 哪 needs a MW/N; "country-person" takes 国.
  5. e.g. 她叫李月。
  6. 你要哪本书? Nǐ yào nǎ běn shū?
  7. 那是谁的手机? Nà shì shéi de shǒujī?
  8. 你去哪儿? Nǐ qù nǎr?
  9. 那是什么? — 那是我汉语老师的书。— 哪个老师? — 李老师。
