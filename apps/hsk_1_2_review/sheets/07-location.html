Sheet 07 · Tier 1

Location: 在 (verb & preposition) · 有 (existential)

~25 min
Reference
Quick reminder

在 as a verb: tells you where something already-known is.

S + 在 + Place

在 as a preposition: locates an action.

S + 在 + Place + V (+ O)

Remember: the 在 Place goes before the verb. English: "I work at a hospital." Chinese: 我在医院工作。

Existential 有: given a place, tells you what's there.

Place + 有 + Thing · negative: 没有

在 vs 是 vs 有 triangle:

  • Subject is a known thing, you want to locate it → 在.
  • Subject is a place, you want to describe what's there → 有.
  • Subject is a place, you want to identify a specific thing there → 是.

Localisers stick on the end of a noun: 桌子上 / 床下 / 房间里 / 学校旁边 / 银行前面 / 医院后面.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: My dad is in Beijing.
  2. Translate: She works at a hospital.
  3. Translate: There's a book on the desk.
  4. Translate: In front of the bank is our company.

Structured practice

  1. Fill in 在, 有, or 是:
    (a) 我的手机 桌子上。 (b) 桌子上 一个手机。 (c) 桌子上的那个 我的手机。 (d) 他 哪儿工作?
  2. Fix: 她工作在医院。
  3. Fix: 我家有在北京。
  4. Negate: 房间里有很多人。
  5. Turn into a question asking where: 我哥哥在上海学习。
  6. Translate: Our school is next to the hospital.
  7. Translate: What's in your bag?
  8. Translate: The teacher isn't in the classroom — she's in the office.
  9. Describe the scene in ≥3 sentences using 在/有/是: a classroom with 20 students, one teacher, and a book on the desk.
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — Where's your younger sister?
    — She's at home. Mum and Dad are home too.
    — What's your sister doing? (we'll revisit 在…呢 in sheet 13, but have a go.)

Warm-up

  1. 我爸爸在北京。 Wǒ bàba zài Běijīng.
  2. 她在医院工作。 Tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.
  3. 桌子上有一本书。 Zhuōzi shàng yǒu yì běn shū.
  4. 银行前面是我们公司。 Yínháng qiánmian shì wǒmen gōngsī.

Structured

  1. (a) 在 (b) 有 (c) 是 (d) 在
  2. 她在医院工作。
  3. 我家在北京。 — no 有; home is a known thing being located.
  4. 房间里没有很多人。 (or 没有几个人 — "only a few" is more idiomatic.)
  5. 你哥哥在哪儿学习?
  6. 我们学校在医院旁边。
  7. 你的包里有什么?
  8. 老师不在教室，(她)在办公室。
  9. e.g. 教室里有二十个学生和一个老师。老师的桌子上有一本书。我的老师是王老师。
  10. 你妹妹在哪儿? — (她)在家。爸爸妈妈也在家。— 你妹妹在做什么?
