Sheet 16 · Tier 2

是…的 focus structure

~25 min
Reference
Quick reminder

是…的 takes an already-known completed action and highlights one detail about it: the when / where / how / who. Both speakers know the action happened; what's being asked or told is which one.

S + 是 + [time/place/manner/agent] + V + 的

我是坐飞机来的。 I came by plane. (We know I came — the how is the news.)
她是昨天到北京的。 She arrived in Beijing yesterday.
这个工作是他帮我介绍的。 He introduced this job to me. (The doer is the news.)

Object placement: the object often hops outside the 的 (especially if it's a name/place): 我是去年去的北京 / 我是去年去北京的 — both fine.

Negation: 不是…的. Even in negative, you keep both 是 and 的.

No 了 allowed. The 是…的 frame already implies completion; adding 了 is an error.

是 can drop in the affirmative (but not the negative): 我去年去的北京. In the negative it must stay.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: I came by plane.
  2. Translate: She arrived in Beijing yesterday.
  3. Translate: He's the one who introduced this job to me.
  4. Translate: I didn't come by plane; I came by train.

Structured practice

  1. Convert to a 是…的 question asking the underlined detail:
    (a) 她昨天来的北京。 (b) 我坐地铁来的公司。 (c) 他在网上买的书。 (d) 这个菜妈妈做的。
  2. Fix: 我是昨天去了的公司。
  3. Fix: 我不昨天来的。
  4. Translate: — How did you get here? — By bike.
  5. Translate: — When did you start learning Chinese? — I started three years ago.
  6. Translate: — Who bought this? — My older sister did.
  7. 是…的 vs 了: which one?
    (a) Someone asks "when did you come?" — you answer: 我 ____ 昨天 ____ 来 ____。 (b) You just finished eating: 我吃 ____ 三碗饭。
  8. Translate: This book wasn't bought on the internet — it was a gift from a friend.
  9. Error-spot: 我是明天去北京的。 — why doesn't this work?
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — Where did you learn Chinese?
    — I learned it in Beijing. I studied at a language school.
    — When did you go?
    — I went in 2022.

Warm-up

  1. 我是坐飞机来的。
  2. 她是昨天到北京的。
  3. 这个工作是他帮我介绍的。
  4. 我不是坐飞机来的，我是坐火车来的。

Structured

  1. (a) 她是什么时候来的北京? (b) 你是怎么来公司的? (c) 他是在哪儿买的书? (d) 这个菜是谁做的?
  2. 我是昨天去公司的。 — no 了 inside 是…的.
  3. 我不是昨天来的。 — negative always keeps 是.
  4. 你是怎么来的? — 我是骑自行车来的。
  5. 你是什么时候开始学汉语的? — 我是三年前开始学的。
  6. 这是谁买的? — 我姐姐买的。 (affirmative can drop 是)
  7. (a) 是…的 — 我是昨天来的。 (b) 了 — 我吃了三碗饭。 — 是…的 is for known-completed events whose detail is being focused; 了 is for "newly completed".
  8. 这本书不是在网上买的，是朋友送的。
  9. 是…的 is only for completed actions. 明天 is future — contradictory.
  10. 你是在哪儿学的汉语? — 我是在北京学的，在一个语言学校学的。— 你是什么时候去的? — 我是2022年去的。

  3. Translate: I still want to buy another cup of coffee. (还 + 再) 10
