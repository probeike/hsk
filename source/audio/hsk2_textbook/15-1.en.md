# HSK 2 · Lesson 15 · Dialogue 1 — At a friend's home

A: Today is December 20th — the New Year is almost here.
B: What are you planning to do for the New Year?
A: I'm thinking of taking a trip to Beijing. Beijing is great — I've been once before.
B: Have you bought your ticket?
A: Not yet. Tomorrow I'll go to the train station to buy it.
