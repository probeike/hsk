节，第四个音节为重读音节。例如:
A quadrisyallbic word that has a neutral-tone syllable is usually an adjective, in which
the second syllable is in the neutral tone and the fourth syllable is stressed. For example:
dudduosudsud         hudnghuangyouyou         momojijr           prlipala
