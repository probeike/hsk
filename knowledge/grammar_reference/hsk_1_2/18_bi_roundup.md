# 比 comparison · round-up & mixed cumulative

#### 比 comparison

A + 比 + B + Adj (+ degree)

"A is more Adj than B." The degree (how much more) comes *after* the adjective:

- 一点儿 / 一些 — a little more

- 得多 / 多了 — a lot more

- a concrete amount: 三岁 / 五块钱

他比我大三岁。 He's three years older than me.

今天比昨天冷一点儿。 Today is a bit colder than yesterday.

他的汉语比我好得多。 His Chinese is a lot better than mine.

**Never** use 很 / 非常 / 太 / 真 inside a 比 sentence — the degree slot goes after the adjective.

**Negation**: 没有 replaces 比. A 没有 B + Adj = "A is not as Adj as B".

#### Round-up: sentence types

- **Pivotal sentences** with 让 / 叫 / 请: 让我想想再告诉你。

- **Imperatives: "don't"** — 别 / 不要 + V + 了. The 了 signals "stop doing the thing you're doing": 别找了。

- **对 preposition** — 对 N + V/Adj = "toward/to N": 他对我很好。

- **Confirmation**: 是不是 / ……好吗?
