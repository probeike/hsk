What you'll learn

  1. 又 A 又 B — "both A and B" for listing qualities.
  2. V₁着 (O) + V₂ (O) — doing V₂ while V₁.
  3. 了 for change — sentence-final 了 signalling "things are different now".
  4. 越来越 + Adj — "more and more…".

1. 又 … 又 … Listing two qualities that coexist

When a speaker reaches for this: to say something or someone has two qualities at the same time — "both X and Y". Think English both tall and pretty, cheap and tasty.

The English trap

English uses and once: "tall and pretty". Chinese brackets each adjective with 又: 又高又漂亮. Forgetting the second 又 is the single most common mistake.

又 + Adj₁ + 又 + Adj₂

这个西瓜又大又红。

Zhège xīguā yòu dà yòu hóng.

this / watermelon / both / big / and / red

This watermelon is both big and red.

外边又黑又冷。

Wàibian yòu hēi yòu lěng.

It's dark and cold outside.

Practice

Build: "The things at this supermarket are both cheap and good."

这家超市的 东西 又 便宜 又 好

Fill the blank: 她 ___ ，大家都喜欢她。
(She is both clever and warm / hard-working — everyone likes her.)

e.g. 又聪明又热情 — bracket each adjective with 又.

Which sentence is correct?

  • 这个蛋糕又便宜好吃。✗ Second 又 is missing — you need 又 before each adjective.
  • 这个蛋糕又便宜又好吃。✓ 又 Adj₁ 又 Adj₂ — cheap and tasty.
  • 这个蛋糕便宜又好吃。✗ First 又 is missing — the pattern needs 又 in front of both adjectives.

Build: "That waitress is both young and pretty."

那个 服务员 又 年轻 又 漂亮

2. V₁着 (O) + V₂ (O) Doing V₂ while V₁ — the accompanying action

Bridge from Lesson 3

Lesson 3 used Place + V着 + NP to freeze a scene: 桌子上放着一本书. Lesson 4 keeps the same held-state idea, but now the V着 phrase becomes the background for another action: 坐着看书 = sitting is held while reading happens. Same 着, new sentence job.

When a speaker reaches for this: to describe two actions going on at the same time, where V₁ is the ongoing state/posture and V₂ is the main activity. English usually uses -ing or while: "standing while chatting", "smiling as she spoke".

The English trap

English learners instinctively try to string verbs with "and" ("she smiled and talked"), but that loses the simultaneity. Chinese uses 着 on V₁ to say "V₁ is the backdrop, V₂ is what she's actually doing." Order: backdrop first (with 着), foreground activity second.

Why 着? (image schema)

着 freezes V₁ into a held state/picture — a still frame the main action plays out against. 笑着说话 = "smiling-as-backdrop, speaking-as-action".

V₁ + 着 + (O₁) + V₂ + (O₂)

她总是笑着跟客人说话。

Tā zǒngshì xiàozhe gēn kèrén shuōhuà.

she / always / smile-着 / with customers / speak

She always smiles while talking to customers.

弟弟吃着苹果写作业。

Dìdi chīzhe píngguǒ xiě zuòyè.

younger-brother / eat-着 / apple / write / homework

My little brother is doing his homework while eating an apple.

Practice

Build: "Many people are standing at the door holding flowers."

很多人 拿 着 鲜花 站 在 门口

Complete: 我喜欢 ___ 。
(I like to listen to music while doing homework.)

e.g. 听着歌写作业 / 听着音乐写作业.

Which sentence means "They watch TV while eating cake"?

  • 他们吃着蛋糕看电视。✓ V₁着 (吃着蛋糕) = backdrop; V₂ (看电视) = main action.
  • 他们吃蛋糕看着电视。✗ 着 is on the wrong verb — the foreground action (看电视) shouldn't take 着 here.
  • 他们看电视吃着蛋糕。✗ The 着-verb (backdrop) must come first, not after the main verb.

What does this sentence mean?
老师站着上课。

  • The teacher stops to teach class.✗ 站 means "stand", not "stop".
  • The teacher teaches class while standing.✓ 站着 = standing (backdrop), 上课 = teaching (main action).
  • The teacher stands up and leaves class.✗ No "leave" here — 上课 is "to teach / have class".

Build: "He is sitting and reading a book."

他 坐 着 看 书 呢

3. Sentence-final 了 — change "Things are different now"

When a speaker reaches for this: to report a change from how things were. "I'm hungry now (wasn't before)." "It's not cold anymore." The 了 sits at the end of the sentence (different from the aspect 了 attached directly to a verb).

The English trap

English uses a new adverb ("now", "anymore", "these days") to signal change. Chinese uses a tiny particle — 了 — at the very end. Miss it and you miss the "but before, it wasn't so" layer of meaning.

Sentence-final 了 = "boundary crossed into a new state". The reader should infer: "so it wasn't this way before."

上个月很冷，现在天气不那么冷了。

Shàng ge yuè hěn lěng, xiànzài tiānqì bú nàme lěng le.

Last month was cold — it's not that cold anymore.

我现在喜欢这里了。

Wǒ xiànzài xǐhuan zhèlǐ le.

I / now / like / here / LE

I like it here now (I didn't use to).

Keep the 了 map visible

Open the three 了s appendix before you practise this section. Here, the 了 is sentence-final change-of-state 了, not verb-了 for completed action and not the V了...就 sequence from Lesson 2.

Practice

What does 我现在没钱了 actually imply?

  • I've never had money.✗ 了 implies a change of state — so before was different.
  • I used to have money; now I don't.✓ The change meaning is in the sentence-final 了.
  • I sometimes have no money.✗ Not the meaning — 了 locates a specific shift.

Fill the blank: 我有点儿饿 ___ ，我们去吃饭吧。
(I'm a bit hungry now — let's go eat.)

了 — sentence-final 了 marks "wasn't hungry before, am now".

What change does this sentence signal?
天气暖和了。

  • The weather is always warm.✗ 了 specifically signals a change, not a constant state.
  • The weather has become warm (it wasn't before).✓ Sentence-final 了 = crossed a boundary into a new state.
  • The weather will be warm.✗ 了 marks a change that has already happened, not a prediction.

Build: "I like it here now (I didn't before)."

我 现在 喜欢 这里 了

4. 越来越 + Adj "More and more…" — trending change over time

When a speaker reaches for this: to note a trend — something's increasing in degree as time passes. "Colder and colder." "More and more people."

Common pitfall

Don't pair 越来越 with another degree adverb — *越来越很冷 (✗) is wrong. 越来越 already carries the "growing" intensity; stacking 很 / 非常 / 特别 is redundant and ungrammatical.

越来越 + Adj / mental verb

我认识的汉字越来越多。

Wǒ rènshi de Hànzì yuè lái yuè duō.

I know more and more characters.

你越来越漂亮。

Nǐ yuè lái yuè piàoliang.

You get prettier and prettier.

Practice

Which is grammatical?

  • 最近天气越来越热。✓ 越来越 + bare adjective.
  • 最近天气越来越很热。✗ Don't add 很/非常 — 越来越 already intensifies.
  • 最近天气热越来越。✗ 越来越 must precede the adjective, not follow it.

Fill: 我 ___ 运动。
(I like exercising more and more.)

越来越喜欢 — mental verbs work the same as adjectives with 越来越.

Build: "You're getting prettier and prettier."

你 越来越 漂亮 了

Mixed practice All four patterns together

Build: "She always smiles when talking to customers."

她 总是 笑 着 跟 客人 说话

What does this sentence imply?
最近学汉语的人越来越多了。

  • A lot of people have always studied Chinese.✗ 越来越 + 了 signals an ongoing increase, not a constant.
  • More and more people are studying Chinese these days.✓ 越来越多 = growing trend; final 了 reinforces the change.
  • Fewer people are studying Chinese.✗ 多 means "many / more", not fewer.

Build: "He works both conscientiously and enthusiastically."

他 工作 又 认真 又 热情

Fill the blank: 冬天到了，天气 ___ 。
(Winter is here — the weather is getting colder and colder.)

越来越冷了 — sentence-final 了 pairs naturally with 越来越 to flag the change.
