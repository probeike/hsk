HSK 2 Review · Sheet 04 · 是…的

这个工作是他帮我介绍的。

Zhège gōngzuò shì tā bāng wǒ jièshào de.

It was through his recommendation that I got this job.

What this sheet is

You said about 是…的: "I could use more examples, I think there's just a lot." So this sheet is a gallery first, drills second. The theory already lives in Custom 1, grammar point 2 — read it there if you want the full argument. Here you get one short refresher, then twelve worked examples sorted by what's under the spotlight (when / where / how / who), then practice. The title sentence above is the HSK 2 textbook's lesson-4 headline — by the end of the gallery it should read as ordinary, not as a puzzle.

1. Sixty-second refresher 是 opens the spotlight, 的 closes it

The event already happened and both of you already know it happened — nobody is announcing anything. What's actually being discussed is one detail: when, where, how, or who did it. 是 goes in front of that detail, everything after it runs to the verb, and 的 shuts the sentence. Negation keeps the whole frame and just adds 不: 不是…的. Full theory in Custom 1 §2.

S + 是 + spotlight + V (+ O) + 的

Key contrast — this is the whole thing

了 reports news: something happened, and I am telling you about it. 是…的 zooms in on a known event: we both know it happened, and I am telling you which day / place / method. Same facts, different job.

了 — here is the news
我来纽约了。
"I've come to New York." You didn't know. The arrival is the information.
是…的 — here is the detail
我是去年来的。
"It was last year that I came." You already know I'm here. The year is the point.

你是什么时候来纽约的？
——我是去年来的。

Nǐ shì shénme shíhou lái Niǔyuē de? — Wǒ shì qùnián lái de.

you / SHI / what-time / come / New York / DE? — I / SHI / last-year / come / DE

When did you come to New York? — Last year.

Two rules that never bend

No 了 inside the frame — 我是去年来纽约了的 is wrong, because 是…的 already means the event is over. And the closing 的 is not optional. (是 itself can drop in casual affirmative speech — 我去年来的 — but never in the negative.)

2. The gallery Twelve sentences, sorted by what sits in the spotlight

Read each block out loud and ask yourself: what single English word would answer this? If the answer is "last year", "online", "by plane", "my mum" — you're looking at a 是…的 sentence.

Spotlight on WHEN — 什么时候

我是去年搬到纽约的。

Wǒ shì qùnián bān dào Niǔyuē de.

I / SHI / last-year / move / to / New York / DE

It was last year that I moved to New York.

我是2019年开始学汉语的。

Wǒ shì èr líng yī jiǔ nián kāishǐ xué Hànyǔ de.

I / SHI / 2019-year / begin / learn / Chinese / DE

I started learning Chinese in 2019. (The year is the answer.)

你是什么时候认识小丽的？
——我是在大学的时候认识她的。

Nǐ shì shénme shíhou rènshi Xiǎo Lì de? — Wǒ shì zài dàxué de shíhou rènshi tā de.

you / SHI / what-time / know / Xiao Li / DE? — I / SHI / at / university / DE / time / know / her / DE

When did you meet Xiao Li? — I met her back at university.

Spotlight on WHERE — 在哪儿

这本书是在网上买的。

Zhè běn shū shì zài wǎngshàng mǎi de.

this / CL / book / SHI / at / net-on / buy / DE

I got this book online. (No subject needed — the book is the topic.)

我是在北京学的汉语。

Wǒ shì zài Běijīng xué de Hànyǔ.

I / SHI / at / Beijing / learn / DE / Chinese

It was in Beijing that I studied Chinese. (Note where 的 landed — see the note below.)

你是在哪儿吃的饭？

Nǐ shì zài nǎr chī de fàn?

you / SHI / at / where / eat / DE / meal

Where did you eat? (I know you ate — tell me where.)

Spotlight on HOW — 怎么

我是坐飞机来的。

Wǒ shì zuò fēijī lái de.

I / SHI / sit / plane / come / DE

I came by plane.

我是走回来的。

Wǒ shì zǒu huílai de.

I / SHI / walk / come-back / DE

I walked back. (The "how" can be a single verb — 走.)

我的舞蹈是跟韩国老师学的。

Wǒ de wǔdǎo shì gēn Hánguó lǎoshī xué de.

my / DE / dance / SHI / with / Korea / teacher / learn / DE

I learned my dancing from a Korean teacher.

Spotlight on WHO DID IT — 谁

Here the thing affected is fronted as the topic, and 是 sits right in front of the doer. This is the pattern of the title sentence.

这个工作是他帮我介绍的。

Zhège gōngzuò shì tā bāng wǒ jièshào de.

this / CL / job / SHI / he / help / me / introduce / DE

It was through his recommendation that I got this job.

这个菜是我妈妈做的。

Zhège cài shì wǒ māma zuò de.

this / CL / dish / SHI / my / mum / make / DE

My mum made this dish.

这件衣服是小丽帮我买的。

Zhè jiàn yīfu shì Xiǎo Lì bāng wǒ mǎi de.

this / CL / clothing / SHI / Xiao Li / help / me / buy / DE

Xiao Li picked this out for me.

A note on the object — 学的汉语 or 学汉语的?

When the verb carries an object, the closing 的 has two possible homes and you will hear both. Nothing changes in meaning. The HSK 2 textbook prefers the first one, so train that as your default and simply recognise the second.

Textbook default — V + 的 + O
我是在北京学的汉语。
的 slips in between the verb and its object. Very common with places and names: 我是去年去的北京.
Also correct — V + O + 的
我是在北京学汉语的。
的 stays at the very end, the way it does when there's no object at all (我是坐飞机来的).

And the negative — 不是…的

不 goes in front of 是, and both halves of the frame stay put. Here 是 may not be dropped.

我不是坐飞机来的，是坐火车来的。

Wǒ bú shì zuò fēijī lái de, shì zuò huǒchē lái de.

I / not-SHI / sit / plane / come / DE, / SHI / sit / train / come / DE

I didn't come by plane, I came by train.

3. Recognition — 是…的 or 了? Read the situation, then pick

Every one of these hangs on the same question: does the listener already know the event happened? If yes → 是…的. If it's fresh news → 了.

小丽 already knows you went to Shanghai last week — she just wants to know how you travelled. What does she ask?

  • 你是怎么去的？✓ The trip is old news; only 怎么 is in question, so it sits in the spotlight between 是 and 的.
  • 你怎么去了？✗ 了 announces a fresh event. It can't ask about a detail of something you both already know.
  • 你是怎么去了？✗ 了 and 是…的 can never share a sentence — and the closing 的 is missing.

You're telling 小刚 something he doesn't know yet: you bought a new phone. What do you say?

  • 我买了一个新手机。✓ Fresh news → plain 了. The purchase itself is the information.
  • 我是买的一个新手机。✗ 是…的 spotlights a detail of an event you both already know about — but 小刚 doesn't know yet. There's also no when/where/how here to spotlight.
  • 我买一个新手机。✗ With no 了 this reads as a plan or a habit, not a completed purchase.

You've arrived in Beijing to visit 小刚. He can see you standing there, and asks 你是什么时候到的？ You reply:

  • 我是昨天到的。✓ The arrival is obvious — you're in front of him. 昨天 goes into the spotlight, and 的 closes.
  • 我昨天到了。✗ Grammatical, but it announces the arrival as news. He can see you arrived; he asked which day.
  • 我昨天是到的。✗ 是 must sit immediately in front of the spotlighted detail: 我是昨天到的。

4. Production Now build them yourself

Fill the spotlight: A: 你是怎么去上海的？ B: 我是 ___ 去的。
(B answers: by train.)

坐火车 — 我是坐火车去的。 The whole method phrase 坐火车 sits between 是 and the verb.

Build: "It was last year that I came to New York." (Use the full frame — every chunk goes in.)

我 是 去年 来 纽约 的

Build: "My mum made this dish." (Spotlight the doer — the dish is the topic.)

这个菜 是 我妈妈 做 的

Transform. This is plain news: 我昨天来了。
Rewrite it so the time is under the spotlight (your friend already knows you're here).

我是昨天来的。 — 了 drops out completely, 是 goes in front of 昨天, and 的 closes the sentence. Keeping both (我是昨天来了的) is the classic error.

Translate the exchange: "— Where did you buy this? — Online."

你是在哪儿买的？——（我是）在网上买的。 — Nǐ shì zài nǎr mǎi de? — (Wǒ shì) zài wǎngshàng mǎi de. In the reply the subject and even 是 can be dropped, but 的 stays. Answering just 在网上 is fine in speech too.

Translate: "I didn't come by plane, I came by train."

我不是坐飞机来的，是坐火车来的。 — Wǒ bú shì zuò fēijī lái de, shì zuò huǒchē lái de. 不 attaches to 是, and both 是 and 的 survive on both halves. In the negative, 是 can never be dropped.

选词填空。Fill the four blanks (one word in the bank is extra). — "Where did you study Chinese?" — "I studied it in New York."

你在哪儿学汉语？——我在纽约学。

Free practice — answer these three about yourself, out loud, in full 是…的 sentences:
你是什么时候来纽约的？ 你的舞蹈是跟谁学的？ 你是怎么去上班的？

我是去年搬到纽约的。 我的舞蹈是跟韩国老师学的。 我是坐地铁去上班的。 — every one keeps 是 in front of the detail and 的 at the end.
