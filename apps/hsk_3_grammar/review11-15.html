Review 11-15

把、方向、条件

Bǎ, fāngxiàng, tiáojiàn

Cumulative review for Lessons 11-15

How to use this review

This page reviews Lessons 11-15: 才/就, 把 + 在/到, compound directionals, 一边…一边, 把 + result or direction, sequencing, 除了…以外, flexible question words, 极了, 如果…就, complex state complements, and adjective reduplication.

1. Quick diagnostic Choose the speaker's intent

Which sentence suggests the speaker thinks the action happened late?

  • 他九点才起床。才 marks "only then" and often sounds later than expected.
  • 他六点就起床了。就 marks "already / as early as."
  • 他起床起得早。This directly says he got up early.

Fill the result location: 请把笔记本电脑放___桌子上。

放在 tells where the object ends up.

What does the direction complement mean?
请把水果拿过来。

  • Bring the fruit over toward the speaker.过来 points movement toward this side.
  • Take the fruit away from the speaker.That would be 拿过去 or 拿走.
  • Put the fruit in the refrigerator.No final location is stated.

Which sentence correctly says two actions happen at the same time?

  • 她一边听音乐一边打扫房间。一边…一边… marks simultaneous actions.
  • 她先听音乐，然后打扫房间。This marks sequence, not simultaneity.
  • 她听音乐才打扫房间。This does not express two parallel actions.

What changed because of 把?
我把房间打扫干净了。

  • The room became clean through cleaning.把 + result complement tells the result of handling the object.
  • The room cleaned me.The room is the handled object, not the doer.
  • I only planned to clean the room.The result is completed.

Complete the sequence: 我先洗澡，再看邮件，___睡觉。

先…再…然后… gives ordered steps.

Which sentence means "Except for the cake, everything is ready"?

  • 除了蛋糕以外，其他都准备好了。除了 X 以外，都 excludes X from the "all."
  • 除了蛋糕以外，还准备好了。还 means "besides X, also..." and needs what else is included.
  • 蛋糕极了，其他准备好了。极了 is degree, not exception.

Interpret the flexible question word:
这件事我怎么想都不明白。

  • No matter how I think about it, I do not understand.怎么…都… can mean "no matter how."
  • How should I think about it?This is not a real question here.
  • I understand it easily.The sentence says 不明白.

Which sentence uses 如果…就 correctly?

  • 如果明天下雨，我就不去了。如果 sets the condition; 就 marks the result.
  • 明天就如果下雨，我不去了。如果 belongs before the condition.
  • 如果明天下雨，才不去了。才 is not the normal result marker here.

2. Contrast drills Result, direction, condition

Which sentence means the object ends up on the blackboard?

  • 老师把字写在黑板上。写在 gives final location.
  • 老师把字写完了。写完 gives completion, not location.
  • 黑板把字写了。The blackboard is not the doer.

Fill the high-degree expression: 这个节目好看___。

Adj + 极了 = extremely adjective.

Which meaning fits?
她说得大家都笑了。

  • She spoke in such a way that everyone laughed.V得 + result-like clause describes the degree/result of the action.
  • She laughed while speaking.That would use 笑着说.
  • Everyone made her speak.No causative marker appears.

Which sentence uses adjective reduplication naturally?

  • 你慢慢说，别着急。AA reduplication softens/describes the manner.
  • 你慢慢慢说。This is not the pattern.
  • 你说慢慢了。The reduplicated adjective should go before the verb here.

3. Sentence-building Control the object and the result

Build: I got up as early as six.

Build: Please put the suitcase here.

Build: He goes online while listening to music.

Build: I cleaned the room clean.

我把房间打扫干净了

Build: If you have time, come help.

5. Mini-reading Preparing a room

小丽：客人六点就要到了，我们现在才开始打扫，可以吗？
小刚：可以。你先把水果拿过来，再把蛋糕放在冰箱里。
小丽：好。我一边听音乐一边洗盘子。除了这些碗以外，其他都洗干净了。
小刚：我把客人的包放在这儿，可以吗？
小丽：可以。如果小云来了，就请她帮我们发电子邮件。
小刚：这个房间干干净净的，舒服极了。
小丽：别忘了把空调关了。天气不冷，不用开空调。

Xiǎolì: Kèrén liù diǎn jiù yào dào le, wǒmen xiànzài cái kāishǐ dǎsǎo, kěyǐ ma?
Xiǎogāng: Kěyǐ. Nǐ xiān bǎ shuǐguǒ ná guòlai, zài bǎ dàngāo fàng zài bīngxiāng li.
Xiǎolì: Hǎo. Wǒ yìbiān tīng yīnyuè yìbiān xǐ pánzi. Chúle zhèxiē wǎn yǐwài, qítā dōu xǐ gānjìng le.
Xiǎogāng: Wǒ bǎ kèrén de bāo fàng zài zhèr, kěyǐ ma?
Xiǎolì: Kěyǐ. Rúguǒ Xiǎoyún lái le, jiù qǐng tā bāng wǒmen fā diànzǐ yóujiàn.
Xiǎogāng: Zhège fángjiān gāngānjìngjìng de, shūfu jí le.
Xiǎolì: Bié wàng le bǎ kōngtiáo guān le. Tiānqì bù lěng, bú yòng kāi kōngtiáo.

客人什么时候到？

  • 六点客人六点就要到了。
  • 九点九点 is not in this passage.
  • 明天早上No morning arrival is mentioned.

小丽要先做什么？

  • 把水果拿过来你先把水果拿过来。
  • 把电脑送到学校No computer is moved.
  • 去图书馆借词典Not in this passage.

除了什么以外，其他都洗干净了？

  • 盘子She is washing plates while listening to music.
  • 碗除了这些碗以外，其他都洗干净了。
  • 水果Fruit is moved, not washed here.

为什么要关空调？

  • 天气不冷，不用开空调This is stated at the end.
  • 空调坏了It is not broken.
  • 客人不喜欢空调No guest preference is mentioned.

6. Translation and production Use the frames together

Translate: He did not arrive until eight.

他八点才到。

Translate: Please put the book on the table.

请把书放在桌子上。

Translate: Except for him, everyone came.

除了他以外，大家都来了。

Translate: If it rains tomorrow, I won't go.

如果明天下雨，我就不去了。

Write 4-5 sentences about preparing for guests, travel, or class. Use at least three of: 才/就、把…放在/到、先…再…然后、除了…以外、如果…就.
