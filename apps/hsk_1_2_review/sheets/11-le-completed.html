Sheet 11 · Tier 2

了 part 1 — completed action

~30 min
Reference
Quick reminder

Verbal 了: attached directly to the verb. Signals the action was completed. It's not a past tense — it's about completion, which is why it also works for future hypotheticals: 我吃了饭就去。

S + V + 了 + (quantified O) + (rest)

Bare "V + 了 + O" is usually incomplete unless:

  • the object is quantified: 买了三本书,
  • there's a second clause: 吃了饭就去,
  • or it's followed by a sentence-final 了 (see sheet 12): 我吃饭了.

Negation: 没(有), and 了 drops: 我没吃饭. "Haven't yet" = 还没…呢.

Questions: …了吗? or …了没有? A-not-A: V了O没有?

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: She bought quite a few clothes.
  2. Translate: I'll go as soon as I've eaten.
  3. Translate: Have you eaten? (2 forms)
  4. Translate: I haven't eaten yet.

Structured practice

  1. Add 了 if it's needed. Leave blank if not:
    (a) 昨天我看 一个电影。 (b) 我每天早上吃 早饭。 (c) 我明天下课 就去图书馆。 (d) 他喝 三杯咖啡。
  2. Fix: 我没吃了饭。
  3. Fix: 我昨天买了书。 (feels incomplete — choose one way to finish it)
  4. Transform to negative: 他去了银行。
  5. Ask "have you …yet?" A-not-A form: 你吃饭。 → ?
  6. Translate: She took the subway and came home. (two sequential actions)
  7. Translate: I've written three emails today.
  8. Translate: After I finish class, I'll go shopping with you.
  9. Fix: 我昨天不吃饭。
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — Did you go to class yesterday?
    — I didn't go. I wasn't feeling well.
    — Have you taken medicine?
    — I took two pills. I'm better now. (two pills = 两片药)

Warm-up

  1. 她买了不少衣服。
  2. 我吃了饭就去。
  3. 你吃饭了吗? / 你吃饭了没有?
  4. 我还没吃(饭)呢。

Structured

  1. (a) 了 (b) — (c) 了 (d) 了 — habitual actions don't take 了.
  2. 我没吃饭。 — 没 drops 了.
  3. 我昨天买了三本书。 or 我昨天买了书就去了图书馆。
  4. 他没去银行。
  5. 你吃饭了没有?
  6. 她坐了地铁回家了。 (second 了 is the "change" 了 of sheet 12 — signals "she's now home")
  7. 我今天写了三封邮件。
  8. 下了课我就跟你一起去买东西。
  9. 我昨天没吃饭。 — past negation uses 没, not 不.
  10. 你昨天上课了吗? — 我没去。我不太舒服。— 你吃药了吗? — 我吃了两片药，现在好多了。
