What you'll do

This lesson introduces no new grammar — it's the synthesis. You'll revisit the HSK 3 structures most prone to English-speaker error, and drill them in mixed practice.

  1. The big four: 把 / 被 / 着 / 了 — one mental model for all of them.
  2. Conditional pairings — 如果…就, 只要…就, 只有…才.
  3. Mixed practice — end-of-course exercises drawing from all 20 lessons.

Opening diagnostic

This is a capstone review, not a new-grammar lesson. Use it to diagnose three things: can you spot the sentence frame before translating, can you tell whether the first noun is the doer or the patient, and can you separate a normal condition from an enough-condition or a required-condition? If an item feels confusing, name the frame first; the English meaning comes second.

1. The big four: 把 / 被 / 着 / 了 One unified picture

Why these four matter: each rearranges English word order in a specific way. Confusing them costs more accuracy than any other error at this stage. Here they are together:
what happened to B Lessons 10, 11, 13 被 — passive flipped: patient → agent → V B 被 A V + result focus on what was done to B Lesson 19 着 — state / frame freeze-frame, still happening V 着 (…) existence or backdrop action Lessons 3, 4 了 — state change boundary crossed / new state V 了 / … 了 completion OR new situation Lessons 4, 10, 11, 13, 19
Four icons, one gallery. When you encounter one of these in a sentence, the first question isn't "what does it mean?" but "which schema am I looking at?"

Active vs passive — same event, two angles

把 (active)
他把我的书拿走了
He took my book.
被 (passive)
我的书被他拿走了
My book was taken by him.

Both describe the same event. Choose based on what you want to foreground: the doer (把) or the thing done-to (被).

他的话使我想起了小时候。

Tā de huà shǐ wǒ xiǎng qǐ le xiǎoshíhou.

His words made me recall my childhood. (causative 使 + extended directional 起 + state-change 了.)

我被他影响了，现在我也喜欢运动了。

Wǒ bèi tā yǐngxiǎng le, xiànzài wǒ yě xǐhuan yùndòng le.

I've been influenced by him; now I like exercising too. (被 passive + 了 change-of-state, twice.)

Practice

What happened in 我的照相机被朋友拿走了?

  • I took my friend's camera.✗ 被 is passive; the subject is the patient, not the agent.
  • My camera was taken (away) by my friend.✓ B 被 A V + result (拿走) + 了.
  • My friend lost my camera.✗ 拿走 means "take away", not "lose".

Which is the correct 把-sentence for "He finished drinking the coffee"?

  • 他把咖啡喝完了。✓ S + 把 + O + V + result + 了.
  • 他把喝完咖啡了。✗ The object must come right after 把, before the verb.
  • 他把咖啡喝。✗ 把 needs a result/complement; a bare verb is incomplete.

Fill the blank: 他拿___一个碗吃饭。
(He's eating (while) holding a bowl.)

着 — the backdrop action "holding" frames the main action "eating".

Build: "I've been influenced by him."

我 被 他 影响 了

Build a 把-sentence: "He put the camera on the table."

他 把 照相机 放 在 桌子上 了

Personalise it. Look up from the screen and describe where you actually are, plus one thing you got done today. Write 3–5 true sentences using all four frames of this section at least once each: 把 + object + result, 被 for something that happened to you or your things, Verb + 着 for a state or a backdrop, and a sentence-final 了 that reports a change. Use four different verbs, and keep every sentence true.

我现在坐在桌子前面写这几句话。旁边的门开着，外面下着一点儿小雨。今天早上我把手机关上了，安安静静地写了一个小时。我的手表被弟弟拿走了，到现在也没还给我。最近天黑得越来越早了，我又开始喝热茶了。

Wǒ xiànzài zuò zài zhuōzi qiánmiàn xiě zhè jǐ jù huà. Pángbiān de mén kāi zhe, wàimiàn xià zhe yìdiǎnr xiǎo yǔ. Jīntiān zǎoshang wǒ bǎ shǒujī guān shàng le, ān'ānjìngjìng de xiě le yí ge xiǎoshí. Wǒ de shǒubiǎo bèi dìdi ná zǒu le, dào xiànzài yě méi huán gěi wǒ. Zuìjìn tiān hēi de yuè lái yuè zǎo le, wǒ yòu kāishǐ hē rè chá le.

I'm sitting at my desk right now writing these few lines. The door beside me is standing open, and outside a little rain is falling. This morning I switched my phone off and wrote in complete quiet for an hour. My watch has been carried off by my little brother and he still hasn't given it back. It has been getting dark earlier and earlier lately, so I've started drinking hot tea again.

Why these are right: 把手机关上了 fronts the object precisely so a result can land on it — that is the whole point of 把, and 把手机关 with nothing after the verb would be unfinished. 手表被弟弟拿走了 is the same kind of event turned round: the affected thing leads, the doer follows 被, and the verb still carries a result. 开着 and 下着 are neither past nor progressive — they freeze a state that is simply there while the main business goes on. And 越来越早了 / 喝热茶了 is the fourth frame: nothing was completed, a boundary was crossed.

Check your own sentences:

  • Both 把 and 被 demand a result: 把手机关上了 ✓ · 把手机关 ✗
  • The noun after 被 is the doer; the noun after 把 is the thing acted on. Did you get the two round the right way?
  • 着 is a state or a backdrop, never a finished event: 门开着 ✓ · 昨天我看着一个电影 ✗
  • Does your final 了 report a change ("it wasn't, now it is") rather than just past time?
  • Four different verbs — and is this really the room you are sitting in?

2. Conditional pairings Three "if/then" frames, sharply distinguished

如果 … 就 … (general if)
如果下雨，我就不去
If it rains, I won't go. (Lesson 15)
只要 … 就 … (sufficient)
只要不下雨，我就去
As long as it doesn't rain, I'll go. (Lesson 17)
只有 … 才 … (necessary)
只有天气好，我才去
I'll only go if the weather's good. (Lesson 19)

Notice the pivot word disambiguates: 就 for generic or sufficient, 才 for necessary.

Practice

"Only if you work hard can you do well." Pick the natural Chinese:

  • 只有努力，才能做得好。✓ Necessary condition pairs 只有 with 才.
  • 只有努力，就能做得好。✗ 只有 must pair with 才, not 就.
  • 只要努力，才能做得好。✗ 只要 pairs with 就, and weakens the claim to "sufficient".

What does 只要不下雨，我就去。 mean?

  • Only if it doesn't rain will I go.✗ That would be 只有…才. 只要…就 is weaker ("sufficient").
  • As long as it doesn't rain, I'll go.✓ 只要 X 就 Y — "X is enough for Y".
  • If it rains, I'll still go.✗ The sentence says "not raining", not "raining".

Fill the blank: 只有真正做事情的时候，___能了解有多难。
(Only when you really do things can you understand how hard it is.)

才 — 只有 pairs with 才 (necessary condition).

Build: "Only by working hard can one do well."

只有 努力 才 能 做得好

Build: "As long as you work hard, you'll do well."

只要 你 努力 就 会 做得好

Personalise it. Write 3–5 true sentences about your own week using all three frames at least once each: 如果 … 就 … (a plain if), 只要 … 就 … (that on its own is enough), and 只有 … 才 … (without it, nothing). Use three different verbs in the result clauses, and pick each frame because it is true of your life, not because it sounds impressive.

我住的地方离地铁站很远。如果晚上下大雨，我就不出去，在家里做饭。只要早上起得来，我就走路去上班，路上还能看看树和花。可是只有到了周末，我才有时间去看朋友。所以我常跟他们说：只有你们来我家，我们才能一起吃个晚饭。

Wǒ zhù de dìfang lí dìtiězhàn hěn yuǎn. Rúguǒ wǎnshang xià dà yǔ, wǒ jiù bù chūqu, zài jiā li zuò fàn. Zhǐyào zǎoshang qǐ de lái, wǒ jiù zǒulù qù shàngbān, lù shang hái néng kànkan shù hé huā. Kěshì zhǐyǒu dào le zhōumò, wǒ cái yǒu shíjiān qù kàn péngyou. Suǒyǐ wǒ cháng gēn tāmen shuō: zhǐyǒu nǐmen lái wǒ jiā, wǒmen cái néng yìqǐ chī ge wǎnfàn.

Where I live is a long way from the subway station. If it rains hard in the evening I don't go out — I cook at home. As long as I can get myself up in the morning, I walk to work, and on the way I get to look at the trees and the flowers. But only once the weekend comes do I have time to see friends. So I keep telling them: only if you come to my place can we have dinner together.

Why these are right: three frames, three different claims. 如果…就… just links a hypothetical to its consequence — heavy rain is one case among many. 只要…就… says the condition is enough on its own: getting up is all it takes, nothing else has to be true. 只有…才… says the condition cannot be dispensed with — no weekend, no friends. In all three the pivot sits inside the result clause, after its subject: 我就不出去, 我才有时间. Which pair you choose is a claim about the world, not a matter of style.

Check your own sentences:

  • 如果 → 就, 只要 → 就, 只有 → 才. A mismatched pivot is the one slip that changes the meaning outright.
  • Is the pivot after the subject of the result clause? 我才有时间 ✓ · 才我有时间 ✗
  • Is your 只有 sentence honestly exclusive? If some other route gets you the same result, it was a 只要 sentence.
  • Don't hang 了 on the end of a 才-clause — 才 restricts, it doesn't report completion.
  • Three different verbs in the result clauses, and all of it true of this week.

  • My friend borrowed my phone.✓ B (my phone) 被 A (friend) V (borrow-away) — passive.

  • 如果你努力才做得好。✗ 如果 pairs with 就, not 才.

Which sentence correctly says "The problem has finally been solved"?

  • 问题终于被解决了。✓ 被 passive + result verb 解决 + state-change 了.
  • 问题终于把解决了。✗ 把 is active (S 把 O V + result); you need 被 for the passive here.

Build: "They are often mistaken for each other by other people."

他们 经常 被 别人 认错

Write the paragraph. Somebody once taught you something practical — cooking, driving, a sport, an instrument, a piece of software. Tell that story in 4–6 connected sentences, using every frame this lesson pulls together at least once: 把 with a result, 被 with a result, Verb + 着 as the backdrop to another action, a 了 that marks a change of state, and one conditional pair (如果 … 就 …, 只要 … 就 … or 只有 … 才 …).

以前我一点儿也不会做菜。今年春天妈妈来看我，住了两个星期，每天我都站在她旁边，看着她做饭。她总是先把菜都洗好，一边做一边跟我说话。有一天她出去了，我自己做了一次，菜被我做坏了，可是大家还是都吃完了。妈妈笑着说：“只有多做几次，你才会知道要用多少水。”她走了以后，我终于能做几个菜了。

Yǐqián wǒ yìdiǎnr yě bú huì zuò cài. Jīnnián chūntiān māma lái kàn wǒ, zhù le liǎng ge xīngqī, měi tiān wǒ dōu zhàn zài tā pángbiān, kàn zhe tā zuò fàn. Tā zǒngshì xiān bǎ cài dōu xǐ hǎo, yìbiān zuò yìbiān gēn wǒ shuōhuà. Yǒu yì tiān tā chūqu le, wǒ zìjǐ zuò le yí cì, cài bèi wǒ zuò huài le, kěshì dàjiā háishi dōu chī wán le. Māma xiào zhe shuō: "Zhǐyǒu duō zuò jǐ cì, nǐ cái huì zhīdào yào yòng duōshao shuǐ." Tā zǒu le yǐhòu, wǒ zhōngyú néng zuò jǐ ge cài le.

I couldn't cook at all before. This spring my mother came to stay with me for two weeks, and every day I stood beside her watching her cook. She always got the vegetables washed first, chatting to me while she worked. One day she went out and I cooked by myself; the dish was ruined by me, but everyone ate it anyway. My mother said with a laugh, "Only by doing it a good many times will you learn how much water to use." After she left, I could finally cook a few dishes.

Why this works as a paragraph: each frame is earning its place. 把菜都洗好 is a disposal — the vegetables are handled and end up in a new state. 菜被我做坏了 flips the same kind of event so the dish leads and the blame follows 被, with 坏 as the result the passive requires. 看着 and 笑着 are backdrops, not events: they hold while something else happens (她做饭, 说). 只有多做几次…你才会知道 is a necessary condition, spoken as advice. And the last 了 is the point of the whole story — 终于能做几个菜了 reports a boundary crossed, not an action finished.

Check your own paragraph:

  • Do your 把 and 被 sentences each end in a result? Neither frame tolerates a bare verb.
  • Is the noun before 被 the thing affected, and the noun before 把 the doer?
  • Is your 着 a backdrop for another verb, rather than a stand-in for past tense?
  • Does at least one 了 mark a change — something you couldn't do before and can now?
  • Does the conditional pair match (只有→才, 如果/只要→就), and does it read as one story rather than five frames in a row?

Four errors. Each sentence below contains exactly one mistake, and all four are mistakes with the frames this lesson reviews. Rewrite all four correctly in the box, then check.
1. 妈妈把菜洗。
2. 我的手表被没弟弟拿走。
3. 昨天我看着一个电影。
4. 只有多跟中国朋友说话，你的中文就会好。

1. 妈妈把菜洗干净了。
2. 我的手表没被弟弟拿走。
3. 昨天我看了一个电影。
4. 只有多跟中国朋友说话，你的中文才会好。

1. Māma bǎ cài xǐ gānjìng le. 2. Wǒ de shǒubiǎo méi bèi dìdi ná zǒu. 3. Zuótiān wǒ kàn le yí ge diànyǐng. 4. Zhǐyǒu duō gēn Zhōngguó péngyou shuōhuà, nǐ de Zhōngwén cái huì hǎo.

1. Mum got the vegetables washed clean. 2. My watch wasn't taken by my little brother. 3. Yesterday I watched a film. 4. Only if you talk to Chinese friends a lot will your Chinese get good.

  1. 把菜洗 → 把菜洗干净了. 把 fronts the object precisely so that a result can land on it; with nothing after the verb the sentence has no point. 洗干净了 / 洗好了 / 洗完了 all work — a bare 洗 does not.
  2. 被没弟弟 → 没被弟弟. Negation stands in front of 被, exactly as it stands in front of 把. There is no room for it inside the passive, and once 没 is there the 了 goes.
  3. 看着一个电影 → 看了一个电影. 着 marks a state or a backdrop that is still holding; a film watched yesterday is a finished event, which is 了 territory. 着 would only be right as scenery for another action: 他看着电影吃饭.
  4. 只有…就 → 只有…才. 只有 states a condition you cannot do without, and only 才 answers it. 就 belongs to 如果 and 只要, and would weaken the claim to "a bit of chatting is all it takes". Note 才 still sits after the subject of the result clause: 你的中文才会好.

Check your own rewrites:

  • Does every 把 and every 被 end in a result or 了?
  • Is the negator in front of 把/被, never inside — and did the 了 disappear under 没?
  • 着 = still holding or backdrop; 了 = finished or changed. Which did the sentence actually mean?
  • 只有 → 才; 如果 / 只要 → 就. Did you check the pair rather than trust the feel?

Translate — four sentences. Write them in Chinese. The structure in brackets is compulsory, and every word listed after 用 must appear.
1. The room was cleaned by 小刚 yesterday. [被 + result + 了] · 用: 房间 · 打扫 · 干净
2. Please put the fruit in the fridge. [把 + place] · 用: 水果 · 放 · 冰箱
3. He does his homework with music playing. [Verb + 着 as backdrop] · 用: 音乐 · 听 · 作业
4. Only if you really give it a go will you find out how interesting it is. [只有 … 才 …] · 用: 试 · 真正 · 多么

1. 房间昨天被小刚打扫干净了。
2. 请把水果放在冰箱里。
3. 他听着音乐写作业。
4. 只有自己真正试一试，你才知道这件事多么有意思。

1. Fángjiān zuótiān bèi Xiǎogāng dǎsǎo gānjìng le. 2. Qǐng bǎ shuǐguǒ fàng zài bīngxiāng li. 3. Tā tīng zhe yīnyuè xiě zuòyè. 4. Zhǐyǒu zìjǐ zhēnzhèng shì yi shì, nǐ cái zhīdào zhè jiàn shì duōme yǒu yìsi.

1. The room was cleaned by Xiaogang yesterday. 2. Please put the fruit in the fridge. 3. He does his homework with music playing. 4. Only if you really give it a go will you find out how interesting it is.

Why these are right: (1) the room is the thing affected, so it leads, and 打扫 alone would leave the passive unfinished — 干净了 is the result 被 requires. (2) A 把 sentence with a destination takes 放在 + place; the object has to sit between 把 and the verb, never after it. (3) 听着音乐 is the backdrop and 写作业 is the main action — the 着-clause comes first, and neither verb takes 了 because nothing is being reported as finished. (4) 只有 opens the condition, 才 waits inside the result clause behind 你, and 多么 keeps its exclamatory force in front of the adjective phrase.

Check your own four:

  • Sentence 1: first noun = the thing cleaned, and a result glued to the verb.
  • Sentence 2: is the object between 把 and the verb? 把水果放在冰箱里 ✓ · 把放水果在冰箱里 ✗
  • Sentence 3: the backdrop clause goes first, and 着 rides on the backdrop verb, not the main one.
  • Sentence 4: 才 after the subject, and you resisted writing 就 after 只有.
  • Did every word listed after 用 actually make it into your sentence?

Answer the questions — full sentences, and true. Each question is built so that one of this lesson's frames is the natural way to answer it; the frame in brackets is compulsory.
1. 今天早上你把什么东西放好了？
2. 你的东西被别人借走过吗？后来还给你了吗？
3. 你写作业的时候，喜欢听着音乐吗？为什么？
4. 你觉得，只有在什么时候，你才能真正休息？ 1. [把 + result + 了] · 2. [被 + result] · 3. [Verb + 着] · 4. [只有 … 才 …]

1. 今天早上我把桌子上的书都放好了，房间看起来干净多了。
2. 我的词典被同学拿去用过一次，第二天他就还给我了。
3. 我写作业的时候不喜欢听着音乐，因为听着音乐我就写得很慢。
4. 我觉得只有把手机关上以后，我才能真正休息。

1. Jīntiān zǎoshang wǒ bǎ zhuōzi shang de shū dōu fàng hǎo le, fángjiān kàn qǐlai gānjìng duō le. 2. Wǒ de cídiǎn bèi tóngxué ná qù yòng guo yí cì, dì-èr tiān tā jiù huán gěi wǒ le. 3. Wǒ xiě zuòyè de shíhou bù xǐhuan tīng zhe yīnyuè, yīnwèi tīng zhe yīnyuè wǒ jiù xiě de hěn màn. 4. Wǒ juéde zhǐyǒu bǎ shǒujī guān shàng yǐhòu, wǒ cái néng zhēnzhèng xiūxi.

1. This morning I put all the books on the desk away, and the room looks a lot tidier. 2. A classmate took my dictionary away to use once, and he gave it back to me the next day. 3. I don't like having music on while I do my homework, because with music playing I write very slowly. 4. I think it's only after the phone is switched off that I can really rest.

Why these answer the questions: (1) the question asks what you dealt with, so 把 is right and 放好了 supplies the result — 把书放 would answer nothing. (2) The dictionary leads because the question is about what happened to your things; 拿去 carries the result, 过 says it happened at least once, and the reply then switches to the doer's side (他还给我了). (3) 听着音乐 is a backdrop clause sitting in front of the main verb 写 — the answer stays in the 着 frame even though it is negative. (4) The question already restricts, so the answer takes the restriction up with 只有…才…, with 我 between the comma and 才 — and 把手机关上 shows the lesson's other frame doing the work inside the condition, since a bare 手机关上 would leave the switching-off without a doer.

Check your own answers:

  • Is each answer a whole sentence? These questions are worth a full clause each.
  • Answer 1: object between 把 and the verb, and a result on the verb.
  • Answer 2: the affected thing first, the doer after 被, a result on the verb — and 过 if it's a "has it ever" answer.
  • Answer 3: does 着 ride on the backdrop verb, with the main verb following?
  • Answer 4: 才 after the subject — and is the claim honestly "only then", not merely "usually then"?
