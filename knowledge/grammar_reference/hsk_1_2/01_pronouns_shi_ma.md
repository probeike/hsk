# Pronouns · 是 · 吗 · negation

Pronouns: 我 你 您 他 她 它 + 们 for plural. The polite 您 has no plural form of its own.

**是 sentences**: S + 是 + N. Don't use 是 with adjectives — adjectives can be predicates on their own (usually with 很: 她很高).

**Yes/no questions**: statement + 吗. The answer echoes the verb: 是 → 是/不是; 有 → 有/没有.

**Negation**: 不 for present, future, habits, adjectives; 没(有) for 有 and for completed-past actions. Tone: 不 goes from *bù* → *bú* before a 4th tone (不是 bú shì).
