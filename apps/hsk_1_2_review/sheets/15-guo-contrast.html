Sheet 15 · Tier 2

过 — experience · and the big three: 了 / 过 / 着

~30 min
Reference
Quick reminder

V + 过: the speaker has the experience of having done this at some point in life.

S + V + 过 + (O)

Negation: 没 + V + 过 — "have never done it". The 过 stays.

Questions: …过吗? or …过没有?

The big three at a glance

ParticleCapturesNegation
了the action was completed没(有) V (drop 了)
过the action has happened ≥ once in life没 V 过 (keep 过)
着the action's resulting state is ongoing没 V 着

Think of it as when along the timeline: 了 — a specific point done; 过 — "ever been to that point"; 着 — standing at that point right now.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: Have you ever seen that film?
  2. Translate: I've never been to Beijing.
  3. Translate each meaning: I ate Peking duck (that day). / I've eaten Peking duck before. / I'm in the middle of eating.
  4. Negate: 他学过汉语。

Structured practice

  1. 了 / 过 / 着?
    (a) 我去 三次北京。 (three trips — lifetime count) (b) 我昨天去 银行。 (that one trip) (c) 他坐 喝咖啡。 (sitting & drinking) (d) 我学 一点儿日语。 (before in my life)
  2. Fix: 他没去过了北京。
  3. Fix: 我学了汉语。 (feels incomplete — add something that lets "learned" count as a life experience)
  4. Translate: Have you tried this dish? It's really good.
  5. Translate: I've been to China twice.
  6. Translate: She's wearing glasses and reading a book.
  7. Translate: Last week I watched an interesting Chinese movie. (which particle?)
  8. Contrast drill. Translate each: (a) He's studying English. (b) He has studied English before. (c) He finished the English lesson. (d) He sat studying English.
  9. Error-spot: 我吃过北京烤鸭在去年。
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — Have you ever been to Shanghai?
    — Yes, I've been three times. The first time was in 2019. Have you?
    — I've never been. I'd like to go this year.

Warm-up

  1. 你看过那个电影吗?
  2. 我没去过北京。
  3. 我(那天)吃了北京烤鸭。 · 我吃过北京烤鸭。 · 我正在吃饭呢。
  4. 他没学过汉语。

Structured

  1. (a) 过 (b) 了 (c) 着 (d) 过
  2. 他没去过北京。 — 没 drops 了 but keeps 过.
  3. 我学过汉语。 or 我学了两年汉语。
  4. 你吃过这个菜吗? 真好吃。
  5. 我去过中国两次。 / 我去过两次中国。
  6. 她戴着眼镜看书。
  7. 上星期我看了一部很有意思的中国电影。 — specific event, use 了.
  8. (a) 他在学英语。 (b) 他学过英语。 (c) 他上完英语课了。 (d) 他坐着学英语。
  9. Time phrases go before the verb: 我去年吃过北京烤鸭。
  10. 你去过上海吗? — 去过，去过三次。第一次是2019年。你呢? — 我没去过。今年想去(一下)。
