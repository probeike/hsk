# Complements: 结果 & 得-state

#### Result complement — V + [result]

A second verb or adjective fuses onto the main verb to say what the result was. The result-word completes the idea. The pair acts like a single verb for 了/过/没 purposes.

我做完了作业。 I finished the homework.

我没听懂。 I didn't catch / understand it.

你说错了。 You said (it) wrong.

Common result words: 完 (finish), 到 (reach/obtain), 见 (perceive), 好 (well-done), 错 (wrongly), 懂 (understand), 会 (mastered), 清楚 (clearly).

Negate the *whole cluster* with 没: 没做完 / 没听懂 / 没看见.

#### State complement with 得

V + 得 + [state phrase] · if there's an object: V + O + V + 得 + [state phrase]

Describes *how* the action is/was done: fast, fluent, poorly.

你穿得太少了。 You're wearing too little.

她(说)汉语说得很流利。 She speaks Chinese very fluently.

Notice the double-verb when there's an object: the first verb carries the object, the second carries 得. The first verb can drop colloquially.

**Negate** the state, not the verb: 说得不好.

Don't confuse 得 (*de*, state-complement) with 的 (*de*, possessive/nominaliser). Different characters, same pinyin.
