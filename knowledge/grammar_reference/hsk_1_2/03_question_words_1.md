# Question words I: 什么 / 谁 / 哪 / 哪儿

Chinese keeps the question word *in the slot it's asking about*. You don't move it to the front the way English does. No 吗 needed — the question word is the question marker.

- 什么 (shénme) — what. 什么 + noun = "what / which ___".

- 谁 (shéi / shuí) — who. Same slot as any noun.

- 哪 (nǎ) — which. Must take a measure word: 哪 + MW + N. For countries and books the measure is often elided: 哪国人 / 哪本.

- 哪儿 (nǎr) — where. Often paired with 在: 在哪儿? = "where at?"

Echo principle: an answer typically slots the replacement right back into the same position as the question word.
