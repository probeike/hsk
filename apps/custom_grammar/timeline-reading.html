Custom reading 02 · Time words

两个星期之后，我要辞职！

Liǎng ge xīngqī zhīhòu, wǒ yào cízhí!

Reading & writing practice

About this reading

Everything here uses only words from HSK 1–2, HSK 3 lessons 1–8, and the twelve new words from the time-words lesson (以后, 之后, 前天/后天, 前年/后年, 练习, 搬家, 辞职, 当, 老板, 舞蹈, 压力, 软件工程师). Watch the purple time expressions as you read — in every passage, ask yourself whether each one is a point (两个月前) or a span (最近两个月), and notice that not one of them ever sits at the end of its sentence. Read characters first; tap Show pinyin only when stuck.

第一部分 · 阅读 Part 1 — Reading

1. 课文 Read the conversation

小丽：好久没看见你了！你最近怎么样？
我：不太好。最近两个月我很忙，公司的事情特别多，压力很大。
小丽：那你打算做什么？
我：两个星期之后，我要辞职。
小丽：辞职？你不是软件工程师吗？你在那个公司工作了几年了？
我：三年了。前年我还很喜欢这个工作，但是最近越来越累了。
小丽：你的老板知道吗？他会很不高兴吧。
我：他还不知道。下个星期一我就告诉他。
小丽：辞职以后你想做什么？
我：下个月我想去找一个舞蹈课。你知道，我最喜欢韩国的舞蹈。
小丽：我记得你以前跳舞跳得很好。
我：那是以前的事情了。最近两个月我几乎没跳舞。
小丽：你是什么时候搬到纽约的？
我：去年。去年三月我从北京搬到这儿，前年还在北京上班呢。
小丽：以后你想当舞蹈老师吗？
我：想。以后我想当舞蹈老师，或者当老板。
小丽：那太好了。下个星期六晚上我们一起吃饭，好吗？
我：好啊。那天下午我就去找你。

Xiǎolì: Hǎo jiǔ méi kànjiàn nǐ le! Nǐ zuìjìn zěnmeyàng?

Xiǎolì: Nà nǐ dǎsuan zuò shénme?

Wǒ: Sān nián le. Qiánnián wǒ hái hěn xǐhuan zhège gōngzuò, dànshì zuìjìn yuèláiyuè lèi le.

Wǒ: Tā hái bù zhīdào. Xià ge xīngqīyī wǒ jiù gàosu tā.

Xiǎolì: Nà tài hǎo le. Xià ge xīngqīliù wǎnshang wǒmen yìqǐ chī fàn, hǎo ma?
Wǒ: Hǎo a. Nà tiān xiàwǔ wǒ jiù qù zhǎo nǐ.

我打算什么时候辞职？ (When am I planning to quit?)

  • 两个星期之后✓ 两个星期之后，我要辞职。
  • 下个月✗ 下个月 is when I'll look for a dance class.
  • 下个星期一✗ That's the day I'll tell the boss, not the day I leave.

我在那个公司工作了多长时间？ (How long have I worked at that company?)

  • 三年✓ 你在那个公司工作了几年了？——三年了。
  • 两个月✗ 最近两个月 is only the busy stretch.
  • 两个星期✗ That's how long until I quit.

我是什么时候搬到纽约的？ (When did I move to New York?)

  • 去年三月✓ 去年三月我从北京搬到这儿。
  • 前年✗ 前年 I was still working in Beijing.
  • 下个月✗ 下个月 is in the future.

辞职以后我想做什么？ (What do I want to do after quitting?)

  • 找一个舞蹈课，以后当舞蹈老师或者当老板✓ 下个月我想去找一个舞蹈课……以后我想当舞蹈老师，或者当老板。
  • 回北京上班✗ Beijing is where I was 前年 — not a plan.
  • 在家休息一年✗ Never mentioned.

3. 短文 Read the passage

我叫马可，是软件工程师，在纽约工作。两个月前我搬家了，因为那个房间太小了。搬家以后，我的东西还没放好，家里也不太安静。 前两个月我几乎每天都很忙。最近两个月我没练习汉语，一次也没练习。以前我每天早上都学习一个小时，现在没有时间了。 上个星期我睡得不好，晚上一直想着公司的事情。昨天早上我起床的时候已经八点了，还没吃饭就去上班了。 下个星期我要开始再练习汉语。我每个星期三晚上有汉语课，下课以后我就去跳舞。跳舞的时候，我特别快乐，压力就没有了。 前年我在北京工作，那时候我一点儿压力也没有。两个星期之后我辞职，我的老板会很不高兴，但是我会很高兴。明年我想当舞蹈老师，以后也想当老板。

马可是什么时候搬家的？ (When did Marco move house?)

  • 两个月前✓ 两个月前我搬家了 — a point, two months back.
  • 前两个月，一直在搬家✗ 前两个月 in this passage describes being busy, not moving.
  • 上个星期✗ 上个星期 is when he slept badly.

最近两个月马可的汉语怎么样？ (What about his Chinese in the past two months?)

  • 他一次也没练习✓ 最近两个月我没练习汉语，一次也没练习。
  • 他每天早上都学习一个小时✗ That was 以前 — before, not now.
  • 他下课以后练习✗ 下课以后 he goes dancing.

马可什么时候去跳舞？ (When does he go dancing?)

  • 每个星期三，下课以后✓ 我每个星期三晚上有汉语课，下课以后我就去跳舞。
  • 每天早上✗ 每天早上 was for studying, and that was 以前.
  • 上个星期✗ 上个星期 he slept badly.

昨天早上马可为什么没吃饭？ (Why didn't he eat yesterday morning?)

  • 他起床的时候已经八点了✓ 起床的时候已经八点了，还没吃饭就去上班了。
  • 因为他搬家了✗ The move was two months earlier.
  • 因为他要去跳舞✗ He went to work, not to dance.

7. 改写 Shift the passage sentence along the timeline

Each sentence below is quoted from a passage you have just read. Move it to a different place on the timeline and type the whole result. The answers are not in the passages — you have to operate the rule, not find the line.

Transform. Passage 5c says 三天以后是小丽的生日。 Six days have gone by since then, so the birthday is behind us. Say the same fact from today: it was three days ago. Keep 是小丽的生日 and start with the time phrase.

三天前是小丽的生日。 — exactly one thing moves: the marker on the far side of 三天 flips 以后 → 前. The length of time never changes seats, because 前 after a number is what pins a single point. Write 前三天是小丽的生日 ✗ and you have turned the birthday into a three-day band. 三天以前 and 三天之前 are equally correct.

Transform. Passage 5c says 小张上个星期就买好了一件绿衬衫。 Change the story: he has bought nothing yet — the shopping happens next week. Rewrite it with 下个星期 and 要买; drop 就 and 好. Keep 小张 and 一件绿衬衫.

下个星期小张要买一件绿衬衫。 — two changes travel together. 上 flips to 下 (the page already turned up over the pad versus the one still hanging below), and the 了 has to go: 了 reports a boundary that has been crossed, and nothing has been crossed yet. 下个星期小张要买了一件绿衬衫 ✗. 下个星期 may stand before 小张 or right after it — never after 买.

8. 回答问题 Answer in a full sentence

Answer the question about passage 5d: 李小美是什么时候搬到上海的？
用: 她 · 是 · 去年 · 搬到上海 · 的

她是去年搬到上海的。 — a 是…的 question asks when a finished event happened, and the answer keeps the frame: 是 opens it, 的 closes it, and the time word sits inside, straight after 是. 她搬到上海去年 ✗ — Chinese has no end slot for time. (她是去年搬到的上海, with 的 in front of the place, is the other standard position and just as good.)

Answer the question about passage 3: 马可以前每天早上学习多长时间？ Start with 他, and put nothing after 一个小时.
用: 他 · 每天早上 · 学习 · 一个小时

他每天早上学习一个小时。 — one sentence, two kinds of time in two different seats. 每天早上 says when, so it stands in front of the verb; 一个小时 says how long, so it stands after it. Swap them and both are wrong: 他一个小时每天早上学习 ✗. Adding 都 after 每天早上 is optional and very natural.

9. 翻译 English → Chinese — new situations

Translate. "Half a year ago my uncle opened a small shop."
用: 半年前 · 我叔叔 · 开了 · 一个小商店

半年前我叔叔开了一个小商店。 — opening a shop happens at one moment, so the length of time comes first and 前 hangs off the back of it. 前半年我叔叔开了一个小商店 ✗ turns the six months into a band and says he spent the whole half-year opening it. The time phrase may also sit right after 我叔叔; it may never sit after 开了.

Translate. "Last Sunday I read for three hours in the library."
用: 上个星期天 · 我 · 在图书馆 · 看了 · 三个小时 · 书

上个星期天我在图书馆看了三个小时书。 — the stage is set before anyone acts on it: 时间 (上个星期天) → 地点 (在图书馆) → 动词 (看了). Then the duration squeezes in between the verb and its object. English orders all four the other way round, which is why 我看书三个小时在图书馆上个星期天 ✗ feels natural and is wrong twice over. 三个小时的书 with 的 is equally good.

10. 改错 Fix the summary

Three errors. A classmate summarised passage 3 (马可) like this. Each sentence contains exactly one time-word mistake. Find all three, write the corrected summary in the box, then check.
马可是软件工程师，他搬到了一个新房间两个月前。他每个星期三晚上有汉语课，下课以前他就去跳舞。跳舞的时候他特别快乐，所以他经常跳舞两个小时。

马可是软件工程师，两个月前他搬到了一个新房间。他每个星期三晚上有汉语课，下课以后他就去跳舞。跳舞的时候他特别快乐，所以他经常跳两个小时舞。

  1. 他搬到了一个新房间两个月前 → 两个月前他搬到了一个新房间. English parks time at the end of the sentence; Chinese has no such slot at all. The offset must stand in front of the verb — at the head of the clause, or straight after 他. Note it is 两个月前 and not 前两个月: moving house is a single moment, not a two-month band.
  2. 下课以前 → 下课以后. These two hang off the back of the same clause and differ by one character, but they point in opposite directions. As written he goes dancing before the class is over, which is not what the passage says — and it leaves the Chinese class doing nothing in the sentence.
  3. 跳舞两个小时 → 跳两个小时舞. How long an action lasted goes between the verb and its object, never behind it. 跳舞 is already verb + object welded together (跳 + 舞), so the duration has to be wedged inside the pair. The other standard repair is to copy the verb: 他经常跳舞跳两个小时.

  • Is every time word in front of its verb? Nothing left dangling at the end of a sentence?
  • Point or span? 两个月前 for the single moment he moved; 最近两个月 for the whole stretch. Check each one you wrote.
  • If you wrote a "hasn't done it for X" sentence, does the length of the gap sit before 没 (最近两个月他没…), with no 了 on the verb?
  • Does 以后 hang off the back of its clause (搬家以后, 下课以后), never in front of it?
  • Are these your words, or the passage's? Compare line by line — a copied sentence teaches you nothing.

12. 写一写 Your own timeline

Now the same shape, but about you. Write 4–5 sentences covering both ends of your own timeline: what your past two months have been like as a whole stretch (plus one thing that happened at a single moment in them), and what the next two weeks hold. Use at least four of: 最近两个月 · X前 · …没…了 · 下个… · 两个星期以后 · …以后…就….

最近两个月我的工作特别多，晚上经常十点回家，所以我一个月没运动了。三个星期前我开始学做中国菜，现在每个周末都自己做饭。下个星期六我要去朋友家吃饭，吃完饭以后我们就一起去看电影。两个星期以后我要去中国旅游一个星期，所以下个星期我就要开始准备了。

Zuìjìn liǎng ge yuè wǒ de gōngzuò tèbié duō, wǎnshang jīngcháng shí diǎn huí jiā, suǒyǐ wǒ yí ge yuè méi yùndòng le. Sān ge xīngqī qián wǒ kāishǐ xué zuò Zhōngguó cài, xiànzài měi ge zhōumò dōu zìjǐ zuò fàn. Xià ge xīngqīliù wǒ yào qù péngyou jiā chī fàn, chī wán fàn yǐhòu wǒmen jiù yìqǐ qù kàn diànyǐng. Liǎng ge xīngqī yǐhòu wǒ yào qù Zhōngguó lǚyóu yí ge xīngqī, suǒyǐ xià ge xīngqī wǒ jiù yào kāishǐ zhǔnbèi le.

These past two months I've had a huge amount of work on — I'm often not home until ten, so I haven't exercised in a month. Three weeks ago I started learning to cook Chinese food, and now I cook for myself every weekend. Next Saturday I'm going to a friend's place to eat, and once we've eaten we're going straight off to a film. In two weeks I'm going to China for a week's travelling, so next week I'll start getting ready.

Self-check — one pass per line:

  • Is every time word in front of its verb? Nothing parked at the end, English-style?
  • Span or point? 最近两个月 for the stretch, 三个星期前 for the one moment — is 前 after the number, not before it?
  • In your "haven't done X for Y" sentence, does the length sit before 没, with 了 at the end and none on the verb?
  • In any "I did X for Y" sentence, does the length sit after the verb (旅游一个星期), and before the object if there is one?
  • Nothing about the next two weeks carries a 了 on its verb, and no 在 or 的 glued to a calendar word (在下个星期 ✗)?
