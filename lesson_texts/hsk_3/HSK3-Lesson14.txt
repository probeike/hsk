The Structure “先……，再/又，然后……和2
“先……，再/又……，然后……”表示动作的先后顺序。 “再”表示动作
还没有发生，“又”表示动作已经发生了。例如:
The structure“先………:，笛/又……，，然后.…… (first..., then..., and then...)” indicates
the sequence of a series of actions. “fi” indicates the action has not happened yet, while “2”

俗语 FBS Xian dao xian dé
Common First come, first served

Saying     “先到先得”，这里“先”是早的意思。这句话的意思是东西有限，到得早的
人就能得到，到晚了就没有了。现在一般用来比喻做事要积极主动，及早准备，
及早动手。

In the saying“先到先得”(first come, first served),
“先”means“early”. The saying means that due to the
limited amount of something, the ones who come early
will get it, while those who come late won’t. Now it is
usually used to encourage people to take the initiative in
doing something and to be ready and get started as soon
as possible.
