的 · 得 · 地

de · de · de

Reading & writing practice

About this reading

Everything here uses only words from HSK 1–2, HSK 3 lessons 1–8, and the twelve new words from the 的·得·地 lesson. Every passage is loaded with all three de's, and the target ones are highlighted: 的 gluing a description to a noun, 得 grading how an action turned out (including the verb-repeating 跳舞跳得很好 pattern), 地 setting the manner before a verb, plus the 是…的 spotlight frame. Read the characters first; tap Show pinyin only when you're stuck.

小丽：好久没见你了！你在纽约怎么样？

  • 舞蹈老师✗ That's what he hopes to become later (以后你想当舞蹈老师吗？).
  • 经理✗ No manager job is mentioned.

  • 去年✓ 她是去年来纽约的 — the 是…的 frame spotlights the year.
  • 上个星期✗ Not mentioned in the dialogue.
  • 两个星期以后✗ That's when the speaker plans to quit his job.

“我”唱歌唱得怎么样？ (How does the speaker sing?)

  • 唱得很大声，但是唱得不好✓ 唱得很大声……唱得不好。我跳舞跳得比唱歌好。

7. 改写 Rewrite a sentence from the passage

Everything below starts from a line you have just read. Go back and find it if you need to — but the sentence you type is never one the passage contains.

Transform. The 短文 says: 上个星期我的汉语老师给我打电话。
Your friend already knows the teacher rang — she wants to know when. Rewrite it with the 是…的 frame so that 上个星期 is the spotlit part. Start with 我的汉语老师. Type the whole sentence.

我的汉语老师是上个星期给我打电话的。 — the passage sentence simply reports the call. Move 是 in front of the time and close the sentence with 的, and the call is taken as known while the week becomes the news. 打电话 is a verb + object that likes to split, so …给我打的电话 is just as good. What you cannot do is keep a 了: 我的汉语老师是上个星期给我打电话了 ✗.

Transform. The 短文 grades his mornings with a bare verb: 早上起得很晚。
Say exactly the same thing, but with the full verb 起床 instead of bare 起. Keep 我 and 很晚. Type the whole sentence — something else has to change too.

我起床起得很晚。 — 起床 is verb + object (get-up-bed), and the object 床 now stands between the verb and 得. 得 must touch its verb, so 起 comes back for a second turn. 我起床得很晚 ✗ is the error this rule exists to block. This is also why the passage wrote it the short way: with the bare verb 起 there is nothing in the way, so no copy is needed.

8. 回答问题 Answer from the passage — in a full sentence

Answer the question. 小丽问：“你的舞蹈老师是从哪儿来的？”
Answer for the speaker, using what the 课文 tells you. Start with 她 and keep the questioner's frame — 她是韩国人 answers a different question. 用: 从 · 韩国

她是从韩国来的。 — a 是…的 question is answered by keeping the frame and dropping the new information into the spotlight; the closing 的 is never optional. 她是韩国人 would be a statement about who she is, not about the coming — and the 课文 gives you both facts (她是韩国人 … 她是去年来纽约的), so read the question carefully before you pick one.

Answer the question. 小丽问：“你的汉语老师说话说得快不快？”
In the 短文 that teacher rings up and speaks 慢慢地. Answer with a verdict on how she speaks. Start with 她. 用: 说话 · 慢 — the object 说话 may be dropped, but the verdict word must be 慢

她说话说得很慢。 — the question asks for a judgement, and judgements hang off the back of the verb with 得. 说话 carries the object 话, so 说 is repeated (or drop the object altogether: 她说得很慢). Notice the swap: the 短文 says 慢慢地说 — 地 in front, colouring one act of speaking — while the answer needs 得 behind the verb, grading how she speaks. Same two words, opposite structures. Keep the 很: 说得慢 alone sounds like the opening of a comparison.

9. 翻译 English → Chinese — new situations

Nothing here is in the passages. Same three characters, different people.

Translate. "My little sister's dance teacher has a very loud voice."
用: 妹妹 · 舞蹈老师 · 声音 · 大 — four decisions about 的 in one short sentence.

我妹妹的舞蹈老师的声音很大。 — walk the chain left to right and decide each join separately. 我妹妹: close family, 的 drops. 妹妹的舞蹈老师: one person belonging to another, so the glue stays. 舞蹈老师: 舞蹈 names the kind of teacher — a job label, no glue. 舞蹈老师的声音: glue again. Two 的 can sit in one chain like this; what can never happen is a 的 after the last noun (…声音的很大), because the thing being described always ends the chain. (Chinese also likes the topic version 我妹妹的舞蹈老师声音很大 — "as for her teacher, the voice is loud".)

Translate. "The children ran happily into the dance classroom."
用: 孩子们 · 高兴 · 地 · 跑进 · 舞蹈教室

孩子们高兴地跑进了舞蹈教室。 — English can park the adverb after the verb ("ran happily in"); Chinese cannot. 高兴 says the mood they go into the running with, so it sits in front of the verb with 地. Put it behind instead and you would need 得 and a different meaning: 跑得很高兴 = a verdict on how the running turned out. The 了 rides on the verb as normal — 地 does not block it. And note 舞蹈教室 takes no 的: it is a job-label-style compound, like 舞蹈老师.

10. 改错 Fix the bad summary

Three errors. Someone has summarised the 短文 in three sentences and got de wrong exactly three times — once per sentence, and each one a different mistake. Find them, write the corrected summary, then check.
搬家的那两天他睡的很不好，星期一又迟到了。这个星期他又开始练习汉语，每天早上认真的写十个字。星期六舞蹈老师高兴地说，他现在跳舞跳地比以前好了。

搬家的那两天他睡得很不好，星期一又迟到了。这个星期他又开始练习汉语，每天早上认真地写十个字。星期六舞蹈老师高兴地说，他现在跳舞跳得比以前好了。

  1. 睡的很不好 → 睡得很不好. A verb (睡) sits in front and a verdict (很不好) follows: that is the report-card slot, so 得. Ask the quick test — is there a noun after the marker? No → it cannot be 的. This is the single commonest slip of the three, simply because 的 is the character you write most often.
  2. 认真的写 → 认真地写. Look at what stands to the right: 写 is a verb, and only 地 may sit in front of a verb. 认真 is telling you the manner he goes into the writing with. (With 的 you would be building a noun phrase — "conscientious writing" as a thing.)
  3. 跳地比以前好 → 跳得比以前好. Here the marker comes after the verb 跳, with a judgement behind it, so it must be 得 — 地 can never follow the verb it belongs to. The first half of the sentence, 高兴地说, was already correct: same sound, different slot, different character. Note too that 跳舞跳 is right as it stands: the object 舞 blocks 得, so 跳 is said twice.

Use your own wording, not the passage's. You must get at least one 的, one 地 and one 得 in, and the 得 one should be a judgement on how something turned out.

两个月前，他离开了那个又小又不安静的房子，搬到了公司附近。刚搬完家的那几天他睡得特别少，所以星期一上班又迟到了。后来他的汉语老师打电话让他别着急，他就开始每天早上安静地练习写字。星期六上舞蹈课的时候，老师说他跳得比以前好多了，所以他决定两个星期以后辞职，去当舞蹈老师。

Liǎng gè yuè qián, tā líkāi le nàge yòu xiǎo yòu bù ānjìng de fángzi, bān dào le gōngsī fùjìn. Gāng bān wán jiā de nà jǐ tiān tā shuì de tèbié shǎo, suǒyǐ xīngqīyī shàngbān yòu chídào le. Hòulái tā de Hànyǔ lǎoshī dǎ diànhuà ràng tā bié zháojí, tā jiù kāishǐ měitiān zǎoshang ānjìng de liànxí xiě zì. Xīngqīliù shàng wǔdǎo kè de shíhou, lǎoshī shuō tā tiào de bǐ yǐqián hǎo duō le, suǒyǐ tā juédìng liǎng gè xīngqī yǐhòu cízhí, qù dāng wǔdǎo lǎoshī.

Two months ago he left that small, not-very-quiet flat and moved to somewhere near his company. In the first few days after the move he slept very little, so on Monday he was late for work again. Then his Chinese teacher rang and told him not to worry, and he started quietly practising his characters every morning. On Saturday at the dance class the teacher said he was dancing much better than before — so he has decided to quit in two weeks and go and be a dance teacher.

Check your own retelling:

  • Every 的 has a noun immediately to its right — including the long ones (又小又不安静的房子). Read rightwards from each one and check.
  • Your 地 has a verb to its right, and the word in front of it says how he went about it.
  • Your 得 has a verb immediately to its left with nothing squeezed in, and a judgement (很/特别/比…) behind it.
  • If that 得 verb carries an object (跳舞, 写字, 睡觉), did you repeat the verb — or use the bare verb on its own, as the passage does with 睡得?
  • Is it genuinely your wording? If a whole clause matches the passage character for character, you recalled the text rather than the story — rewrite that one.

12. 写一写 Now your version

Personalise it. The narrator had two months where work swallowed everything and he stopped doing the thing he cares about — then he started again. Write 4–5 true sentences about a time you stopped something you cared about (a class, a sport, a language, an instrument) and how it went when you picked it up again. Use at least one Adj + 地 + V, one V + 得 + (很) Adj, one 的 joining a description to a noun, and one 是…的 sentence saying when or where you started again.

去年冬天我的工作特别忙，压力也很大，所以我三个月都没去上舞蹈课。那个时候我每天回家都很累，吃完饭就想睡觉，什么也不想做。后来一个同事高兴地告诉我，公司旁边开了一个很大的舞蹈教室。我是今年一月开始在那儿上课的，现在我跳舞跳得比去年好多了。

Qùnián dōngtiān wǒ de gōngzuò tèbié máng, yālì yě hěn dà, suǒyǐ wǒ sān gè yuè dōu méi qù shàng wǔdǎo kè. Nàge shíhou wǒ měitiān huí jiā dōu hěn lèi, chī wán fàn jiù xiǎng shuìjiào, shénme yě bù xiǎng zuò. Hòulái yí gè tóngshì gāoxìng de gàosu wǒ, gōngsī pángbiān kāi le yí gè hěn dà de wǔdǎo jiàoshì. Wǒ shì jīnnián yīyuè kāishǐ zài nàr shàngkè de, xiànzài wǒ tiàowǔ tiào de bǐ qùnián hǎo duō le.

Last winter my job was especially busy and the pressure was high, so for three months I didn't go to dance class at all. Back then I came home exhausted every day — after dinner I just wanted to sleep and didn't feel like doing anything. Then a colleague happily told me that a big dance studio had opened next to our office. I started going there in January this year, and now I dance much better than I did last year.

Self-check — one pass per line:

  • The 地 sentence: description before the verb, and a verb — not a noun — standing to the right of 地.
  • The 得 sentence: 得 immediately after the verb, a 很/非常/比 in front of the adjective, and the verb repeated if it carries an object (跳舞跳得…).
  • The 是…的 sentence: both halves present, 是 touching the time or place it spotlights, and no 了 anywhere in it.
  • Each 的 followed by a noun — and dropped where Chinese drops it (我同事, 舞蹈老师, 我妈妈).
  • Four to five sentences that actually tell a story — stopped, why, restarted, how it is going now — and all of it true of you.
