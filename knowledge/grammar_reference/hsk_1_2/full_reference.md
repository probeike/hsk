# HSK 1 + 2 Grammar — Reference

All patterns from the Standard Course 1 & 2, grouped by topic. Each entry links to the practice sheet.

 ── 01 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 01 · Pronouns · 是 · 吗 · negation → practise

### Pronouns

Singular: 我 / 你 / 您 / 他 / 她 / 它 — wǒ / nǐ / nín / tā / tā / tā.

 Plural: add 们 — 我们 / 你们 / 他们.

### 是 sentences (A is B)

Subject + 是 + Noun

她是我的老师。
Tā shì wǒ de lǎoshī.
She is my teacher.

### 吗 yes/no questions

Statement + 吗?

你是学生吗?
Nǐ shì xuéshēng ma?
Are you a student?

### Negation with 不 and 没

不 negates present/future/habitual actions and all adjectives.
 没 (or 没有) negates 有 and completed past actions.

我不是美国人。 Wǒ bú shì Měiguó rén. I'm not American.

我没有哥哥。 Wǒ méi yǒu gēge. I don't have an older brother.

Tone note: 不 is bù, but changes to bú before another 4th-tone syllable (不是 bú shì).

 ── 02 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 02 · 的 — possessive & nominaliser → practise

### Possessive 的

Noun/Pronoun + 的 + Noun

Indicates possession. With close relationships (family, close friends) and institutions you belong to, 的 is usually dropped.

这是我的书。 Zhè shì wǒ de shū. This is my book.

我妈妈 / 我们学校 wǒ māma / wǒmen xuéxiào my mum / our school (no 的)

### 的 as a nominaliser ("the one that…")

Adj / Verb phrase + 的 = "the … one"

红色的是我的。 Hóngsè de shì wǒ de. The red one is mine.

那个卖水果的是我朋友。 Nàge mài shuǐguǒ de shì wǒ péngyou. That fruit-seller is my friend.

 ── 03 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 03 · Question words I: 什么 / 谁 / 哪 / 哪儿 → practise

Chinese keeps its question-words *in place*: replace the thing you're asking about with the question word. No word-order change, no 吗.

### 什么 (shénme) — what

你叫什么名字? Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? What's your name?

### 谁 (shéi) — who

她是谁? Tā shì shéi? Who is she?

### 哪 (nǎ) — which

哪 + measure word / noun + noun

你是哪国人? Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén? What nationality are you?

### 哪儿 (nǎr) — where

你儿子在哪儿工作? Nǐ érzi zài nǎr gōngzuò? Where does your son work?

 ── 04 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 04 · Question words II: 几 / 多少 / 怎么 / 怎么样 / 多 → practise

### 几 vs 多少 — how many

几 expects a *small* number (< 10), and **requires a measure word**: 几 + MW + N.

多少 is open-ended, can be any number, and the measure word is optional.

你家有几口人? Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén? How many people are in your family?
你们学校有多少学生? Nǐmen xuéxiào yǒu duōshao xuésheng? How many students at your school?

### 怎么 — how / why

怎么 + Verb asks *how* (manner) or — with a tone of surprise — *why*.

这个字怎么写? Zhège zì zěnme xiě? How do you write this character?
你怎么不吃了? Nǐ zěnme bù chī le? How come you're not eating anymore?

### 怎么样 — how is it / what's it like

明天天气怎么样? Míngtiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng? What's the weather like tomorrow?

### 多 + adjective — how [big/old/far]

你今年多大? Nǐ jīnnián duō dà? How old are you this year?
你家离公司多远? Nǐ jiā lí gōngsī duō yuǎn? How far is your home from the office?

 ── 05 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 05 · Numbers & measure words → practise

### Numeral + Measure Word + Noun

Num + MW + N · 这/那 + MW + N · 几 + MW + N

You cannot put a number directly before a noun: you need a measure word.

三本书 sān běn shū three books
这个人 zhège rén this person

### Common measure words at HSK 1–2

个 (general) · 口 (family members) · 本 (books) · 只 (animals) · 件 (clothes / matters) · 张 (flat: tickets, paper) · 杯 (cups) · 瓶 (bottles) · 块 (yuan; pieces) · 岁 (years of age).

### Reduplicated measure words — "every"

MW + MW = every ___

个个都很漂亮。 Gègè dōu hěn piàoliang. Every one is beautiful.

### 一下 — "a bit" / brief action softener

Verb + 一下

让我想一下。 Ràng wǒ xiǎng yíxià. Let me think for a moment.

### Tone change: 一 (yī)

Before 1st/2nd/3rd tone → 4th: 一天 yì tiān, 一年 yì nián.

 Before 4th tone → 2nd: 一个 yí ge, 一定 yídìng.

 Standalone / in numerals → stays 1st: 第一 dì yī.

 ── 06 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 06 · Time & date expressions → practise

### Date: year → month → day

YYYY年 + M月 + D日/号 + 星期X

2026年4月18号星期六 èr líng èr liù nián sì yuè shíbā hào xīngqīliù Saturday, 18 April 2026

号 (hào) is spoken, 日 (rì) is written.

### Clock time

N点 (N分) · 差 N 分 N 点 · N点半 · N点 一刻 / 三刻

现在七点二十。 Xiànzài qī diǎn èrshí. It's 7:20 now.
差五分八点。 Chà wǔ fēn bā diǎn. Five to eight.

### Time expressions go *before* the verb

Subject + TIME + Verb … (*not* Verb + Time like English)

我每天六点起床。 Wǒ měi tiān liù diǎn qǐchuáng. I get up at six every day.

### …的时候 — "when…"

(Subject + Verb phrase) + 的时候 …

我吃饭的时候不说话。 Wǒ chī fàn de shíhou bù shuōhuà. I don't talk while eating.

 ── 07 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 07 · Location: 在 · 有 (existential) → practise

### 在 as a verb — "to be at/in"

Subject + 在 + Place

我爸爸在北京。 Wǒ bàba zài Běijīng. My dad is in Beijing.

### 在 as a preposition — "at"

Subject + 在 + Place + Verb …

她在医院工作。 Tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò. She works at a hospital.

Keep the order *Subject → 在 Place → Verb*. Not Verb-first like English.

### Existential 有 — "there is / are"

Place + 有 + Thing

桌子上有一本书。 Zhuōzi shàng yǒu yì běn shū. There's a book on the desk.

Negate with 没: 桌子上没有书。

### 在 vs. 有 vs. 是

**在**: known thing → tells you where it is.

**有**: known place → tells you what exists there.

**是**: specific thing in a specific place → identifies which one.

 ── 08 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 08 · Conjunctions → practise

### 和 — "and" (nouns only)

Never use 和 to join two verbs or two sentences; that's what commas and other connectors are for.

我和我哥哥都在北京。 Wǒ hé wǒ gēge dōu zài Běijīng. My brother and I are both in Beijing.

### 因为…所以… — "because…so…"

Both halves are commonly present in Chinese, even though English would drop one.

因为下雨，所以我没去。 Yīnwèi xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ méi qù. Because it rained, I didn't go.

### 虽然…但是… — "although…still…"

虽然很累，但是我很高兴。 Suīrán hěn lèi, dànshì wǒ hěn gāoxìng. Although tired, I'm very happy.

### 也 (also) / 还 (also, in addition)

Both go *before* the verb. 也 mirrors a previous subject; 还 adds another item of the same kind.

我也是学生。 Wǒ yě shì xuésheng. I'm also a student.
我买了一本书，还买了一支笔。 Wǒ mǎi le yì běn shū, hái mǎi le yì zhī bǐ. I bought a book, and a pen too.

 ── 09 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 09 · Modal verbs: 想 / 要 / 会 / 能 → practise

### 想 (xiǎng) — would like to, want to

A *wish*: softer than 要. Negative: 不想.

我想喝茶。 Wǒ xiǎng hē chá. I'd like to drink tea.

### 要 (yào) — want, will, have to

Stronger intent than 想. Negative: usually 不想, not *不要 (which means "don't!").

我要去北京旅游。 Wǒ yào qù Běijīng lǚyóu. I'm going to travel to Beijing.

### 会 (huì) — learned ability / possibility

我会说汉语。 Wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ. I can (have learned to) speak Chinese.
明天会下雨。 Míngtiān huì xià yǔ. It will rain tomorrow.

### 能 (néng) — can (capability + permission)

我能坐这儿吗? Wǒ néng zuò zhèr ma? May I sit here?
今天我不能去。 Jīntiān wǒ bù néng qù. I can't go today (circumstances).

Rule of thumb: 会 = "I learned how"; 能 = "I'm able / allowed in this situation".

 ── 10 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 10 · Degree adverbs → practise

### 太…了 — too…

太贵了! Tài guì le! Too expensive!

### 最 — most / -est (superlative)

他汉语最好。 Tā Hànyǔ zuì hǎo. His Chinese is the best.

### 真 — really (exclamation)

真漂亮! Zhēn piàoliang! Really beautiful!

### 有点儿 (mildly negative) vs. 一点儿 (a little)

有点儿 + Adj expresses an unwelcome degree.

Adj + 一点儿 is comparative: "a little more ___".

今天有点儿冷。 Jīntiān yǒudiǎnr lěng. Today is a bit cold.
便宜一点儿吧。 Piányi yìdiǎnr ba. Make it a bit cheaper.

### 还 (still) · 就 (already, emphasising early) · 再 (again)

他还在睡觉。 Tā hái zài shuìjiào. He's still sleeping.
他六点就来了。 Tā liù diǎn jiù lái le. He came as early as 6 o'clock.
再说一遍。 Zài shuō yí biàn. Say it once more.

 ── 11 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 11 · 了 part 1 — completed action → practise

### Verb-了 (aspect)

Subject + Verb + 了 + (Object)

Marks an action as *completed*. Usually needs either a quantified object or a second clause — bare "V + 了 + N" often sounds incomplete.

她买了不少衣服。 Tā mǎi le bùshǎo yīfu. She bought quite a few clothes.
我吃了饭就去。 Wǒ chī le fàn jiù qù. I'll go as soon as I eat.

### Negation: 没(有) — drops the 了

我没吃饭。 Wǒ méi chī fàn. I haven't eaten.

### Questions

你吃饭了吗? / 你吃饭了没有? Nǐ chī fàn le ma? / …le méi yǒu? Have you eaten?

 ── 12 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 12 · 了 part 2 — change of state & imminent action → practise

### Sentence-final 了 — change of state

… + 了 (at the end) = "now"

我会说汉语了。 Wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ le. I can speak Chinese now (I couldn't before).
下雨了。 Xià yǔ le. It's raining (it's started).

### 都…了 — emphasising a surprising degree

都十点了，他还没来。 Dōu shí diǎn le, tā hái méi lái. It's already ten o'clock and he still isn't here.

### Imminent action: 要 / 快(要) / 就要 … 了

(快)要 + Verb + 了 · 就要 + Verb + 了

就要…了 can take a specific time phrase; 快要…了 cannot.

新年就要到了。 Xīnnián jiù yào dào le. The new year is about to arrive.
火车快要开了。 Huǒchē kuài yào kāi le. The train is about to leave.

 ── 13 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 13 · 在…呢 — action in progress → practise

Subject + (正)在 + Verb + (Object) + 呢

Any piece can drop except the verb: 在V, V呢, 在V呢, 正在V呢 all work. 正 emphasises "right at this moment".

他在学做中国菜呢。 Tā zài xué zuò Zhōngguó cài ne. He's learning to cook Chinese food.
我正在看书呢。 Wǒ zhèngzài kàn shū ne. I'm (right now) reading.

 ── 14 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 14 · 着 — continuing state → practise

Verb + 着 — the *resulting state* of an action persists

Focus is not on the action but on the state it leaves behind: a door that stays open, a hat that stays on.

门开着呢。 Mén kāi zhe ne. The door is open.
他穿着一件红色的衣服。 Tā chuān zhe yí jiàn hóngsè de yīfu. He's wearing red clothes.

### V₁着 V₂ — doing V₂ while V₁-ing

他笑着说。 Tā xiào zhe shuō. He said, smiling.

 ── 15 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 15 · 过 & contrast 了 / 过 / 着 → practise

### Verb + 过 — ever done it at least once

Subject + Verb + 过 + (Object) · negate: 没 + V + 过

你看过那个电影吗? Nǐ kàn guo nàge diànyǐng ma? Have you ever watched that film?
我没去过北京。 Wǒ méi qù guo Běijīng. I've never been to Beijing.

### 了 vs 过 vs 着 — one-liner contrast

**了** — the action was completed (one specific time).

**过** — the action happened at least once in life (experience).

**着** — the state from the action is still going on.

我吃了北京烤鸭。 I ate Peking duck (just now / that day).

我吃过北京烤鸭。 I've had Peking duck before.

我吃着饭呢。 I'm in the middle of eating.

 ── 16 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 16 · 是…的 focus structure → practise

Used for *completed* actions whose **time, place, manner, or agent** the speaker wants to highlight. It doesn't introduce new information; it asks *which* of them.

Subject + 是 + [focus phrase] + Verb + 的

我是坐飞机来的。 Wǒ shì zuò fēijī lái de. I came by plane.
她是昨天到北京的。 Tā shì zuótiān dào Běijīng de. She arrived in Beijing yesterday.
这个工作是他帮我介绍的。 Zhège gōngzuò shì tā bāng wǒ jièshào de. He's the one who introduced this job to me.

Negate with 不是…的. Never use 了 inside this frame.

 ── 17 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 17 · Complements: 结果 & 得-state → practise

### Result complement: Verb + [result]

A second verb or adjective sticks right onto the main verb to say *what the result was*. Negate the whole cluster with 没.

题太多，我没做完。 Tí tài duō, wǒ méi zuò wán. Too many questions — I didn't finish.

Common results: 完 finish, 到 reach/obtain, 见 perceive, 好 done well, 错 wrongly, 懂 understand, 会 mastered.

### State complement with 得

Verb + 得 + [how] · if there's an object: V + O + V + 得 + [how]

你穿得太少了。 Nǐ chuān de tài shǎo le. You're wearing too little.
她(说)汉语说得很流利。 Tā (shuō) Hànyǔ shuō de hěn liúlì. She speaks Chinese very fluently.

Negate the complement, not the verb: 说得不好.

 ── 18 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 

## 18 · 比 comparison · round-up → practise

### A 比 B + Adj (+ degree)

他比我大三岁。 Tā bǐ wǒ dà sān suì. He's three years older than me.
今天比昨天冷一点儿。 Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng yìdiǎnr. Today is a little colder than yesterday.

Never put 很/非常 in a 比 sentence — the degree word goes *after* the adjective (一点儿, 得多, 多了, 三岁).

### Pivotal sentence (兼语句): 让 / 叫

让我想想再告诉你。 Ràng wǒ xiǎngxiang zài gàosu nǐ. Let me think first, then I'll tell you.

### Imperative "don't…": 别 / 不要 … (了)

别找了，手机在桌子上。 Bié zhǎo le, shǒujī zài zhuōzi shàng. Stop looking — the phone is on the table.

### 对 — "to / toward" (relational)

Subject + 对 + N + Verb phrase

他对我很好。 Tā duì wǒ hěn hǎo. He's very kind to me.

### Confirmation questions: 是不是 · …好吗

你是不是中国人? Nǐ shì bu shì Zhōngguó rén? Are you Chinese (or not)?
我们一起去，好吗? Wǒmen yìqǐ qù, hǎo ma? Let's go together, okay?
