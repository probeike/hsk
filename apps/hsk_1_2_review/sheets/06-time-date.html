Sheet 06 · Tier 1

Time & date expressions

~25 min
Reference
Quick reminder

Dates go big → small: year → month → day → weekday. Year digits are read out one at a time: 2026年 → èr líng èr liù nián.

YYYY年 + M月 + D号/日 + 星期X

号 is spoken, 日 is written / formal. Same day.

Clock: N点 (N分). Half past = N点半. Quarters = 一刻 / 三刻. "To" = 差 N分 N点 (差五分八点 = 7:55).

Time phrases go BEFORE the verb — never after like English. With a topic (usually the subject), it can also sit at the very start.

(Topic/Subject) + TIME + Verb …

…的时候 — the whole Verb-phrase + 的时候 acts like a "when-clause": 我吃饭的时候不说话.

Warm-up recall

  1. Say the date in Chinese: Saturday, 18 April 2026.
  2. Say the time: 7:55 · 3:15 · 8:30 · 12:00 midnight.
  3. Translate: I get up at six every day.
  4. Translate: I don't talk while eating. (use 的时候)

Structured practice

  1. Fix the time-position error: 我去学校每天七点。
  2. Fix: 我起床在六点。
  3. Translate: What time is it now? — and — What day is today?
  4. Translate: My son's birthday is 3 June.
  5. Translate: When I was a child, I liked cats. (的时候)
  6. Translate: The class starts at nine; the class ends at ten thirty.
  7. Translate: We'll meet at half past seven at the school gate.
  8. Complete: 今天是 ___ 月 ___ 号，星期 ___。 (today)
  9. Transform: 我每天晚上十一点睡觉。 — rewrite starting with the time phrase.
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — What time is your class tomorrow?
    — 9:15. What about yours?
    — I don't have class tomorrow; I'll go to the library in the afternoon.

Warm-up

  1. 2026年4月18号星期六。 èr líng èr liù nián sì yuè shíbā hào xīngqīliù.
  2. 差五分八点 · 三点一刻 · 八点半 · 半夜十二点 / 零点
  3. 我每天六点起床。 Wǒ měi tiān liù diǎn qǐchuáng.
  4. 我吃饭的时候不说话。 Wǒ chī fàn de shíhou bù shuōhuà.

Structured

  1. 我每天七点去学校。 — time goes before the verb.
  2. 我六点起床。 — no 在 with clock times.
  3. 现在几点? · 今天星期几?
  4. 我儿子的生日是6月3号。
  5. (我)小时候喜欢猫。 / 我小的时候喜欢猫。
  6. 九点上课，十点半下课。
  7. 我们七点半在学校门口见。 (place phrase, like time, goes before the verb.)
  8. e.g. 今天是4月18号，星期六。
  9. 每天晚上十一点我睡觉。 (TIME-first topicalised version; natural in Chinese.)
  10. 你明天几点上课? — 九点一刻。你呢? — 我明天没有课，下午去图书馆。

  3. Negate: 他是医生。 01
