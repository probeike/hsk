3 Personalized Grammar Lessons

Built from your own tutoring sentences — 的/得/地, time ordering, and action status. Same pedagogy as the HSK 3 course: function first, English traps named, image schemas, minimal pairs.

How these differ from the HSK 3 lessons

Each lesson pair grew out of sentences you actually produced in class (两个星期之后我要辞职, 跳舞跳得很好, 前两个月我搬家了…). The vocabulary is restricted to HSK 1–2 plus HSK 3 lessons 1–8, with your personal words (辞职, 老板, 舞蹈, 工程师, 压力…) declared in each lesson's own "New vocabulary" table. About half the examples use your real contexts — New York, dance, quitting to become a boss — and the rest use the familiar cast (小丽, 小刚…).

All pages work offline. Companion course: HSK 3 Grammar — 20 interactive lessons.
