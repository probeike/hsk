过、起”，我们学过的动词还有“买、带、搬”。例如:

In Chinese, “V + 来/去”indicates the direction of an action, with “3€” indicating
the direction towards the speaker and “2:” the direction away from the speaker. The most

frequently used verbs include “.”, “F”, “HE”, “HH”, “TBI”, “it” and “起”as well as some
