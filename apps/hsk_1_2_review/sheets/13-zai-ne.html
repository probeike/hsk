Sheet 13 · Tier 2

在…呢 — ongoing action

~20 min
Reference
Quick reminder

Progressive (English -ing) is marked by some combination of (正)在 … (呢):

S + (正)在 + V + (O) + (呢)

Any of the three pieces can drop as long as at least one remains:

  • 他在学汉语。 — "he's studying Chinese" (neutral).
  • 他学汉语呢。 — casual, conversational.
  • 他在学汉语呢。 — slightly emphatic.
  • 他正在学汉语(呢)。 — "right this very moment".

Negation: "not doing it (at the moment)" is typically 没(有)在 + V — 在 stays, 呢 drops.

Don't confuse progressive 在 (pre-verb) with locative 在 (pre-noun). Locative is about where; progressive is about what's in progress.

Warm-up recall

  1. Translate: He's learning to cook Chinese food.
  2. Translate: I'm (right now) reading. (use 正在)
  3. Translate: She's on the phone. (打电话)
  4. Translate: I'm not sleeping.

Structured practice

  1. Rewrite with a different legal combo of 正/在/呢 (at least 3 variations):
    他在看电视呢。
  2. Fix: 他在在学校工作呢。
  3. Ask a question: What is she doing right now?
  4. Answer the question you just wrote with three different activities.
  5. Translate: When my dad got home, I was watching TV. (combine with 的时候)
  6. Translate: The kids are sleeping — please be quiet.
  7. Translate: I'm not listening; I'm working.
  8. Error-spot: 他正在呢。 — what's missing?
  9. Translate: They're waiting for you downstairs.
  10. Mini-dialogue:
    — Are you busy?
    — Yeah. I'm writing an email.
    — Can you come a bit later? Mum is cooking and Dad is sleeping.

Structured

  1. 他看电视呢。 · 他在看电视。 · 他正在看电视。 · 他正在看电视呢。
  2. 他在学校工作呢。 — the locative 在 and the progressive 在 collapse into a single 在.
  3. 她正在做什么呢? / 她在做什么?
  4. e.g. 她在看书。 · 她在睡觉。 · 她在打电话。
  5. 我爸爸回家的时候，我正在看电视呢。
  6. 孩子们在睡觉呢，请小声(点儿)。
  7. 我没听，我在工作呢。
  8. Missing verb: 他正在(V)呢 needs a verb.
  9. 他们在楼下等你呢。
  10. 你忙吗? — 嗯，我正在写邮件呢。— 你能晚一点儿来吗? 妈妈在做饭，爸爸在睡觉。
