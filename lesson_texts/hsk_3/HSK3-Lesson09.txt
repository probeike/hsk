PA 比较句1: A跟B一样 ( +Adj ) Comparative Sentences 1: A跟B一样 (+ Adj)

“A跟B一样”表示A和B两者相比较，结果相同。后边可以用形容词表示
比较的某一方面，如“一样大”表示大小相同。否定式是在“一样”的前边
加“不”。例如:

“Atk B—#” means A and B are the same after comparison. The structure can take an

adjective to indicate the aspect being compared, for example, “—##” means “the same in

size”. In the negative form, “4S” is added before “—##:”. For example:

(1) 这本
(2) 儿子
(3) 这辆
(4) 她的

三人行，必有我师 San ren xing, bi you wa shi
When I walk with two others, there must be one whom I can learn from

“三人行，必有我师”的意思是三个人一起走路，里边一定有一个人可以做我
的老师。这人句话用来比喻别人身上可能有我们没
有的优点，我们要学习别人身上的优点。

“= AFL RIM’ means “When I walk with two
others, there must be one whom I can learn from’. In

the metaphorical sense, it means everybody may have
some merits that we lack, so we should learn from

others.
