## Grammar introduced

### 1. 被字句 — The passive construction — "B was V-ed (by A)"

**When a speaker reaches for this:** when the speaker wants to foreground *what happened to B* — especially something *done to* B that B didn't choose. Historically 被 carried an adversative flavour (something bad); modern Chinese has softened this, but the "something happened to a patient" frame remains its core.
 

### The English trap — the First Noun Principle

Your English brain reads *first noun = doer*. That instinct is **wrong** in 被-sentences. In English passive, "was" / "by" signal the flip: "The mouse *was eaten by* the cat." In Chinese, the flip lands on one small word — 被. If you skip past 被, you'll read the sentence completely backwards.

**Train yourself to stop at 被 and re-assign roles:** the noun *before* 被 is the *patient* (done-to), the noun *after* 被 is the *agent* (doer) — or, if no agent is given, it's omitted.

鱼被猫吃完了 — "The mouse was eaten up by the cat." First noun = patient; 被 flips roles.

我的手机被小弟拿走了。

Wǒ de shǒujī bèi xiǎodì ná zǒu le.

my / phone / [passive] / little-brother / take / away / PERF

My phone was taken (away) by my little brother.

蛋糕被吃完了。

Dàngāo bèi chī wán le.

cake / [passive] / eat / finish / PERF

The cake was all eaten. *(Agent omitted — unknown or unimportant.)*

他被老师叫到办公室去了。

Tā bèi lǎoshī jiào dào bàngōngshì qu le.

He was called to the office by the teacher.

### 被 vs 把 — mirror images

They describe the *same event* from opposite angles:

- 猫把鱼吃完了 — active: Cat (agent first) → mouse → done. Focus on the doer.

- 鱼被猫吃完了 — passive: Mouse (patient first) → cat → done. Focus on what happened to the mouse.

Both require a **result** (了, complement, place). Neither works with bare stative verbs.

### 被 needs a result, too

**他被看* (✗) alone is incomplete. Always pair 被 with some result or complement: 他被看见了 (✓ seen), 他被人打了 (✓ got hit), 衣服被洗好了 (✓ got washed). Same rule as 把.

### Negation and questions

Negation and modals go **before 被**, not between 被 and the verb:

我的电脑没被拿走。

Wǒ de diànnǎo méi bèi ná zǒu.

My computer wasn't taken. *(没 before 被, not *被没.)*

**Who did what to whom** in: 小狗被他带回家了?

- The puppy brought him home.✗ First-noun trap! 被 flips the roles — the puppy is the patient, not the agent.

- He brought the puppy home.✓ B + 被 + A + V: puppy (B, patient), he (A, agent), bring-back-home.

- The puppy and he went home together.✗ 被 is not a connector; it marks the passive flip.

In 杯子被拿走了 (no agent given), what happened?

- The cup took something away.✗ First-noun trap again. Stop at 被.

- The cup was taken away (by someone unspecified).✓ No agent after 被 is fine — the action happened to the cup.

Pick the correct passive for "He was called by the teacher":

- 他被老师叫了。✓ B + 被 + A + V + 了.

- 他叫被老师了。✗ 被 must come before the agent, not between the patient and the verb wrongly.

- 老师被他叫了。✗ This says "the teacher was called by him" — roles reversed.

### 2. 只有 … 才 … — "Only if … then (and only then) …"

**When a speaker reaches for this:** to mark a *necessary* condition — **without** X, Y will not happen. Contrast with 只要…就… (L17), which marks a *sufficient* condition.
 

### The English trap

English blurs the two: "only if", "as long as", "only when" all sound similar. Chinese keeps them sharply separate through the pivot word:

- 只要 … **就** … → X is enough.

- 只有 … **才** … → X is required. Without X, no Y.

The 才 is doing the work you met in Lesson 11 ("only then / not until"). It restricts.

只有
+
condition
,
Subj
+
才
+
result

只有努力，才能学好。

Zhǐyǒu nǔlì, cái néng xué hǎo.

only-have / work-hard, / only-then / able / learn-well

Only by working hard can you learn well.

只有你愿意帮忙，这件事才能做完。

Zhǐyǒu nǐ yuànyì bāngmáng, zhè jiàn shì cái néng zuò wán.

Only if you're willing to help can this job get done.

只要 … 就 … (sufficient)

只要你来，我就高兴

Your coming is enough; I'll be happy.

只有 … 才 … (necessary)

只有你来，我才高兴

I'm only happy if you come — no one else will do.

In 只有考得好，才能上好大学, what's being claimed?

- A good exam score is enough to get in.✗ That's 只要…就…

- A good exam score is *required* to get in.✓ 只有…才… = without it, no good university.
